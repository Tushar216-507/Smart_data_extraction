Seize the opportunity to pursue a rigorous degree program led by dedicated, research-oriented faculty and distinguished practitioners. Our instructors employ cutting-edge teaching methods and challenge you to engage with complex, real-world economic issues.

Studying alongside a diverse international student body, you’ll gain a truly global perspective—making this master’s program an ideal launchpad for an international career.

The Master of Science in International Economics and Economic Policy offers a structured, research-oriented curriculum that combines rigorous core training with a wide range of courses exploring key dynamics in the global economy. Students begin with foundational modules in international economics, public policy, econometrics, macro- and microeconomics, and continue with advanced electives, practitioner-led supplementary courses, seminars, and a master’s thesis.

This carefully designed program provides both theoretical depth and practical perspective, preparing graduates for careers in academia, international organizations, government institutions, or national and multinational corporations.
