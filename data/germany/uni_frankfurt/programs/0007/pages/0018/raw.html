Department EQ: Economic Policy and Quantitative Methods

We are dedicated to producing innovative research, high-quality teaching & supervision of students at all levels. We combine the development and application of modern quantitative methods with economic theory and its history, and actively contribute to the fields of econometrics, growth, development, international, public and labor economics. Our research findings are published in leading international journals and inform economic policy makers as well as the public debate.
