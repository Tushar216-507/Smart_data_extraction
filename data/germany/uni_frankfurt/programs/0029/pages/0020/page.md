## Internationalization – A Strategic Approach

Goethe University's Internationalization is based on a collaboratively developed strategy that guides our actions and university-wide development across all faculties.

Goethe University maintains partnerships with over 450 universities across Europe and around the world. It promotes international exchange among students as well as international research, thereby strengthening global networking.
