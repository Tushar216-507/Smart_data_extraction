The Data and Software Centre lets you download state-wide spatial datasets, geology series map data, resources such as Department of Mines, Petroleum and Exploration (DMPE) software (including user guides and videos where available), and documentation on map services provided by DMPE.

Type a keyword in the search tool on the right to search for specific datasets in a specific coordinate system.

Nightly process to update dynamic datasets is scheduled to be completed by approximately 3am following each business day.

After 31st March 2026, we will only create GDA2020 outputs.

Most GDA94 downloads are now unavailable since 31st March 2026.

ESRI will retire ArcMap 10.8.2 on the 1st March 2026.

We will keep creating ESRI layerfiles (lyr) up to 31st March 2026, after this date we will only create ArcGIS Pro layer files (lyrx) for new or updated layers.

What’s changing?
