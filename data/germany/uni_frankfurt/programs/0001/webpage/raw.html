Double Degree Master's Program Paris Dauphine-PSL

The Faculty of Economics and Business offers a Double Degree Master's Program with the partner university Paris Dauphine-PSL. This program is open to students enrolled in the "Master of Science in Money and Finance" (MMF) program. 

Participants will obtain two degrees: 

  •  the Master's degree of the Faculty of Economics and Business, and
  •  the Master's degree in “Economie et Finance” of the University Paris Dauphine-PSL.  

This double degree provides a special qualification and excellent opportunities on the international job market.

Benjamin Gonzales, Student Double master degree Summer 2021
“My exchange semester at the PSL-Paris Dauphine University was one of my biggest highlights during my master's studies. Not only did I grow professionally and personally in a competitive university context, but I made memories that I will always recall with fondness.”

Remi Montagu, Student Double master degree Summer 2023
"My year in Frankfurt during the Master's program was an unforgettable chapter in my academic journey. Immersed in a large number of diverse experiences, I embraced learning German alongside engaging in enriching finance workshops at prestigious institutions. This program uniquely combined cultural immersion, language acquisition, and professional growth, making it the most beautiful and pivotal year after completing the first part of my studies in France."

The Double Degree Master's Program is designed to be completed in four semesters. The first and second semester largely correspond to the curriculum of the Master's program at the home institution. Students spend their third semester (winter term) in Paris and are enrolled in the Master's program “Economie et Finance”. The fourth semester (summer term) can be completed either at University Paris Dauphine-PSL or in Frankfurt. 

Over the course of both semesters, Frankfurt students obtain 36 ECTS credit points from the French study program and 24 ECTS through the master's thesis within the Frankfurt study program.

The Double Degree Master's Program is open to students enrolled in the "Master of Science in Money and Finance". A total of five study places are available within the program.

Participants in the Double Degree Master's Program receive a grant from the Erasmus+ Program.
