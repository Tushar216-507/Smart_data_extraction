How To Find Us:

The Department of Sociology is located on the 1st floor of the Foundation Building, University of Limerick (AKA, UL Concert Hall)

Take the glass elevator to the 1st floor and go through the double doors. Turn left and walk towards the far wall. You'll see lots of posters in relation to our research and publications!

Once there, proceed left towards F1003 the main Sociology Department Office. For Sociology queries, please contact the Department Administrator, e-mail anne.mccarthy@ul.ie
