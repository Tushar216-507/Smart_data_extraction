Do you want to inspire future generations by creating immersive classroom experiences? UL’s Professional Master of Education programme empowers you to deliver learning that makes a real impact.

On the programme, you will understand the tools and classroom experience you need to be a confident educator. 

You will also consider what it means to be a great teacher today, with a focus on research skills, using new technology, and delivering the right learning for everyone. 

You have the option to pursue teaching qualifications in the following disciplines:

  • Business (Accounting, Business, Economics)
  • Languages (French, German, Irish, Japanese, Spanish, English as a Second Language (ESL)
  • Mathematics
  • Music
  • Physical Education
  • Science (Agricultural Science, Biology, Chemistry, Physics)
  • Technological Education (Engineering, Technology, Construction Studies, Design and Communication Graphics)

Students are encouraged to specialise in two subject areas where their Level 8 qualifications allow. Please contact any of the Pathway Leaders if you have a question about your suitability.

Those studying on the Languages pathway (French, German, Irish, Japanese, and Spanish) with credits in only one language must take English as a Second Language (ESL).  ESL is not available with a non-language discipline. 

Those students studying on the Technological Education pathways must have the required credits in Design and Communication Graphics (DCG) plus one other subject. 

This programme includes in-person teaching placements to ensure you are ready to start teaching as soon as you are qualified. 

At UL, you will become a student of the School of Education,  ranked in the Top 100 Worldwide in the QS Global Rankings.
