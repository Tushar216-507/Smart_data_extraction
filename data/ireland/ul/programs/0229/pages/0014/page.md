Campus car parking locations and the location of the Kemmy Business School can be found on the [Map of UL Campus](https://www.ul.ie/media/22919/download?inline). The KBS building is marked as number 28. Google maps coordinates for the KBS are 52°40'21.3"N 8°34'36.3"W. The Kemmy Business School is located close to the main entrance to the UL campus.
