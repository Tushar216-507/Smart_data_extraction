# Bachelor Dual Degree

The KBS currently has six dual degree partners that are available to our BAIB and BBS students. Each of these agreements works on the basis of a 2+2 structure, whereby students spend their first two years studying as normal at UL and their remaining two years (which includes their Co-op placement) at the dual degree partner. At the end of their four years, students graduate with their degree from UL, as well as a degree from the partner School. There are no extra academic fee implications for students as no academic fees are paid to the partner institution and students just pay their normal fee to UL over their four years.
