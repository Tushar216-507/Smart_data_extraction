### Rhona Tarrant

Journalist Rhona Tarrant is a graduate of New Media and English at UL, which is now offered as English and Digital Culture and Communications through the Bachelor of Arts.

Rhona has a distinguished career in journalism and is now Executive Editor for CBS Confirmed, a unit within CBS News that works on disinformation, AI, open-source investigations and visual verification.

Previously, Rhona served as Head of Editorial for Storyful in New York and spent 10 years working in broadcast journalism in Ireland, including at RTÉ, where she worked in current affairs. She also completed two fellowships at Harvard University, on disinformation and AI.

Rhona says: “UL constantly pushed me out of my comfort zone, between the course, work experience, Erasmus, and the different people I met and became friends with. By the time I graduated, I had a lot more confidence to push myself even further.”
