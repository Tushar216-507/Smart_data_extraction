Welcome to the Department of Economics

The Department of Economics at the University of Limerick’s triple accredited Kemmy Business School. We have fourteen faculty members, four full time teaching assistants, and several postdoctoral researchers and PhD students. Learn more about our people in the 'Faculty' link below.

Our Research Our faculty publish in leading international journals, ensure their research has an impact in the real world, and actively contribute to the development of public policy.

Our Programmes Economics is a way of seeing the world. We are outstanding teachers of economics and proud of our faculty members who have won regional, national and international teaching awards. Our programmes will help you achieve your career goals. Learn more about programmes we teach by following the link below.

Our Alumni Our graduates become entrepreneurs, executives, policy makers, academics, politicians, and change makers. We are proud of all of them. To keep up with what the department is doing, please follow our [LinkedIn page](https://www.linkedin.com/in/economics-department-150748183/).
