UL’s Master of Arts (MA) in Dance Performance is a one‑year, full‑time programme at NFQ Level 9. It supports dancers to deepen technical skill, evolve creative voice, and perform new work.

Choose your pathway in Irish Traditional Dance Performance or Contemporary Dance Performance. The programme fosters collaboration across both through shared workshops, seminars and electives.

You will undertake intensive, primarily studio‑based research in performance, choreography and creative practice, supported by Somatics, writing/documentation, and media technologies.

Your studies culminate in a Final Presentation in the summer semester.
