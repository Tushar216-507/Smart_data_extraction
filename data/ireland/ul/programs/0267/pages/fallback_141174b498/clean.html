Microsoft Project is a project management app developed by Microsoft and included in the Office suite. Designed to help you plan, execute and monitor every stage of your work, Microsoft Project allows you to organize all parts of your project without losing sight of deadlines, resources and associated costs, whether for individual or team projects.

An Excel-like interface

The Microsoft Project interface is very similar to that of other Office programs. You can create new projects, open those you have recently edited, use templates, and more. The program itself contains a wide variety of actions that can be carried out in each project, and you'll see any changes and edits in real-time. Microsoft Project's features and interface will often remind you of Excel.

Create visual and dynamic timelines

Microsoft Project uses the Gantt chart, a visual timeline that shows you each task, its dependencies and progress at a glance. Thanks to this, you can extend, reduce or edit timelines. Additionally, if a task is delayed, the tool automatically recalculates its impact on the rest of the schedule.

In-depth management of resources, costs and assignments

With Microsoft Project, you can also set out who does what and with what resources. You can create a list of team, material or financial resources, assign these to specific tasks and monitor their availability to avoid excessive workloads or waste of materials. In addition to this, you can set hourly or usage-based rates, allowing you to calculate the total cost of each phase of the project and compare it to your budget in real-time.

Monitor progress in real-time

Once the project is underway, you can record the progress of each task directly in the app. Microsoft Project shows you the percentage completed, and any delays and deviations from the original plan. You can also generate automatic reports summarizing progress, consumed resources and critical tasks.

Collaborate with your team in a centralized environment

Microsoft Project integrates with other Microsoft tools such as Teams and SharePoint, allowing you to share your projects with other users, assign collaborative tasks and receive real-time updates. Each team member can view their own part of the project, mark progress and leave comments.

Adapt to any type of project, regardless of its size

Whether you're planning a product launch, coordinating the construction of a building site or managing the timing of a marketing campaign, Microsoft Project adapts to the specifics of your industry. Its flexibility and customization options allow you to create customized templates, automate repetitive processes and adjust each parameter according to your needs.
