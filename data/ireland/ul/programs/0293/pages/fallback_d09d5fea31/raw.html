Biomedical Science Courses in India with Fees, Colleges, After 12th and 10th, Jobs and Salary 2026

Ishika Ray

Updated 3+ months ago

Biomedical Science is a branch of science that understands the biological principles that govern the functioning of the human body and helps in finding new and distinct ways to cure the disease by developing advanced diagnostic tools and therapeutic strategies.

This course is beneficial for the students who are desirous of teaching at the college level and have skills like identifying blood groups, interpreting results and liaising with medical staff, maintaining records, and writing medical reports.

Biomedical Science courses are offered at Graduate, Postgraduate, Diploma, and as well as at Doctorate level presently. Apart from that, there are plenty of Biomedical Science certificates offered by various online websites.

Diploma Biomedical Science Courses

Diploma courses differ in terms of the eligibility criteria they ask for. A diploma Biomedical Science course can be pursued after you complete class 12 (class 10 also in some cases.

  • Just like certificate programs, a diploma in Biomedical Science courses also gives you the flexibility to choose from a huge set of specializations.

Job Options

What are the Top Job Options after Biomedical Science Courses?

Biomedical Scientist

The responsibility of biomedical scientists includes handling different biological samples, screening, and testing of lifestyle diseases, development, the discovery of treatments like medicines or drugs, etc. They also conduct research to improve human health.

Starting Salary: INR 2-4 LPA

Skills Required

  • Communication skills
  • Organizational skills
  • Interpersonal skills
  • Critical Thinking

Pharmacist

The work of a Pharmacist includes dispensing and managing medicines and looking after the overall health care of patients in the setting of pharmacies or dispensaries. Their work also includes preparing medications by reviewing and interpreting physician orders and detecting therapeutic incompatibilities. 

Starting Salary: INR 4,93,286

Skills Required

  • Organizational skills
  • Communication skills
  • Analytical skills

Biomedical Engineer

The job of biomedical engineering is to design systems and products including artificial organs, devices that replace body parts for diagnosing medical issues. They are also responsible for installing, adjusting, maintaining, and repairing or providing technical assistance for biomedical equipment. 

Starting Salary: INR 2-4 LPA

Skills Required

  • Problem-Solving skills
  • Critical Thinking 
  • Communication skills
  • Organizational skills

Clinical Psychologist

The job of a clinical psychologist is to diagnose or evaluate mental and emotional disorders of individuals. They observe patients in various situations and interpret intelligence, personality, or other psychological tests to diagnose disorders.

Starting Salary: INR 6,20,000

Skills Required

  • Mathematical Proficiency
  • Organization skills
  • Communication skills
  • Interpersonal skills

Research Associate

The work of her research associate is to monitor the progress of research projects in coordinating information between departmental sections. They perform highly specialized and advanced experiments and collect, prepare, analyze and evaluate specimens or tissue cultures.

Starting Salary: INR 3,20,000

Skills Required

  • Organizational skills
  • Analytical skills
  • Communication skills

FAQs

Biomedical Science Courses: FAQs

Ques. Which is the Biomedical Science Free Online Course in India?

Ans. There are various courses online, but pursuing a Biomedical Science course from a recognized academy adds value to your resume. Some of the best online Biomedical Science courses are offered by:

  • Udemy
  • Coursera
  • edX

Ques. What is the duration of a Biomedical Science Course?

Ans. The duration of the Biomedical Science Course varies. It depends on what level you are studying. For UG, it is 3 years, at PG level it is around 2 years, a diploma is 3 years and for Certification, the course is for 3 months to 1 year.

Ques. What are the different subjects that are taught in B.Sc in Biomedical Science courses?

Ans. Bio-organic chemistry, cell and radiation biology, principles of genetics, Human physiology and anatomy, biochemistry, medical microbiology, molecular biology, medicinal chemistry, toxicology, and pharmacology, biophysics, human pathology are some of the subjects are taught in BSC biomedical science course.

Ques. What are the different topics that are taught in M.Sc Biomedical Science Course?

Ans. Medical microbiology, pharmacology, and toxicology, legal and ethical issues in the management, bio-psycho pathology,  physiopathology, clinical management, mild mental disorder, clinical biochemistry are some of the topics that are taught in the M.Sc biomedical science course.

Ques. What are the top colleges to pursue B.Sc Biomedical Science Course?

Ans. Acharya Narendra Dev College, Guru Nanak College, Bhaskaracharya College of Applied Sciences, Bundelkhand University are some of the top colleges that offer B.Sc in Biomedical Science.

Ques. What are the top recruiters after pursuing a biomedical science course?

Ans. Apollo Hospital Enterprises, Delhi University, Fortis Healthcare, Max Hospital, Government Offices had some of the top recruiting companies in India which includes students in this field.
