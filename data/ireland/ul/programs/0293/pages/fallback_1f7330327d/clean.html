What is Biomedical Sciences

Biomedical Sciences is a specialised field in medicine that focuses on how certain areas of biology and chemistry can be applied to healthcare. Biomedical Science is a specialised field with a very wide range; it combines three general areas of speciality, bioengineering, physiological sciences, and life sciences. Around 27 colleges in the country provide biomedical sciences courses.

A career in Biomedical Sciences entails mostly research and laboratory work, all aiming for the improvement and advancement of medical knowledge. As a Biomedical Scientist, candidates will undertake work similar to finding new medicines to fight cancer, 3D printing a heart, and growing embryos for IVF

Eligibility Criteria (UG & PG) of Biomedical Sciences

Eligibility requirements for the Biomedical Sciences course differ depending on the institute and the course level. For undergraduate degrees, students generally need to complete their 10+2 from a recognised institute in India. Similarly, for postgraduate degrees, they require a bachelor's degree from a recognised university in India. In this section, we will discuss the eligibility details of undergraduate and postgraduate degrees.

Eligibility Criteria for UG Courses

Candidates who seek admission in B.Sc. Biomedical Sciences courses at any university must fulfil certain eligibility criteria to qualify for the university’s merit list. While these criteria may vary in certain aspects from university to university, the basic common points include:

  • Students must have successfully passed their Higher Secondary Examination or any other equivalent examination from a recognised board.
  • Students must have studied Biology,Chemistry, andPhysicsas compulsory subjects in Class 12. Some colleges also provide admission to students who have studied the Biotechnology subject.
  • Students must have scored at least 50 per cent when averaged.

Entrance Exams for PG Courses

Students who wish to pursue further studies in Biomedical Sciences after completion of their Bachelor's degree can do so through a Master of Science (M.Sc) in the subject. Admissions into M.Sc. Biomedical Science courses are conducted either based on merit or with the help of entrance exams.

While there are no common entrance exams, most colleges offer admission based on their performance in graduation. The remaining few institutes prefer to conduct their entrance examinations as part of the screening process during admissions.

There are enormous career prospects in India and abroad for Biomedical Science graduates. Careers in Biomedical Sciences

The field of Biomedical Sciences offers a variety of job profiles for aspirants primarily related to research. Candidates can choose their field of interest and pursue a highly rewarding and lucrative career in the pursuit of this field. Some of the top job profiles that Biomedical Sciences aspirants are offered employment in include:

Job Profile Responsibilities

Biomedical Scientist

Biomedical Scientists are responsible for handling different biological samples, testing, and screening lifestyle ailments. They also discover and develop treatment aids such as medicines and drugs.

Lecturer

Lecturers are professionals responsible for teaching and delivering lectures at the university level and guiding students in the related discipline to identify their strengths and interests. They also help students understand how to harness and pursue these strengths and interests.

Biomedical Engineer

Biomedical Engineers are professionals responsible for designing systems and products for devices for replace body parts and helping diagnose medical issues. They also work on creating artificial organs.

Pharmacist

Pharmacists are responsible for dispensing and managing medicines. They also take care of the overall health of patients in the setting of dispensaries or pharmacies.

Lab Manager

Lab Managers are professionals responsible for processing specimens, preparing experimental setups and equipment, and managing the lab. They can work in hospitals and pharmaceutical companies.

Sales Engineer

Sales Managers are professionals responsible for collaborating with sales teams to understand and deliver customer requirements. They also promote the sales of the company's products and enhance overall company sales.

Emerging Biomedical Careers:

Apart from these career options, some new career paths that students can pursue after Biomedical Sciences courses include Molecular Biologist, Bioinformatics Analyst, Regulatory Affairs Associate, Medical Laboratory Scientist & Clinical Research Associate.

Upcoming trends

The field of Biomedical Sciences fuels medical advances in many different areas of research. Recently, technology has played a major role in the development of nearly every industry, and Biomedical Sciences has been no exception. Some of the latest developments, many of them owing to technology, that have been transforming Biomedical Sciences are:

  • Nanorobotics
  • Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare
  • Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCIs)
  • Regenerative Medicine
  • Nanomedicine
  • Organ-on-a-Chip Technology
  • 3D Bioprinting

Course Curriculum for Biomedical Sciences

Courses in biomedical sciences prepare students for careers in the field by teaching them about the structure and functions of living organisms, such as cell organisation and biological information transmission. Through hands-on lab training, data analysis, and learning how to use math and statistics to interpret biomedical data, they gain fundamental research skills.

Students also learn how to gather information from various sources to make well-informed decisions regarding biological matters. They learn how to design and carry out scientific experiments, and they also talk about ethical concerns such as responsible research techniques. All things considered, these courses offer invaluable information and abilities for upcoming professions in biological science.
