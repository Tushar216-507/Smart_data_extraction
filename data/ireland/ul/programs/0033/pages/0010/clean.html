Your postgraduate journey

At University of Limerick, we support you throughout your postgraduate journey, from application to graduation.

Entry requirements

Each programme will have requirements listed that you need to meet to be eligible to apply. You will upload the supporting documents to your application.

Examples of supporting documents:

  • Personal statement
  • Transcripts
  • Nomination and confirmation from a supervisor/mentor
  • Other documents that support your knowledge, skills and experience

Starting your application

Each programme has specific entry requirements outlined in the programme information. If you're eligible to apply for the programme you're interested in, you can choose to "apply now" and create an account to start your application.

There will be a 50 euro application fee and you'll receive a confirmation email to confirm your submission. No changes can be made while your application is in process.

Learn how to apply to a postgraduate programme. 

Preparing for your interview

You may be invited for an online or in-person interview depending on the programme you choose.

Tracking your application

Once you submit your application, you can track it's status through the online application system.

Accepting your offer

You'll receive an email from the university to acknowledge your application has been successful.

Administrative tasks

Once your application has been accepted, you'll receive an email with instructions to begin a few administrative tasks. These tasks may include:

  • Activating your student account
  • Completing financial tasks
  • Enrolling for your programme

Student fees and financing

There are two student fees classifications: EU and Non-EU.

Each programme will detail the annual fees and are subject to change each year. You can also find information on whether you're eligible for student funding and scholarships. 

Starting your programme

If your programme begins in Autumn or Spring, an online induction will be run by your faculty. Induction takes place during the 10 day-window before your programme begins. This date will be communicated with you.

Induction will provide you with all the information you need to start your programme. A timetable will be created and you'll learn more about what to expect each semester.

At this point you will also gain access to UL's Virtual Learning Environment (VLE) which is called Brightspace. You are encouraged to log into BrightSpace in particular to familiarise yourself with the platform and wealth of supports associated with using it. 

For many Autumn programmes, an on-campus orientation will take place on the Friday before week 1 of the semester.
 

myUL App

myUL is UL’s app for students, helping you stay informed, supported and connected on campus. The app includes your timetable, an interactive campus map, Clubs & Societies, access to key student supports - all in one place.
