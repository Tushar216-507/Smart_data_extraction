Welcome!

Welcome to the University of Mumbai's Online Admission Portal for Academic Year 2026–27, designed to automate the entire college admission process from application submission to final enrollment.

This platform streamlines all administrative tasks for prospective students seeking admission to University of Mumbai affiliated colleges, including application processing, document verification, merit list generation, admission confirmation, and enrollment through a transparent, secure, and user-friendly digital system.
