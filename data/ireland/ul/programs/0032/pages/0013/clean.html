There are two student fee classifications

  • EU
  • Non-EU

EU fee status applies to Domestic or EU students or students who have been resident as a taxpayer in EU/ EEA/Swiss State for 3 out of the last 5 years. Non-EU fee status applies to all other students.

Fee payment plans

  • Fees are billed in instalments.
  • Instalment 1 for the Autumn Semester is due in early September of that year. Instalment 2 for the Spring Semester is due in the first week of the semester in January.
  • Once registered, students may be eligible to apply for a monthly payment plan.
