FAQ

Questions about second hand machinery

Where can I buy second hand machinery in India?

OldMachine lists second hand machinery from verified sellers across India. Browse by machine type, category, or city, then enquire directly from the listing page on store.oldmachine.in.

Is second hand machinery reliable?

Many second hand machines are production-proven assets. Reliability depends on maintenance history, hours run, and condition — which is why inspection and trial runs before purchase are essential.

What is the difference between used and second hand machinery?

The terms are interchangeable for buyers. Both refer to pre-owned industrial equipment. On OldMachine, listings include condition notes, year, and photos to help you assess value.
