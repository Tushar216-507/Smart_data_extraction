Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MachineJunction

What is MachineJunction?+
MachineJunction is India's marketplace for buying and selling used and refurbished industrial machinery. We connect verified sellers with serious buyers across all major cities in India — directly, with no middlemen.
Is it free to list a machine on MachineJunction?+
Yes, completely free. There are no listing fees, no commissions, and no subscription charges for sellers. You can list as many machines as you want at no cost.
How do I contact a seller?+
Click on any machine listing and fill out the quick enquiry form with your name, mobile number, and message. The seller's contact details — phone number and email — will be revealed instantly.
Which cities does MachineJunction cover?+
MachineJunction covers all major industrial cities across India including Mumbai, Delhi, Pune, Ahmedabad, Surat, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, Ludhiana, Rajkot, Coimbatore, Kolkata, and many more.
What types of machines are listed on MachineJunction?+
We cover all categories of industrial machinery including CNC machines, lathe machines, milling machines, generators, compressors, forklifts, packaging machines, textile machines, pharma machines, construction equipment, industrial robots, and more.
How long does it take for a listing to get approved?+
Our team reviews and approves listings within 24 hours on business days. You'll be able to see your listing status in your seller dashboard.
Can I negotiate the price with the seller?+
Yes. Once you unlock the seller's contact details, you can call or email them directly to negotiate price, arrange inspection, and finalize the deal — completely on your own terms.
Is MachineJunction only for dealers or can individuals also sell?+
Both individuals and dealers can sell on MachineJunction. Whether you're a business upgrading your equipment or an individual with a single machine to sell, you can list for free.
