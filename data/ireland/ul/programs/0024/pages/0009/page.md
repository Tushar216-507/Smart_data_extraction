### Arts, Humanities & Social Sciences Scholarships

#### AHSS Faculty - Professor Tom Lodge Bursary AY26/27

10 x €5000 bursaries
Applicants must meet all of the following criteria to be considered:

- Programme of Study
  - Must be applying to a Taught Master's programme within the Faculty of Arts, Humanities and Social Sciences (AHSS) at the University of Limerick.
  - Programmes offered by the Irish World Academy of Music and Dance are excluded. Please see Scholarships and bursaries | University of Limerick for Irish World Academy of Music and Dance funding opportunities.
- Residency Status
  - Must be an Irish or EU applicant.
- Academic Qualifications
  - Applicants must hold, or be expected to hold, a minimum of a 2:1 (Second Class Honours, Grade 1) or First Class Honours in a Level 8 undergraduate degree.
  - If the final award has not yet been confirmed at the time of application, applicants should provide evidence of their expected classification (e.g. transcripts, academic references, or a letter from their institution).
  - Final confirmation of the award will be required before the waiver is granted.
- Access Route
  - Must provide evidence of having entered higher education through a recognised access route, such as the Higher Education Access Route (HEAR), within the last six years.
- Application and Enrolment
  - Applicants may apply for the waiver at the time of applying for their programme of study.
  - However, the waiver will only be awarded to those who are accepted and fully enrolled in their programme.
- Attendance Requirement
  - Awardees must be based full-time at the University of Limerick.
  - Remote or online-only students are not eligible.

### Irish World Academy Scholarships

#### Fee waiver

A limited number of full and/or partial fee waivers for research students at MA and PhD level. Distributed on an annual basis, the decision will be made by the Academy Director.

#### RTÉ Lyric FM Scholarships

The RTE Lyric FM Scholarships were established in 1999 in support of the MA Classical String Performance at the Irish World Academy of Music and Dance, University of Limerick. The aim of the scholarship is to support young talented string players who wish to study at UL. Since the scholarship was established in 1999, it has been awarded to students from Ireland, Australia, Malaysia, China, Hungary, Romania, Spain, Italy and the US. Recipients may be required to engage in work at the Academy, aimed towards professional development while making a valuable contribution to Academy life.

#### Universal Music UK Sound Foundation Bursary

Universal Music UK Sound Foundation is an independent music education charity, established in 1997 to celebrate the centenary of EMI Records and now generously supported by Universal Music. The foundation’s aim is to improve access to music education in the UK and in Ireland. A bursary was made available to students of Community Music, Irish Music Performance and Music Education at the Irish World Academy of Music and Dance. Patrons: Nick Mason CBE, Sir Paul McCartney, Yoko Ono, Sir Simon Rattle, Sir Cliff Richard, Diana Ross and Tina Turner.

### Education & Health Science Scholarships

#### Participatory Health Research

The Refugee and Migrant Health Partnership (RMHP) is offering six bursaries for migrants to undertake the Postgraduate Diploma in [Participatory Health Research](/study/postgraduate/participatory-health-research-professional-diploma) at University of Limerick's School of Medicine.

Who Can Apply?

- A person who has been through the International Protection process;
- A Beneficiary of Temporary Protection;
- A work visa holder; or
- A member of the Roma community.

What the Bursary Covers

- Full programme fees (€4,000) for the academic year 26/27 which is delivered part-time, and online with one in-person training block on-campus each semester.

How to Apply

Submit a Letter of Interest (maximum 3 A4 pages) outlining:

- Your eligibility for the bursary;
- Confirmation that you meet the programme [eligibility requirements](https://www.ul.ie/study/postgraduate/participatory-health-research-professional-diploma/entry-requirements#course-detail);
- Your interest in Participatory Health Research and the project or idea you would like to develop.

Letters of Interest should be submitted to [phr.diploma@ul.ie](mailto:phr.diploma@ul.ie) by 12pm on 29 July 2026.

All Letters of Interest will be reviewed by the Course Director, Prof. Jon Salsberg, RMHP UL lead, Prof. Anne MacFarlane, and two migrant representatives from the Refugee and Migrant Health Partnership.

Successful bursary applicants will also be required to [apply directly to the programme](/study/postgraduate/participatory-health-research-professional-diploma).

### Sports Scholarships

#### Sports Scholarships

The [UL Sports Scholarships](/sportsscholarships) are designed to support top athletes in pursuing and excelling in their sport while also undertaking academic study at the University of Limerick.

The scholarships are open to all current, full-time, UL students or those who are applying to come to UL for the first time, either through CAO or transferring at an undergraduate or postgraduate level. Applicants must be a member of a national governing body of sport recognised by the Irish Sports Council, in the sport in which they are applying for a scholarship.

### Merit based scholarship for International Students

We have several partial merit-based scholarships available to international postgraduate candidates. Once you submit an application for a programme, you are automatically reviewed for a scholarship. If you are eligible for a scholarship, you will receive the decision at the same time as your offer decision.
