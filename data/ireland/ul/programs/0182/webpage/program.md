## Why Study Midwifery at UL?

The BSc Midwifery course will prepare you to become a competent midwife sensitive to the needs of pregnant women, mothers and their families. The course leads to registration as a midwife (R.M.)

A midwife gives care and support to women and their families during pregnancy, labour, birth and to new mothers and their babies and is responsive to the socio-economic, cultural, educational, physical and psychological needs of women.

This programme is full-time with maternity practice placements at the University of Limerick Hospital Group, St Luke’s Hospital, Kilkenny, Tipperary University Hospital, University Hospital Kerry, and University Hospital Waterford. You will learn about midwifery based on a wellness model of woman-centred care and gain knowledge and skills in midwifery practices. The curriculum includes the study of maternal and neonatal health, reproductive health, physiology, psychology, sociology, pharmacology, research, management, law and ethics.
