Applications for the TELME master’s and scholarship should be submitted via  telme.hypotheses.org/


Designed to develop  educational skills and knowledge, and gain hands-on teaching experience to become a confident and effective language teacher. 

The TELME master's degree offers postgraduate students the tools to become  confident and effective  language teachers across diverse global settings. 

Developed for postgraduate students with a proficiency in English, German, French or Spanish, incoming students should be passionate about teaching and developing a career in language instruction. 

Delivered by partners in Ireland, France, Finland, Poland and Spain, the international structure of this master’s allows students, depending on their language choices, to study at UL in Year 1, before moving to a partner university in Year 2. Options for study in year 2 include Université Rennes 2, France; Abo Academy, Finland; Adam Mickiewicz University, Poland and University of Burgos, Spain. This multi-university approach prepares graduates to thrive in diverse educational environments worldwide.

Experience different academic cultures and develop both practical skills and research expertise as a modern language teaching professional.

Scholarships are available for a number of students through the TELME consortium.
