Mathematics (or maths) is the study of numbers, shapes, patterns, and relationships. It helps develop logical thinking and problem-solving skills and is widely used in subjects such as science, engineering, economics, and computer science.

## Topics in Mathematics

Mathematics is made up of different branches that focus on various aspects of numbers and shapes. These include algebra, geometry, calculus, statistics, and many others, each with its own purpose and uses.

## Arithmetic

Arithmetic deals with basic numbers and operations like addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division. It’s the foundation of all math and is used in everyday tasks like shopping or budgeting.

## Shapes and Measurements

Shapes help us understand the form and size of objects around us, while measurements tell us how big, long, or tall they are.

## Divisibility and Factors

Factors and multiples deal with how numbers are related to each other through division and multiplication.
