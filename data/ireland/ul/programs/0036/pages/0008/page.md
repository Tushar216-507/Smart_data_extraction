# How to apply for professional and flexible programmes

## To make an application please follow instructions below

- Go to [Professional Education Programmes](/study/postgraduate/professional-flexible/alphabetical-list), choose the programme of your choice
- Pay an application fee of €50 (electronic transfer) which allows you to apply for two postgraduate programmes in one online application.
- The final step is to SUBMIT your application.

If you experience any difficulty with your [application,](/gps/contact) [please contact us](/gps/contact).

### Recognition of Prior Learning

A key element in facilitating the return of adult learners to education is the University's Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL). This is a system whereby learning acquired through certified programmes and/or through experience can be acknowledged as a basis for admission onto programmes and/or for gaining exemptions from specific parts of a programme. The purpose of RPL is to expand access to programmes and formal qualifications and to reduce the time and cost of programmes for people who already possess some of the knowledge, skills and competence which a programme seeks to develop. Further information about recognition of [Prior Learning](/priorlearning).

#### Funded Programmes

At UL you'll find a range of programmes that may be partially or fully funded by the Government of Ireland. The courses are designed specifically to address the skills gaps in the economy, and employability is a key focus for the course offerings. The application process for some of these programmes may involve two steps. More details on the application process will be available according to the funding stream. [See more information on funding for Professional learners](/study/postgraduate/applying/funding-your-course/professional-learners).
