### Education & Health Science Scholarships

#### Participatory Health Research

[Participatory Health Research](/study/postgraduate/participatory-health-research-professional-diploma) at University of Limerick's School of Medicine.

Who Can Apply?

- A person who has been through the International Protection process;
- A Beneficiary of Temporary Protection;
- A work visa holder; or
- A member of the Roma community.

What the Bursary Covers

How to Apply

Submit a Letter of Interest (maximum 3 A4 pages) outlining:

- Your eligibility for the bursary;
- Confirmation that you meet the programme [eligibility requirements](https://www.ul.ie/study/postgraduate/participatory-health-research-professional-diploma/entry-requirements#course-detail);
- Your interest in Participatory Health Research and the project or idea you would like to develop.

Letters of Interest should be submitted to [phr.diploma@ul.ie](mailto:phr.diploma@ul.ie) by 12pm on 29 July 2026.

Successful bursary applicants will also be required to [apply directly to the programme](/study/postgraduate/participatory-health-research-professional-diploma).

#### Key details of Essay Competition for 2026/7

[Brightspace](https://learn.ul.ie/d2l/home/59102) no later than 31 January 2026.
Essay length: The essay should be no more than 1,500 words in length (excluding references).
Referencing and Plagiarism: See the university’s ‘Handbook of Academic Regulations and Procedures’ and ‘Research Integrity Policy’ for more details on maintaining academic fairness. Both are viewable at on [UL's Policy Hub](/policy-hub). For more details on what constitutes plagiarism, see Appendix 1 of the ‘Research Integrity Policy’. Referencing should follow the UL Harvard Referencing System guidelines.

Eligibility: To be eligible to enter, you must;

- submit your essay, along with a cover sheet, before the deadline
- submit a CV with your essay submission
- a complete application for the MSc in Accounting (submit a complete application to the MSc in Accounting at [Accounting - MSc | University of Limerick](/study/postgraduate/accounting-msc)before 5pm on 31 January 2026.

A successful student will be informed before 30 June 2026
