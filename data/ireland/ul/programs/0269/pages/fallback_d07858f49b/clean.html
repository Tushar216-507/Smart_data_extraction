The BJP has taken a jab at Rahul Gandhi's recent claims about dismantling patriarchy, with Bansuri Swaraj accusing him of fabricating a deceptive narrative aimed at generating memes instead of meaningful dialogue. She also pointed out what she said was the Congress party's hypocrisy when discussing women's issues.

Delhi Aam Aadmi Party president Saurabh Bharadwaj criticized the Delhi Lakshmi Yojana. He claimed most women in the national capital have been excluded from the scheme. Chief Minister Rekha Gupta stated the scheme reflects Prime Minister Narendra Modi's resolve. Nearly 9.5 lakh women registered for the scheme within one month. The first beneficiaries will receive funds for August on September first.
