### Age requirements

- Applicants must be at least 16 years of age on the 15 January of the year of entry.
- If a candidate below that age wishes to enter the University of Limerick, a special application must be made to the University via the [Admissions Appeals Process](https://www.ul.ie/academic-registry/ul-admissions-appeal-process#:~:text=The%20applicant%20shall%20write%20a,of%20notification%20of%20the%20decision.).

### Course Specific Entry Requirements

- Please see the relevant [course page](https://www.ul.ie/courses/)for course-specific entry requirements.

### Minimum Entry Requirements for Degrees (Level 8)

- Two H5 (Higher Level) grade, AND
- Four O6 (Ordinary Level) grades OR four H7 (Higher Level) grades.

### Minimum Entry Requirements for Certificate/Diploma (Level 6/7)

- Five O6 (Ordinary Level) grades, OR
- Five H7 (Higher Level) grades.

### How to apply?

Applications are only accepted online. The closing date for applications is 01 July 2026.

- Complete the [UL Language Exemption online application](https://unioflimerick.eu.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_819dA0P6aQaQlRI).
- Category 2 and 3: Download and complete the [UL Language Declaration Form](https://www.ul.ie/academic-registry/current-students/policies-procedures-handbooks-0)and submit it with your UL Language Exemption online application.

### Other required information:

To complete your online application you will require the following:

- CAO Application Number
- PPS Number/ National Insurance Number
- Contact Details including email address and phone number
