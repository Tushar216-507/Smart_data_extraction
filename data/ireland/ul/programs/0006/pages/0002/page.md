# EU Applicants

This page is for individuals from/residing in EU/EFTA/UK countries who wish to undertake a full degree at University of Limerick.

EU applicants are considered in competition with Irish school leavers. Consequently, offers cannot be issued until Round 1 offers after the Irish Leaving Certificate results are released. See the [timetable of events](https://www.cao.ie/?page=timetableevents&bb=importantdates) for key dates. Eligible applicants remain on the waiting list and may be offered in order of merit in later rounds, in accordance with CAO procedures. We do not issue conditional offers.

Please note: The definition of EU/Non-EU applicants is based on fee assessment criteria. If you are unsure whether you are an EU or non-EU applicant, please visit the [fee classification page.](https://www.ul.ie/fees)

Information about Erasmus+ and other study abroad and exchange programmes can be found on the [UL Global website.](https://www.ul.ie/global/)

### How do I apply?

Generally, if you are eligible for EU fees and applying for the first year of an undergraduate degree, you must apply via the [Central Applications Office](https://www.cao.ie/) (CAO). The [CAO handbook](https://www.cao.ie/handbook.php) gives details of application procedures and closing dates.

Applicants not eligible for EU fees and transfer applicants (post year 1) must apply directly to the University.

### What are the entry requirements?

#### Recognised qualifications for EU/EFTA/UK countries

Detailed information regarding the recognised qualifications from each EU/EFTA/UK country can be found on the [CAO website](https://www.cao.ie/index.php?page=scoring&bb=studentresources).

This document outlines the recognition of the school leaving examinations for each EU/EFTA/UK country and includes details on the recognition of multi-national qualifications, such as the European Baccalaureate and the International Baccalaureate.

#### Understanding the page for your country

- Firstly, read the section titled "Understanding the page for each country" before navigating to the relevant country page.
- Under the section "What is the minimum I need?" on the country pages, please use the “2 H5 section” for all Level 8 courses at University of Limerick.
- To use Tables 1 Subject Comparisons and 2 Points Conversion, please review the specific subject and points requirements for each course on the [Course Pages](/group/8)or the[Undergraduate Prospectus](/study/undergraduate/why-ul/prospectus).

Applicants presenting older qualifications or qualifications not listed in the document should [submit a query](https://www.ul.ie/admissions-askus) , attaching scanned copies of those qualifications.

### English Language requirements

All University of Limerick students must have a high level of competence in the English language.

- Applicants whose first language is not English must provide evidence of this competence by achieving the minimum standard in a recognised English language test, as outlined below.
- Proficiency test results must normally have issued within two years before the start of your course (i.e. for entry in September 2026, only results attained since 01 February 2024 will be accepted).
