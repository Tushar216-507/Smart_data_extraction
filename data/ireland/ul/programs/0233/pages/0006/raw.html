Advanced Healthcare Practice - MSc Fees

Enhance your clinical expertise, academic capabilities and leadership qualities as an allied healthcare practitioner.

Key information

NFQ Level NFQ Level 9 Major
Duration One year or two years
Attendance Full-time or Part-time
Programme type Taught, Professional / Flexible
Delivery Blended
Start date September / January
Award Masters (MSc)

Full-time fees

  • EU - €7,200
  • Non-EU - €14,700

Part-time fees

  • EU -  €3,900 per annum*
  • Non-EU – €7,800 per annum*

* Year 2 fees are subject to change 

Please note that this programme is not available to students who require a study visa for Ireland. Annual fees are billed by semester.

Further information on fees and payment of fees is available from the Student Fees Office  website. All fee related queries should be directed to the Student Fees Office (Phone: +353 61 213 007 or email student.fees.office@ul.ie).
