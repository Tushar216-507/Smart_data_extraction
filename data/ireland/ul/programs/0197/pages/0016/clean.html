Loïs Lambert-Jouan, BA Irish Dance 

I discovered the BA in Irish Dance through my dance teacher who had studied at UL. Limerick also stood out as the only place that I had seen with a course like this. I have been dancing since I was eight and always knew that I wanted to pursue it professionally. I considered other areas but just could not see myself studying any other subject at University. 

Three years before starting the course, my parents and I came from France to visit the campus. Despite it being too early to begin my studies, I was welcomed by the faculty and was impressed by the facilities. 

The Academy is like a family. We work very closely with our tutors and can go to them with any issues or questions. The variety of classes surprised me. I wasn’t expecting to have so many opportunities for self-development with ballet, yoga and even hip hop as well as my core Irish dance classes. 

One of the most exciting opportunities during my studies was working with Breandán de Gallaí’s youth company on a creative residency on an island off the coast of Donegal. Together, we devised and performed an original piece for the renowned Earagail Arts Festival. Another standout moment in my final year was the privilege of performing with Dr de Gallaí’s professional company, Ériu, at a special anniversary gala – an unforgettable experience!


My advice to incoming students would be to take the course seriously. You have to be mentally and physically prepared to do a programme like this. It is important to eat and sleep well, to take care of yourself and to practice. This will position you to get the best out of the course.
