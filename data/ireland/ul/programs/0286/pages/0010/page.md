Note that students can take Sociology or Politics and International Relations as single honours pathways, meaning that they can focus entirely on these if they wish, without combining them with another subject.

### How are modules (classes) balanced every semester?

Students take five modules in each semester; two modules from each of their chosen subjects and then one additional module.

In the first semester, students take an additional broadening module chosen from a number of modules from across the programme and elsewhere. For the remaining three semesters, for their fifth module, students take research focused modules where they are exposed to best practice in research culminating in the student’s own research project with one-on-one supervision from faculty in their fourth year.
