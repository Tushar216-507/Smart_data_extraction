Ask Anything

**law**, the [discipline](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/discipline) and profession concerned with the customs, practices, and rules of conduct of a [community](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/community) that are recognized as binding by the community. Enforcement of the body of rules is through a controlling [authority](https://www.britannica.com/topic/authority).
