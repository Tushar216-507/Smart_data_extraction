USMI 4 years ago
Prev Article Next Article

Welcome to the ultimate A to Z list of languages! From Afrikaans to Zulu, this article presents an extensive overview of all the languages in the world.

Whether you’re a language enthusiast looking for new words or a student studying for their linguistics exam, this informative guide will provide you with valuable insight into both ancient and modern languages.

Each entry on the list contains information about the language’s origin, current use, and even some fun facts.

FAQ’s

What is the longest language name?

The longest language name is Maori, also known as Te Reo Maori. While the name itself is not particularly long, it is often used with a very long descriptive phrase that can make it seem longer.

For example, the full name of the Maori Language Commission is Te Taura Whiri i te Reo Maori, which means “The Strands of the Weaving of the Maori Language”.

What country has 37 official languages?

The country with 37 official languages is the Republic of India.

The Indian Constitution recognizes 22 languages as official languages of the Union, and some states have also declared additional languages as official at the state level.

However, it’s worth noting that while these languages are recognized, Hindi and English are the two most widely used official languages in India.

How many languages are in the world?

It’s difficult to say exactly how many languages there are in the world, as different sources and scholars have different criteria for what counts as a distinct language. However, it’s generally accepted that there are at least 7,000 living languages in the world today.

Some estimates suggest there could be as many as 10,000 or more, but many of these are endangered or spoken by very small numbers of people.
