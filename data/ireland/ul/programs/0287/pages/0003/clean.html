Age requirements

  • Applicants must be at least 16 years of age on the 15 January of the year of entry.
  • If a candidate below that age wishes to enter the University of Limerick, a special application must be made to the University via the Admissions Appeals Process.

Course Specific Entry Requirements

  • Please see the relevant course pagefor course-specific entry requirements.

Minimum Entry Requirements for Degrees (Level 8)

  • Two H5 (Higher Level) grade, AND
  • Four O6 (Ordinary Level) grades OR four H7 (Higher Level) grades.

Minimum Entry Requirements for Certificate/Diploma (Level 6/7)

  • Five O6 (Ordinary Level) grades, OR
  • Five H7 (Higher Level) grades.

Entry Requirement Details

  • Subjects must include:

Subject Combination

  • English and English Composition count as one subject.
  • Physics, and Physics & Chemistry count as one subject.
  • Chemistry and Physics & Chemistry count as one subject.
  • Physics, Chemistry, and Physics & Chemistry count as two subjects.

  • If you are reapplying to the CAO and were previously granted a second language exemption, please contact Academic Registryto reactivate your exemption advising your current and previous year's CAO number.

How to apply?

Applications are only accepted online. The closing date for applications is 01 July 2026.

  • Complete the UL Language Exemption online application.
  • Category 2 and 3: Download and complete the UL Language Declaration Formand submit it with your UL Language Exemption online application.

Other required information:

To complete your online application you will require the following:

  • CAO Application Number
  • PPS Number/ National Insurance Number
  • Contact Details including email address and phone number
