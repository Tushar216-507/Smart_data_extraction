### Aislinn Kelly

Broadcast journalist Aislinn Kelly is a graduate of the BA in Journalism and Digital Communications at UL.

During her time at UL, Aislinn gained a considerable amount of real-world journalism experience while learning from some of the best in the business.

She says: “There’s nothing quite like the exposure you have in UL to industry leaders. The department maintains strong links to leading media professionals, and the regular guest talks from investigative journalists, renowned broadcasters and media experts throughout the four years were really inspiring."

As the journalism assignments are geared towards being published with a news outlet, Aislinn was able to build her portfolio as she progressed with her studies. Her first articles were published in UL’s student newspaper, An Focal, where she worked on the editorial team for two years. For her co-op placement, Aislinn spent six months at Limerick’s Live 95FM and got her first taste of the fast-paced world of radio.

Aislinn also spent her Erasmus studying in Stockholm, which she says was a “really transformative experience”.
