This online MSc in Engineering Practice is designed to support you in producing a significant, practice-focused research thesis that delivers measurable impact within your organisation. 

It emphasises collaboration both with your fellow learners across diverse industries while giving you the flexibility to build on your previous studies and apply them directly to real-world engineering challenges in your workplace.

The MSc is open to learners who have already completed two Professional Diplomas or 60 ECTS Credits at Level 9 in areas such as aviation, operations management, ICT, artificial intelligence, data analytics, supply chain, or regulatory affairs. 

The programme also supports Recognised Prior Learning for applicants who may have only one Professional Diploma but have a minimum of 5 years relevant work experience.

This programme provides learners not only the opportunity to drive improvements in engineering practices but also gain opportunities to showcase and apply their research work across professional and industry settings.

Particularly suited to mid to senior managers and experienced professionals seeking to strengthen their expertise and broaden their influence within their organisation.
