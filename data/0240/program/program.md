# Bachelor's degree in Prevention, Inclusion, and Rehabilitation (PIR) for Hearing Impairment

Prospective students and current students can find information here about the bachelor's degree program in Prevention, Inclusion, and Rehabilitation (PIR) for Hearing Impairment.
