Do you want to study at TU Delft? Then think carefully about which programme suits you and check the admission requirements to see whether you really qualify for it. We recommend that you participate in several information activities, so that you can check out if the content of the programme meets your expectations. Below you will find an overview of all bachelor's programmes that we offer. Please note that only four programmes are offered fully in English; Aerospace Engineering, Computer Science and Engineering, Earth, Climate and Technology and Nanobiology. All these bachelor's programmes last three years.
