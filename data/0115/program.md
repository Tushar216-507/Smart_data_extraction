## Beschreibung des Studienfachs

The Master's Degree Program is a specialized and interdisciplinary program that serves to train future leaders and founders in digital businesses with expertise in the intersection of management and digital technologies. The program attracts highly talented and ambitious students from all over Europe and the world with academic backgrounds in business and informatics. The Master's Degree Program is jointly offered by the Munich School of Management and the Institute for Informatics at the LMU Munich.

What makes this Master's Degree Program stand out from other programs?

- Excellence: Offered by one of Europe's most renowned universities and taught by leading researchers
- Unique curriculum: Offer of unique and future-oriented courses with distinct entrepreneurial orientation
- Small group teaching: Small cohorts allow for a tailored learning experience in close exchange with lecturers
- Hands-on experience: Students can directly apply their acquired theoretical knowledge in our multiple mandatory practical courses with business partners
- Placement of alumni: Students are provided with outstanding and diverse career perspectives
- International orientation: Given the high share of international students, students can acquire intercultural skills and adopt an international mindset
- Personal growth opportunities: Students can benefit from a personal mentor from leading companies and a large variety of career events

The curriculum consists of five major building blocks:

- Management: Courses address digital technologies from a business perspective
- Management and Digital Technologies: Courses integrate contents from business and informatics. Course formats are highly interactive and will be partly conducted as "project courses" in collaboration with industry partners
- Digital Technologies: Courses address digital technologies from a technological perspective
- Electives: Students have the opportunity to elect courses from business and informatics according to their preferences and interests. A broad catalogue of courses is provided by the Business and Informatics faculties. Each student acquires 24 ECTS credit points, thereof at least 6 credit points in each discipline
- Final Module: Students write their master thesis about a relevant topic at an institute from the Munich School of Management or the Institute for Informatics

More Information: <https://www.mmt.bwl.uni-muenchen.de/index.html>
