## Paying your tuition fees

We charge tuition fees for every year that your course lasts.

How much you pay depend on a number of factors, including:

- Your fee status (Home or Overseas)
- Your course or department
- The year you started your course at Imperial
- What financial support you may be eligible for towards your fees

Important: Compliance with our Tuition Fee Policy forms part of your contractual obligations with Imperial College London. You can find our [Tuition Fees Policy](/media/study---restricted-images/fees-and-funding/Tuition-Fee-Policy-Aug-25.pdf).
