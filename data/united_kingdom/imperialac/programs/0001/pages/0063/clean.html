After receiving decisions on all your applications, it’s time to reply to your offers by the deadline.

  • If you receive all of your offers by 31 March 2026, you must reply to UCAS by 6 May 2026.
  • If you receive all of your offers by 13 May 2026, you must reply to UCAS by 3 June 2026.

If you decide to accept your offer, you can choose to make Imperial either your firm or insurance choice.

Firm

This means we’re your first choice. If your offer is unconditional, then congratulations – you’re in! If it’s conditional, your place will be confirmed after you’ve met the conditions of your offer.

Insurance

This means we’re your backup choice. You’ll be offered a place here if you don’t meet the conditions of your firm choice offer but meet the conditions of your Imperial offer.

Find out how to reply to your offer on UCAS.

Imperial accommodation is well-maintained with round-the-clock support, and the rent includes all bills. If you pick Imperial as your firm choice, we offer a guaranteed place in our accommodation if you want it.*

If Imperial is your firm choice, you can apply for accommodation in mid to late May. You’ll be invited to apply in late July if we're your insurance choice. You can choose your preferences based on your preferred hall, room type, and price.

We aim to make our accommodation the best choice for as many students as possible, but we know it won’t be suitable for everyone. Our Accommodation Office can also offer advice about renting privately and how to find the right place for you.

*You’re guaranteed a place in our accommodation if you make Imperial your firm choice, submit your accommodation application by the July 2026 deadline, meet all conditions of your offer and hold an unconditional offer by (date to be confirmed; as a guide, the unconditional offer deadline for 2025-26 entry was 24 August 2025).

UCAS will send us your A-level, International Baccalaureate (IB), Irish Leaving Certificates, Scottish Advanced Highers and STEP grades on your results day. However, you may need to send us the results for other qualifications.

It’s organised by Imperial and our Students’ Union and gives you the chance to make friends, get involved in loads of events and activities, and get to know your new home at Imperial.
