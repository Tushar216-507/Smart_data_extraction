## What we can help you with?

Our ICT Service Desk team can help staff, students and visitors with a wide range of issues from; connecting to [Wi-Fi](/admin-services/ict/self-service/connect-communicate/wifi-and-networks/), resetting [passwords](/admin-services/ict/self-service/be-secure/passwords-and-extra-security/passwords/), [software installation](/admin-services/ict/self-service/computers-printing/devices-and-software/) and much more.

Contact us online via [ASK](https://servicemgt.imperial.ac.uk/ask) or call us on +44 (0)20 7594 9000.

If you don't have an Imperial email and password, e.g. you are alumni, a prospective student or external supplier, you can contact the ICT Service Desk using our [ASK customer facing service portal](https://servicemgt.service-now.com/cp?id=icl_cp_index). First time using ASK? [Please register online to use ASK our customer facing portal](https://servicemgt.service-now.com/cp?id=sn_user_registration&sys_id=342711311bee2110b6f70dcad34bcbd3).

Our ICT Service Desk team can help staff, students and visitors with a wide range of IT issues. You can contact us in person, online via [ASK](https://servicemgt.imperial.ac.uk/ask) or call us on +44 (0)20 7594 9000.
