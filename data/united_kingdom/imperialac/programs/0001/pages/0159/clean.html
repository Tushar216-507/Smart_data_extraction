The Faculty of Medicine EPSRC University Doctoral Landscape Awards provide funding for 3.5 year PhD studentships. The financial package covers the following:-

  • A tax-free stipend, £23,805per annum (2026-27),for 3 years of research, plus a further 6 months for the writing-up period.
  • Tuition fees paid atUKRI level for 3 years. The writing-up period does not incur tuition fees.
  • A research training support grant (RTSG) of £2,000 per annum for 3 years, paid to the supervisor for supporting the student’s training and research.
  • A conference fund of £300 per annum for 3 years to facilitate registration and attendance at conferences and scientific meetings.
