## Tuition fees

The fee you pay depends on whether you qualify for Home or Overseas fee status.

### Home fees

The UK government sets the maximum tuition fee that universities can charge Home students each year. Imperial’s Home tuition fees are set in line with this cap. For students starting in 2026, the Home fee is £9,790.

In October 2025, the UK government announced that tuition fees will increase in line with inflation in future years. As a result, the final fee for your course may be higher than the current guide price.

### Overseas fees

The tuition fee for Overseas students is set by Imperial and is available [on our course pages.](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/study/courses/)

### Funding your studies

There are different options to fund your studies, depending on your fee status.

Home students can apply for Tuition Fee and Maintenance Loans from the UK government to cover costs during university.

## Thinking about your living costs

It’s really important to consider what your living costs could be while you’re at uni to help you to budget and manage your money.

[You can use our guide to living costs](/study/fees-and-funding/living-costs/) to understand what you could spend on food, leisure and transport. Don’t forget, you’ll also need to think about costs for travel to London and visas (if you need one), accommodation deposits and household essentials. You should also check if there are any additional costs for your course such as study materials or field trips.
