International Student Support

To help you feel settled when you arrive in London, our International Student Support team offers specialist immigration advice, webinars, trips plus online and in-person welcome events. 

Visas and immigration


If you don’t have a full British passport, settled or pre-settled status in the UK, or hold another visa that allows you to study in the UK for your entire course, you’ll need a student visa.


Applying for your student visa? You’ll need a CAS – a Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies. We’ll issue your CAS when you meet all your offer conditions.

We work with our partner Enroly CAS Shield to collect and verify your personal details before we issue your CAS. When you’re eligible to receive a CAS, you’ll get an email to set up an account with Enroly CAS Shield.

Important: Changes to UK Student visas for some nationalities

The UK Government has introduced a new “visa emergency brake” affecting Student visas for nationals of Afghanistan, Cameroon, Myanmar and Sudan from 26 March 2026.
