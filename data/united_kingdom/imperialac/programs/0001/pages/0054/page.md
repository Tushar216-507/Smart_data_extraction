## Be part of our international community

At Imperial you’ll be in good company as an international student. We’re a global academic community where people from different backgrounds and cultures contribute new ideas and fresh approaches to solve complex problems.

## International Student Support

To help you feel settled when you arrive in London, our [International Student Support team](/students/international-students/) offers specialist immigration advice, webinars, trips plus online and in-person welcome events.

## Visas and immigration

If you don’t have a full British passport, settled or pre-settled status in the UK, or hold another visa that allows you to study in the UK for your entire course, you’ll need a student visa.

Applying for your student visa? You’ll need a CAS – a Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies. We’ll issue your CAS when you meet all your offer conditions.

We work with our partner Enroly CAS Shield to collect and verify your personal details before we issue your CAS. When you’re eligible to receive a CAS, you’ll get an email to set up an account with Enroly CAS Shield.

Important: Changes to UK Student visas for some nationalities

The UK Government has introduced a new “visa emergency brake” affecting Student visas for nationals of Afghanistan, Cameroon, Myanmar and Sudan from 26 March 2026.

## International student events

The International Student Support team runs discounted events throughout the year, including walking tours, theatre trips and visits to places like the Houses of Parliament, Windsor Castle and Harry Potter World.

You can also use HOST UK to book a short stay with host families across the UK and join the International Students House Travel Club to book trips and events around the world.
