## Be part of our community

Whether it's joining one of our 370 student-run clubs, representing Imperial in one of a wide range of sport teams, or bringing a new idea to market with Imperial's enterprise ecosystem, discover what you can get involved with while you study with us.

## London/SU

### Living in London

Voted as the top three best student city\*, London offers everything you need on your doorstep. Enjoy bars, restaurants, free museums, outside green spaces, great shopping experiences and so much more.

Plus, London is a global hub for many industries, presenting plenty of opportunities to advance in your career.

\*QS Best Student Cities 2026

### Imperial Students' Union

It’s not all labs and lectures.

At Imperial, we have over 370 student-run clubs, societies and projects. With cultural, faith-led, academic, arts, charitable, sports and more, you’ll find something to be part of. You can also enjoy the Sci-Fi Library, Imperial Cinema, Media Suite and other student spots in the Union.

## Entrepreneurship and innovation

From idea exploration to venture incubation and start-up investment, Imperial has everything you need to turn an idea into tangible, real-world impact.

Visit our [entrepreneurship page](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/entrepreneurship) for more information.

### Enterprise Lab

Supports over 2,000 students annually with mentoring and skills development workshops

### The WE Innovate programme

Awards a prize fund of £30,000\* to outstanding women-led start-ups. \*subject to sponsorship

### The Venture Catalyst Challenge

Welcomes students to pitch ideas to a panel of industry leaders for a chance to win a share of the £100,000\* prize fund. \*subject to sponsorship

### E.quinox Society

Invites new members to contribute ideas and skills to create sustainable and affordable technologies for rural communities in the Global South

## Great Exhibition Road Festival

Visiting London in the summer? On 19-20 June 2027, join us at the Great Exhibition Road Festival. It’s a free annual celebration of science and the arts each summer in South Kensington. Enjoy hands-on workshops, fascinating talks, performances, and installations from iconic museums and research and culture organisations, including Imperial College London, the Natural History Museum, the Science Museum, V&A, and many more.
