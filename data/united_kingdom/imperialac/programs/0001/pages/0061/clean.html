Living in London

Voted as the top three best student city*, London offers everything you need on your doorstep. Enjoy bars, restaurants, free museums, outside green spaces, great shopping experiences and so much more.

Plus, London is a global hub for many industries, presenting plenty of opportunities to advance in your career.

*QS Best Student Cities 2026

Imperial Students' Union

It’s not all labs and lectures.

At Imperial, we have over 370 student-run clubs, societies and projects. With cultural, faith-led, academic, arts, charitable, sports and more, you’ll find something to be part of. You can also enjoy the Sci-Fi Library, Imperial Cinema, Media Suite and other student spots in the Union.

Great Exhibition Road Festival

Visiting London in the summer? On 19-20 June 2027, join us at the Great Exhibition Road Festival. It’s a free annual celebration of science and the arts each summer in South Kensington. Enjoy hands-on workshops, fascinating talks, performances, and installations from iconic museums and research and culture organisations, including Imperial College London, the Natural History Museum, the Science Museum, V&A, and many more.
