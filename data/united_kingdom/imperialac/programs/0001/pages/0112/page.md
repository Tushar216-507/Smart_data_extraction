## How to apply for Imperial Inspires

## Accordion - How to apply for Imperial Inspires

- [Are Imperial Inspires scholarships available for all courses at Imperial?](#tabpanel_1779627)
- [Is there a separate application process for Imperial Inspires?](#tabpanel_1779629)
- [Who can apply for an Imperial Inspires scholarship?](#tabpanel_1779630)
- [When should I apply to Imperial to be considered for the Imperial Inspires scholarship?](#tabpanel_1779631)

We offer Imperial Inspires scholarships for all our undergraduate courses, including integrated Master’s courses, such as MEng and MSci courses, as well as selected postgraduate taught courses.

Imperial Inspires scholarships are not available for postgraduate research and doctoral courses.

Yes. If an Imperial Inspires scholarship is available for the course you apply for and you meet the scholarship eligibility criteria and the minimum entry requirements for the course, you will be invited to apply to be considered for an Imperial Inspires scholarship.

We’re currently offering Imperial Inspires scholarships for undergraduate courses and selected postgraduate taught courses to international students who are eligible to pay our Overseas rate of tuition. In future years, we plan to launch additional scholarships for students from the UK.

We strongly encourage you to apply as early as possible after applications open.

Undergraduate applications for 2027 entry can be submitted to UCAS from 2 September 2026. Postgraduate taught applications for 2027 entry can be submitted from September 2026.

Read more about our [application process and deadlines](/study/apply/).

This ensures full consideration for both your academic application and any scholarship opportunities linked to your degree programme.

No. Attending our events does not form part of the scholarship or admissions selection process.

Imperial Inspires is designed to recognise students who combine academic excellence with the potential to make a meaningful contribution to their field and to society. We are particularly interested in applicants who can demonstrate curiosity, initiative, leadership skills and a global outlook.

Imperial Inspires is a highly prestigious and competitive programme. We expect strong interest from outstanding applicants around the world

## Terms and conditions for Imperial Inspires scholarships

## Accordion - Terms and conditions

- [Is the Imperial Inspires scholarship guaranteed for the full duration of my course?](#tabpanel_1779651)
- [Are there conditions for retaining the Imperial Inspires scholarship each year?](#tabpanel_1779652)
- [Can the Imperial Inspires scholarship be withdrawn?](#tabpanel_1779653)
- [Can I appeal or reapply within the same cycle?](#tabpanel_1779654)

The scholarship is intended to support you for each year of your degree, provided you maintain satisfactory academic progress and meet any continuing requirements set by Imperial.

Yes. As with most scholarship programmes, you will be expected to maintain good academic standing and engagement with your course.

The scholarship may be withdrawn in circumstances such as withdrawal from your course, failure to meet academic expectations, or breaches of university regulations.

Scholarship decisions are final within each application cycle and there is no formal appeals process.

However, if you apply again to Imperial in a future admissions cycle and meet the eligibility criteria at that time, you may be considered again for an Imperial Inspires scholarship.

## Payment and other funding

## Accordion - Payment and other funding

- [How is the scholarship paid?](#tabpanel_1779660)
- [Can I combine Imperial Inspires with other funding?](#tabpanel_1779661)
- [Will I need to pay the remaining tuition fee myself?](#tabpanel_1779662)

Your Faculty will determine how the scholarship is paid. The scholarship may be split equally between your tuition fees and living costs (offered as a maintenance stipend) or the full award may be used towards your tuition fees.

Successful applicants will be informed of how their scholarship payment will be arranged after selection.

In some cases, it may be possible to receive additional funding alongside an Imperial Inspires scholarship. This will depend on the terms of each award and will be confirmed as part of the selection process.

Yes. Imperial Inspires is a partial scholarship, so you will be responsible for covering any remaining tuition fees and living costs not covered by the award.

## Deferring your place or changing your course

## Accordion - Deferrals and changes

- [Can I defer my scholarship to a later year?](#tabpanel_1779671)
- [If I change my course after receiving the scholarship, will I keep it?](#tabpanel_1779672)
- [What happens if I withdraw from the course?](#tabpanel_1779673)

No, the scholarship cannot be deferred. If you defer your place at Imperial, you will need to re-apply for the scholarship in the year you start your studies. This also applies to students who need to complete compulsory national or military service before starting university.

Scholarships are linked to specific courses. If you change course, your eligibility will be reassessed and the award may not continue.

If you withdraw or suspend your studies, your scholarship will end.
