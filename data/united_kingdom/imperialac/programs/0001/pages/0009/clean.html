Planning to live at home?

Not everyone lives in halls. Lots of Imperial students live at home during their studies. We’re here to support you too and to make you feel part of the Imperial community from day one.

Our New Students website has loads of information about our welcome events, what to expect and how you can prepare for life at Imperial.

Being from London, I liked the idea of staying at home – it made sense financially. But I've still been able to feel part of the Imperial community.
