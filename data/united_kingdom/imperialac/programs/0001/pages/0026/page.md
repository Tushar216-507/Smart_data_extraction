## Nurture your talent

Imperial students are always in high demand – you might even get headhunted before you graduate.

That’s because many of the world’s top employers know our graduates have a reputation for being exceptionally bright, entrepreneurial and hard-working.

- We’re ranked number 1 in the UK for graduate prospects by The Guardian University Guide 2026.
- In the 2021/22 Graduate Outcomes Survey, over 93% of our graduates who completed a postgraduate taught course between August 2021 and July 2022 entered work or further study.\*

\*Higher Education Statistics Agency (HESA), Graduate Outcomes Survey 2021/22

From your first day here, you’ll have access to a huge range of support services to help you prepare for – and secure – the next step in your academic or professional life.

## After you graduate

### Careers Service

Imperial sets you up for a rewarding career in innovative fields. We’re here to help you get the most from your future with our [Careers Service](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/careers/).

You’ll benefit from:

- professional advice, guidance and support to develop your CV and maximise online professional networking
- access to a year-round programme of events
- opportunities to connect with alumni mentors

## Studying at Imperial Business School?

You can also[access tailored career support](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/business-school/programmes/careers/)from the Business School’s Careers team.
