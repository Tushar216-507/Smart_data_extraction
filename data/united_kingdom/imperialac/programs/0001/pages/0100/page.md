## Alternative Guide to Postgraduate Funding

Charities are an under-utilised source of funding for education.

These voluntary bodies vary considerably in size and resources, from huge multi-million pound organisations like Oxfam to small trust funds run by a few volunteers.

Whether you are looking for £500 for a research trip, or you need additional funding to finance a Master's or PhD course, it's worth exploring what this very important sector could offer you.

Imperial subscribes to the externally-run [Alternative Guide to Postgraduate Funding Online gateway,](https://www.postgraduate-funding.com/) which contains a searchable database of almost 1,000 charities.

If you are an Imperial student you can use your email address to access the portal directly.
