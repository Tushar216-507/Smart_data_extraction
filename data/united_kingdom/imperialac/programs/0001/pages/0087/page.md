[The Disability Advisory Service](/admin-services/disability-advisory-service/) provides support for all students at Imperial.

The Disability Advisory Service has guidance for prospective students and offer holders about disclosing, how to request support and adjustments, advice about accommodation and which teams and networks are there for you at Imperial and in your department.

There’s also information for parents and carers, including how to contact the Service.

[Student Counselling and Mental Health Advice Service](/students/counselling/) offers individual and group counselling, workshops and courses, as well as access to SilverCloud, an online platform for self-help programmes.

The Service can also provide advice for coping with feelings of anxiety, stress, loneliness, low mood, depression, grief and loss, or problems at home.

The [Student Support Zone](/students/student-support-zone/) has [advice on mental health](/students/student-support-zone/your-health/mental-health/), including tips about how you can support your mental wellbeing.
