Australia is one of Imperial’s top research collaborators and we have connections with many leading universities and organisations in the region including University of Auckland, University of Otago, ANU, University of Melbourne, Monash University, the University of Queensland, CSIRO, the Australian Academy of Science, EMBL Australia, and Geoscience Australia.

Through recent collaborations, our researchers have helped confirm Australia’s Yarrabubba crater as the [world’s oldest preserved impact structure](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/news/194833/earths-oldest-known-impact-might-have/); helped to better understand [underwater earthquakes in the Hikurangi subduction zone](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/news/193050/treasure-trove-quake-clues-could-unearthed/) near New Zealand; and shown that an [antiviral reducing influenza spread in ferrets](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/news/196893/antiviral-reducing-influenza-spread-ferrets-could/) could help control flu outbreaks.

In December 2020, [Professor Miia Kivipelto was awarded the 2020 Ryman Prize](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/news/210133/professor-kivipelto-receives-2020-ryman-prize/) for over 20 years of research into Alzheimer's Disease and dementia. The annual prize was by the Right Honourable Jacinda Arden, Prime Minister of New Zealand.

Many of our undergraduate courses offer an option of a year abroad with previous exchange students in Bioengineering, Chemical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Earth Science and Engineering and Mechanical Engineering studying at partner universities in Australia.

As a student in London, you'll be welcomed into a city where over 300 languages are spoken and where over 100,000 students from 200 countries come to study each year. Imperial has been welcoming students from Oceania for many years.

Your relationship with Imperial does not end when you graduate.

[remain in the UK and seek work](/students/international-students/visas-and-immigration/working-in-the-uk/working-in-the-uk-after-your-studies/) after you have completed your studies. We can provide specific advice, guidance and assistance with the [Graduate visa](/students/international-students/visas-and-immigration/working-in-the-uk/working-in-the-uk-after-your-studies/graduate-visa/), which enables graduates to work in the UK for two years after completing an undergraduate or Master's degree, or three years after completing a PhD. We can also provide limited advice and guidance on the Skilled Worker, Global Talent, Tier 5 (Government Authorised Exchange) and Tier 5 (Youth Mobility Scheme) work visas.
