Research divisions

Neurology

The Division of Neurology undertakes ground-breaking research at laboratory level and brings the successes of that research to the bedside for the benefit of patients.

Learn more

Neuroscience

The Division of Neuroscience investigates mechanisms of neurodegeneration and neuroinflammation common to many neurological disorders and is also home to the Multiple Sclerosis and Parkinson’s Tissue Bank.

Learn more

Psychiatry

The Division of Psychiatry's mission is to conduct pioneering, innovative research that results in more effective prevention, assessment and treatment of psychiatric disorders.

Learn more
