Finding your new home

Your new home away from home

To study at your best, it’s important you feel at home. Our Student Accommodation Office is on hand to help you find a place to live and get settled in. 

Whether you prefer accommodation that's been designed with postgraduates in mind or you’re considering London's huge range of private rental options, we can help you find a home that's right for you. 

Intro

GradPad

GradPad provide private halls with all-inclusive rents exclusively for postgraduate students at Imperial and other London universities.

They’re available in two London locations:

• Griffon Studios, Battersea
• Wood Lane Studios, White City

Find prices, facilities and how to book on the GradPad website.

Explore GradPad

Private accommodation

You’ll have plenty of options for private accommodation in London, from flat shares and shared houses to studio apartments.

If your course starts in late September, the best time to start looking for a place to live is between late June and August. Our Student Accommodation Office has lots of information about popular areas and guidance about average rents.

Find out more

Silwood Park Campus

If your course is based at our Silwood Park Campus, you can choose from single and double accommodation in our five on-site halls.

Learn about Silwood Park

Living at home

You may consider living at home and travelling into university. Many local Imperial students already do this to cut down on living costs. Rest assured, there are plenty of opportunities to make sure that you feel part of the Imperial community from day one: 

Living at home

  • Students’ Union
  • Clubs and societies
Students’ Union

The Students’ Union is a community of all our students working to achieve the best experience for every student. Every one of the Union’s members is able to lead, create and influence change in every one of our activities, campaigns and services. 

Clubs and societies

Getting involved in student activities is a great way to meet new people. There are over 370 clubs, societies and projects which are all student-led and student-run. This is one of largest varieties of student led activities at a University in the UK. 

You’re guaranteed to find a group that shares your interests, and if not, create your own!
