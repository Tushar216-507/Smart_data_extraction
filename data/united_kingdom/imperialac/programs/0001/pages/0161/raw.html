International students

Imperial is a place for everyone, where you can be yourself, find your people and pursue your passion. Our community spans 150+ nationalities at home in London – ranked No.1 city in the UK and Europe for Students by QS Best Student Cities 2026.

Top links:

Important: Changes to UK Student visas for some nationalities

The UK Government has introduced a new “visa emergency brake” affecting Student visas for nationals of Afghanistan, Cameroon, Myanmar and Sudan from 26 March 2026.
