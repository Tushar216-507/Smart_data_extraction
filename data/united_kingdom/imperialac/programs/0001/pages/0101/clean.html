We're here to help with any questions you may have about tuition fees and student funding.

We have different teams for different types of queries so explore below which team is best placed to help you.

Student Financial Support team

For information on scholarships, please see our Scholarships listings. Each scholarship will include a contact email for specific questions. 

For all general enquiries relating to scholarships and student funding at Imperial College London, please contact student.hub@imperial.ac.uk.

For specific enquiries relating to your bursary or financial support (including the Student Support Fund for hardship assistance), please contact us at: student.funding@imperial.ac.uk. 

Tuition fees enquiries

For questions about tuition fees, please use the relevant details below to ensure your query is dealt with by the right team:

  • Visa and CAS enquiries: admissions-cas@imperial.ac.uk
  • Request a formal receipt for payment of fees: studentpay.queries@imperial.ac.uk
  • Request a tuition fee refund: tuition.fees@imperial.ac.uk
  • General enquiries about tuition fees: tuition.fees@imperial.ac.uk

If you wish to book a call with the Student Fees and Credit Control teams, you can use our online booking system.
