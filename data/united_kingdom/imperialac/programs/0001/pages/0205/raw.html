Digital Education Team

If you are engaged in any online digital learning projects or are looking to explore the digital learning option for one of your courses, the Digital Education Team is here to support you.

We will work alongside you to identify, scope and develop the best solutions for your digital learning requirements. We will also support and advise you in the areas of course design and development, project and resource management, and course implementation and evaluation. Please see the Digital Education Office page for more information.
