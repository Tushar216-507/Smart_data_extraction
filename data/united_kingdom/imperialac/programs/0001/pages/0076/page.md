Our Professional Doctorates are for working professionals who are looking to make a unique contribution to their area of practice.

At Imperial, they're available through the following qualifications.

### MD(Res) in the Faculties of Medicine and Engineering

- MD(Res) in Bioengineering Research offered within the [Department of Bioengineering](/engineering/departments/bioengineering/study/postgraduate-research/doctoral/)
- MD(Res) in Mechanical Engineering Research offered within the [Department of Mechanical Engineering](/engineering/departments/mechanical-engineering/study/phd/phd-opportunities/)
- MD(Res) Clinical Medicine Research offered within various departments within the [Faculty of Medicine](/medicine/research-and-impact/a-z-directory/)
