This information is applicable to both 2025-26 and 2026-27 academic years.

Guidance documents have now been published for the 2026-27 academic year.

Imperial College London accepts a number of visiting (non-degree) students each year:

- Most visiting students at Imperial are undertaking a degree at another institution (including exchange students).
- We admit a small number of visiting students on an independent basis.
- Visiting students are not awarded a degree by Imperial but may be issued a transcript for any official assessments they undertake and may use the credit gained to contribute to a degree awarded by another institution.

Whether you are a prospective exchange or non-exchange student please ensure that you read the detailed information below before making a visiting student application.

If you are an Imperial staff member please read our guidance about hosting visiting students on our [internal webpages](/admin-services/placements/information-for-staff-at-imperial-college-london/hosting/).

Students who are admitted by Imperial as Visiting Students will find useful orientation on this page.

Imperial will consider applications for the following:

#### Undergraduate level

- Independent projects; or
- A study plan composed from Imperial undergraduate Bachelor or Master's courses (with or without a project)

#### Postgraduate taught (Master's) level

- Independent projects; or
- The Imperial Business School have a [student exchange programme](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/business-school/programmes/incoming-exchange-students/)

#### Postgraduate Doctoral level

We welcome applications for placements at Doctoral level.

Please note that any applicant at any level of study who is seeking to undertake only an independent research period or a project should have the provisional agreement of a supervisor at Imperial to host them before applying.

- Study period with access to Year 1 of an undergraduate course
- Study period based on the undergraduate medicine curriculum
- Study period based on the undergraduate medical biosciences curriculum
- Study period at undergraduate level in Imperial Business School
- Study period at postgraduate doctoral (PhD) level in Imperial Business School, except within the framework of an official international exchange agreement
- Study period at undergraduate level in the Department of Mathematics and Department of Computing, except within the framework of an official student exchange agreement
- Non-exchange visiting students are not normally able to undertake study periods at postgraduate taught (Master's) level. Enquiries can be made to an academic department, but they are usually not available. However, Imperial does host students for Master's level projects for which a supervisor must be provisionally secured before the student applies as a Visiting Student.

Penultimate or final year undergraduate medical students seeking clinical internships/electives study periods at one of the NHS teaching hospitals partnered with Imperial should read the guidance on the [Faculty of Medicine website](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/medicine/study/undergraduate/electives-and-clinical-attachments/).

#### Summer school

If you wish to apply for a Summer School (for university level students) please do not apply as a visiting student.

Instead, you should follow the application and admissions information for the Summer School of interest. Explore [Summer at Imperial](/study/visit/summer-schools/).

Imperial is also unable to offer study opportunities involving teaching from more than one department or from both the undergraduate curriculum (BEng/MEng/BSc/MSci) and postgraduate curriculum (MSc/MRes), unless:

- that teaching is normally available to degree students of the admitting academic department
- you are an exchange student and you have been advised by the exchange programme co-ordinator at your home university or the exchange programme co-ordinator at Imperial that such access can be arranged

Admission for less than a full academic year is not possible unless:

- you are an exchange student and you have been advised by the exchange programme co-ordinator at your home university or the exchange programme co-ordinator at Imperial that such access can be arranged

Please ensure you have read the guidance for applicants.

#### Student exchange partners

Staff working in partner universities should read: [Guidance for universities who are student exchange partners](/admin-services/placements/student-exchanges/information-for-student-exchange-partner-universities/).

You should always apply as a visiting (non-degree) student if you have been awarded an Erasmus+ Traineeship grant by your home (non-UK) university and:

- you are currently a PhD student and would continue to be registered as such at your home university for the duration of the planned research period at Imperial; or
- you are currently a Bachelor or Master's-level student and would continue to be registered as such at your home university for the duration of the planned research period at Imperial.

Please note that Imperial will classify any Erasmus+ Traineeship student it admits as a non-exchange (visiting) student.

Imperial will no longer automatically waive the tuition fee for a student it hosts who has Erasmus+ (Traineeship) status. The student must discuss tuition fees with any proposed supervisor, and it is the decision of the host department whether the tuition fee can be reduced or waived.

For queries about Erasmus+ Traineeships, please email Adrian Hawksworth, Assistant Registrar (Placements) at [a.hawksworth@imperial.ac.uk](mailto: a.hawksworth@imperial.ac.uk)

## Undergraduate Research Opportunities Programme (UROP)

Imperial's Undergraduate Research Opportunities Programme (UROP) is an alternative mechanism for hosting undergraduate students from other universities for research experiences (often a project).

Applications for UROP are made informally to potential supervisors.

UROP research experiences, which are agreed to by the host department, are then subject to a UROP registration. It is the decision of the host department to use the UROP option for registration.

#### Who is eligible?

Students currently at universities outside the UK will normally be ineligible, unless they have UK or Ireland nationality.

Details on eligibility are available on the [UROP website](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/urop/what-is-urop/).

#### Tuition fees

There are currently no tuition fees applicable to a UROP registration.

#### More information

Further details are available on the [UROP website](http://www.imperial.ac.uk/urop).
