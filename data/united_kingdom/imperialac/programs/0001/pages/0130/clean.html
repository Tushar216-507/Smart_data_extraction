This information is for students starting their course in autumn 2026.

What you'll get

Each scholarship covers:

  • Tuition fees paid in full (Home or Overseas rate).
  • Stipend for living costs equating to £16,500 per annum.
  • £1,500 one-off payment for the purchase of a laptop or other educational equipment (to be paid in first year).

The award is for a maximum of 4 years, excluding repeat periods of study. If you are studying a course longer than 4 years, you are eligible to apply, but will still only receive funding for 4 years, if successful.

There is one scholarship available for undergraduate study.

Check if you're eligible

To be eligible for the Sanctuary Scholarship you must:

  • Have Home or Overseas Fee Status
  • Submit your UCAS application and have received an offer to study an undergraduate course starting in Autumn 2026, by Friday 24th April 2026
  • Fall into one of the following immigration status groups: 
    • A refugee – a person who has been granted refugee status either in the UK or in another country
    • A person who has been granted humanitarian protection or some other form of protective leave to remain in the UK (including a Ukrainian Family Scheme, Ukraine Extension Scheme or Ukrainian Sponsorship Scheme visa).
    • An asylum seeker – a person who has made a claim within the UK for refugee status.

All eligible applicants must be able to evidence their immigration status. If you have an uncertain immigration status, please get in touch with Student Financial Support directly to discuss your situation.

How to apply

To apply for a Sanctuary Scholarship, please complete our Sanctuary Scholarship application form by the deadline of 11:59pm (UK local time), Friday 24th April 2026.

If you have any questions regarding your eligibility or this scholarship please contact us at scholarships@imperial.ac.uk.

When you'll hear

If you are successful, we'll notify you by email no later than 30th June 2026. If you have not heard from us by this date please assume you have been unsuccessful in your application.
