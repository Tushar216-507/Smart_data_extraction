If you are an international student considering studying at Imperial, please email us at international-recruitment@imperial.ac.uk. Our friendly International Student Recruitment team is here to answer any questions you may have.

If you're a current international student at Imperial and you have questions about student visas, ATAS, or other immigration issues, please email the International Student Support team at international@imperial.ac.uk.

If you are a visiting student and you have a question about student exchanges and other opportunities for visiting students at Imperial, please check out our Visiting student pages and email the relevant Admissions Team if you have any questions.
