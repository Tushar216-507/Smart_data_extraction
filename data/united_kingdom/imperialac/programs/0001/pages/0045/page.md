## What's your situation?

## I met my offer

If you’ve met the conditions of your offer, Congratulations! Your place at Imperial will be confirmed in [UCAS Hub](https://www.ucas.com/dashboard) (look for 'Unconditional firm'). If your status shows as 'Unconditional firm (UF)' this means that your place at Imperial has been confirmed.

You don’t need to do anything right now – we’ll contact you soon with everything you need to prepare for joining.

We carefully check all results. If you think there’s been a mistake, contact the Admissions team through our [Admissions Enquiry form](/study/apply/contact/) as soon as possible.

You must meet the exact conditions of your offer, including grades, subjects and deadlines. Equivalent tariff points are not accepted.

Your place is confirmed – congratulations.

If you need a student visa, we will contact you with next steps, including your CAS.

We’ll also contact you about enrolment and accommodation.

We don’t usually consider mitigating circumstances after results are released.

If something happened that affected your exam performance, and you have contacted your exam board, that information will have already been taken into consideration. If you have not contacted your exam board, please do so as soon as possible.

If you want to change course within the same department, contact the [department Admissions team](/study/apply/contact/).

In many cases, it is often easier to transfer after you start, during your first year.

Changing to a different department is usually not possible, as courses are often full.

To request a deferral, contact the  Admissions Team through our [Admissions Enquiry form](/study/apply/contact/).

Please remember:

- Deferrals are not guaranteed
- If your deferral is approved, it is very unlikely that we can reverse this decision later. So please be sure you want to defer before making your request.

If your request is not approved, will can:

- Start your course this year, as planned; or
- Withdraw and apply again next year.

If your offer is still conditional, we might still be waiting for your results.

If we’ve asked for specific results, you must send them to us before we can confirm your place.

Contact the [Admissions team](/study/apply/contact/) if anything is missing.

## I'm a UK student and want to be considered for Imperial's reconsideration pool

If you are from the UK, qualify for our [contextual admissions scheme](/study/apply/undergraduate/process/admissions-schemes/) and were not made an offer when you applied, you may be able to apply to our reconsideration pool.

This allows us to review your application again if places are available.

## I met my offer and I'm an international student

You can apply if:

- You were identified as eligible for the reconsideration pool in your decision
- You have your results and you meet the minimum entry requirements for the course you originally applied for

The reconsideration pool form is open 9.00 - 13.00 (UK time) on Results Day (Thursday 13 August 2026).

You must submit your application during this time window.

No - we will not contact you on Results Day.

If you want to be reconsidered, you must submit the form yourself within the deadline.

We understand this can feel disappointing, but please read this section carefully before taking any action.

## I didn't get the grades for my offer

- [Appealing results and changed grades](#tabpanel_1359853)
- [If Imperial is your Firm Choice](#tabpanel_1528020)
- [If Imperial is your Insurance Choice](#tabpanel_1528021)
- [If you have been rejected and want to appeal](#tabpanel_1359841)
- [If you haven't received your results](#tabpanel_1359840)

If your grades have changed following a formal appeal and you now meet or exceed your exact offer, your school or exam board must send official confirmation the [Admissions team](/study/apply/contact/).

We accept updated grades until 31 August 2026.

If you still don’t meet your offer conditions after the formal appeal, the outcome displayed on your UCAS Hub will still stand.

Important: We are aware of reports of marking discrepancies affecting some CBSE examination results. Applicants who wish to request a review should follow CBSE's verification and re-evaluation process.

Any revised results must be received directly from CBSE by 31 August 2026. We are unable to guarantee that results received after this date will be considered for 2026 entry.

Please note that we are unable to accept remarked resultssubmitteddirectly by applicants. All revised results must be verified either through your examination board, or via Qualification Check where this is included in your offer conditions.

If you have narrowly missed your offer, we will review your results carefully.

In some cases, we may still be able to confirm your place.

You don’t need to contact us – we’ll update your decision in [UCAS Hub](https://www.ucas.com/dashboard#/).

If we are unable to confirm your place, your application will be considered by your insurance choice.

If you are unsuccessful by your Firm Choice and have narrowly missed our offer, we will review your case.

In some cases, your place may still be confirmed.

Check [UCAS Hub](https://www.ucas.com/dashboard#/) for updates - you do not need to contact us.

If you are unsuccessful with both choices, you will be entered into Clearing.

Decisions made after results are final and cannot be appealed at this stage.

Due to high competition, we can only confirm places for applicants who meet all offer conditions, or in limited cases, those who narrowly miss them.

If your [UCAS Hub](https://www.ucas.com/dashboard) status shows “unsuccessful”, this is the final outcome after review.

We cannot provide your exam results.

If you don’t have them, contact your school or college immediately .

## I want to change my UCAS choices

You may be able to change your plans, but options are limited once decisions are confirmed.

Read the guidance below carefully before taking any action, as some changes are not possible at this stage.

## I want to change my UCAS choices

- [You have Imperial as your insurance choice but you want to come here instead](#tabpanel_1359858)
- [Imperial is your Firm Choice, but you now want to go to your Insurance Choice](#tabpanel_1359859)
- [If you no longer want to study at Imperial](#tabpanel_1359860)

If you have accepted an unconditional place at your firm choice, you cannot change to Imperial at this stage.

If you your place at Imperial is confirmed, changing to your insurance choice is not guaranteed.

First, contact your insurance choice to check if they would be willing to accept you through Clearing.

If they agree, you can release yourself into Clearing via [UCAS Hub](https://www.ucas.com/dashboard#/) .

If you miss this deadline, you will need to contact the [Admissions team](/study/apply/contact/) to request release into Clearing.

You can decline your place in [UCAS Hub](https://www.ucas.com/dashboard#/).

This cannot be reversed, and you will given up your place at Imperial.

Once you decline, you can enter UCAS Clearing and apply elsewhere.

If you miss the deadline, you will need to contact the [Admissions team](/study/apply/contact/) to request that they release you into Clearing.
