Accepted qualifications

Entry requirements
  • Accepted qualifications

We accept a wide range of international qualifications for postgraduate study at Imperial.

We've provided general guidance in the country and region index below on how qualifications awarded by public institutions overseas may satisfy our minimum academic admission requirements.

But you should always refer to our course pages, or to the relevant Admissions team, for specific guidance on requirements for courses of interest. 

Requirements or acceptability may also differ for other types of institution.

The demanding and competitive nature of our courses means that individual departments, centres and institutes may set higher admissions standards than the minimum requirements listed in our index.

Specific grade averages may also be required in addition to the degree classification stated. This may not be detailed in the index below. 

Holistic approach

While academic grades are important, it's not the only factor that we take into account during selection, so even if you meet all of our academic requirements that does not automatically guarantee admission.

We also consider other factors such as suitability for the course and your proficiency in English.

Your academic references are also important and some courses may require relevant work experience.

Updates to entry requirements

We review our requirements annually. Updates usually happen in September each year before the next academic year of entry.

Applicants may be considered for admission to a postgraduate doctoral course if they hold:

  • a Bàtxelor degree; and
  • a Master's degree with grades specified above.

Postgraduate taught study

Applicants for a postgraduate doctoral course should also hold a Magister degree with at least 7.5/10 from a mainstream university.

Doctoral study

Applicants for a postgraduate doctoral course should hold a Master's degree with a grade of 4/5 or better, although a grade of 4.5/5 is normally preferred.

A minimum of an Upper Second Class honours is however normally required for most of our courses.

Postgraduate doctoral study

Applicants for a postgraduate doctoral course should hold:

  • a first degree, ideally with Honours; and
  • a Master's degree.

Applicants who graduated after the Bologna reforms should hold a 1st cycle Bachelor's degree of at least 3 years' duration with a grade of 2 or better.

Postgraduate doctoral study

Applicants for a postgraduate doctoral course should hold a Master's degree with a grade of 4/5 or better, although a grade of 4.5/5 is normally preferred.

Pakistan

To be considered for admission to postgraduate study, applicants should have completed a four year Bachelor's degree awarded from 2016 onwards from one of the prestigious universities funded by the Higher Education Commission (HEC). 

Degrees should have been awarded with a final overall mark of 75% or better.

Selectors may demand a higher mark from some institutions.

Where Bachelor degrees were awarded before or up to 2015, a Master's degree is also normally required, on top of a Bachelor's degree.

Palestine

To be considered for admission to a Master's e.g. MSc, MRes, MBA etc, applicants should have completed a Bachelor's degree awarded with an overall mark of ‘Very Good' or Excellent

Applicants for a postgraduate doctoral course should hold:

  • a Master's degree with Very Good or Excellent
  • a Bachelor's degree at the requisite level
Papua New Guinea

To be considered for admission to a Master's e.g. MSc, MRes, MBA, etc, applicants should hold a Bachelor's degree with a minimum of Lower Second Class Honours.

A minimum of Upper Second Class honours is however normally required for most of our courses.

Applicants for a postgraduate doctoral course should hold:

  • a first degree with a First or Upper Second Class Honours; and
  • a Master's degree with 65% or better.
Paraguay

To be considered for admission to a Master's e.g. MSc, MRes, MBA etc, applicants should have completed a minimum of four years study and been awarded a Licenciado or professional degree from a mainstream university with a minimum overall mark of 3.9/5 or better.

Applicants fora postgraduate doctoral course should hold:

  • a Master's (Maestria) degree with a minimum overall mark of 3.9/5; and
  • a four year first degree at the prescribed level
Peru

To be considered for admission to a Master's e.g. MSc, MRes, MBA etc, applicants should have completed a minimum of five years study and been awarded a Licenciado or professional degree from a mainstream university with a minimum overall grade of 14/20 or better; most of our courses will require higher marks.

Applicants fora postgraduate doctoral course should hold: 

  • a Maestria degree with minimum overall grade of 14/20 or better; and
  • a first degree at the prescribed level.
The Philippines

To be considered for admission to postgraduate study, applicants should have completed a four year Bachelor degree and Master's degree.

Departments seek applicants achieving 3.5/4 (ascending scale) or 1.5 (descending scale) or better overall.

Poland

To be considered for admission to a Master's e.g. MSc, MRes, MBA etc, applicants should have completed a minimum of four years study and been awarded the Magister, with an overall grade of dobry or better.

Post-Bologna applicants should have completed a three year 1st Cycle Bachelor's degree with a minimum of 180 ECTS points and a final grade of dobry or better.

Licencjat or Inzynier degrees taken since 2002 as a post-Bologna degree are also acceptable with a final grade of dobry or better.

Applicants for a postgraduate doctoral course should hold: 

  • a Magister or a Master's with dobry or better; and
  • a Bachelor's degree.
Portugal

Postgraduate taught study

To be considered for admission to a Master's e.g. MSc, MRes, MBA etc, applicants should have studied at a mainstream university and obtained the Licenciatura/Licenciado or a 1st Cycle Bachelor's degree with a minimum of 180 ECTS points and a final overall grade of 13/20 or better.

A minimum of 15/20 is however normally required for most of our courses.

Departments may look for higher minimum grades from particular universities.

Doctoral study

Applicants for a postgraduate doctoral course should hold:

  • a Mestrado or a Master's degree at the level stated above; and
  • a Bachelor's degree awarded at the prescribed level.

Romania

Postgraduate taught study

To be considered for admission to a Master's e.g. MSc, MRes, MBA etc, applicants should have:

  • completed a minimum of four years study and been awarded the Diploma de Licenta/Diploma de Inginer degree with a minimum final grade of 8/10 or better; or
  • obtained a 1st cycle Bachelor's degree with a minimum of 180 credits and a final grade of 8/10 or better; a minimum of 9/10 is however normally required for most of our courses.

Doctoral study

Applicants for a postgraduate doctoral course should hold:

  • the Magistras with 9/10; and
  • a Diploma de Master, DEA Diploma or Diploma de Studii Postuniversitare; and
  • the Diploma de Licenta/Diploma de Inginer.
Russia

Postgraduate taught study

To be considered for admission to a Master's e.g. MSc, MRes, MBA etc, applicants should have:

  • completed a minimum of five years study and been awarded the Specialist Diploma; or
  • have completed four years of study and been awarded the Bakalavr.

For both qualifications the requirement is an overall average grade of 4.5/5 or better.

Doctoral study

Applicants for a postgraduate doctoral course should hold a Magistr degree with a grade of 4/5 or better, although a grade of 4.5/5 is normally preferred.

Rwanda

Postgraduate taught study

Doctoral study

Entry to both levels of study

The following may be accepted for entry to a Master’s or postgraduate doctoral course:

  • an integrated Master’s with requisite grades
  • a BMS or Diplôme de Docteur en Médecine with requisite grades

Sri Lanka

Applicants for research should hold a first degree with a First or Upper Second Class Honours and should also be in possession of a Master's degree with 65% or better.

Sudan

To be considered for admission to a Master's e.g. MSc, MRes, MPH, MEd, MBA etc, applicants should have completed a five year Bachelor's (Hons) degree, at a mainstream university, awarded with a classification of 2:2 or better. Usually, a 2:1 will be required.

Applicants for Research should hold a Master's degree passed at 65% or better in addition to a Bachelor's degree passed at the prescribed level.

Sweden

To be considered for admission to a Master's e.g. MSc, MRes, MBA etc, applicants should have a post Bologna 1st cycle Bachelor’s degree with an overall average grade of 4 or better, or an average grade of C or better, depending on the marking scheme used by the university.

Applicants for research should be in possession of a Master's degree with an overall average grade of 4 or better, or an average grade of C or better, depending on the marking scheme used by the university, in addition to a 1st Cycle Bachelor's degree.

Applicants should contact the relevant Admissions team for advice if their degree has not been awarded with the above marking scheme, or has been awarded with a combination of marking schemes.

Switzerland

Post Bologna applicants should present a 1st Cycle Bachelor's degree (180 ECTS credits) with an overall mark of 4.5/6 or better or 7.5/10 or better.

Applicants for research should hold a Diplome/Diplome or Master's degree awarded with an overall mark  specified above in addition to a 1st Cycle Bachelor's degree.
