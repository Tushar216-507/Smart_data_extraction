The MRC MultiSci iCASE is a 3.5 year studentship and the financial package covers the following:

  • A tax-free maintenance award, currently £26,305 per annum,for 3 years of research, plus a further 6 months for the writing-up period.
  • Tuition fees atUKRI levelfor 3 years. The writing-up period does not incur tuition fees.
  • An enhanced research training support grant (RTSG) of £6,400 per annum for 3 years, paid to the supervisor for supporting the student’s training and research.
  • A conference fund of £300 per annum for 3 years to facilitate registration and attendance at conferences and scientific meetings.
  • Supplement fundsto support a wide range of research and training activities including internships, high cost training and outreach.
