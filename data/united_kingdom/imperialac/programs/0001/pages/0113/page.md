# Imperial Inspires scholarships

Our transformative new scholarships programme

Imperial Inspires is designed to support talented students from around the globe to access an Imperial education.

For 2027 entry, we’re offering at least 300 scholarships worth £15,000 per year for international students eligible to pay our Overseas rate of tuition. In the years ahead, we’ll be launching even more Imperial Inspires Scholarships for students from the UK.

“As a world-leading university, Imperial thrives on global collaboration. Through Imperial Inspires, we will welcome the brightest minds from every corner of the world to join our community in tackling the greatest challenges of our time. Wherever you come from, we know that the best answers emerge when we work together, because the most creative ecosystems are the most diverse, and the next generation of innovators should reflect the full breadth of global talent.”

Professor Peter Haynes, Imperial Provost and Deputy President

## Overview

Our sector-leading Imperial Inspires scholarships provide financial support for outstanding international applicants to undergraduate and selected postgraduate taught courses.

For students who apply for autumn 2027 entry, we are offering at least 300 Imperial Inspires scholarships across our four Faculties:

- Engineering
- Natural Sciences
- Medicine
- Imperial Business School

## About Imperial Inspires

Imperial Inspires is part of our mission as a world-leading and world-changing university to inspire the next generation of leaders and innovators in science, technology, engineering, medicine and business – no matter their background.

We’re proud to be a welcoming home for everyone who believes in the power of science to build, heal and lead. Imperial Inspires supports our ambition for continued global excellence and broadening access to education and opportunity.

## What you could receive

Successful applicants will be awarded £15,000 per year for each year of course, providing meaningful support towards your tuition fees and living costs.

## Are you eligible?

To be eligible for the scholarship for 2027 entry, you must be:

- An international student paying the Overseas rate of tuition
- Applying for a full-time undergraduate course, or selected full-time postgraduate taught course, which offers an Imperial Inspires scholarship in the Faculty of Engineering, the Faculty of Natural Sciences, the Faculty of Medicine or Imperial Business School.

## How to apply

- Step 1: Submit your Imperial undergraduate or postgraduate application through [UCAS](https://www.ucas.com/)or the[My Imperial website](https://myimperial.powerappsportals.com)after the application opens in September 2026.
- Step 2: If an Imperial Inspires scholarship is available for your course and you meet the minimum entry and eligibility requirements, you will be invited to complete an additional essay question from September 2026.

We will publish further detail about the participating departments, application and assessment process for Imperial Inspires scholarships on this page in due course. Check our website regularly for the latest information and updates.
