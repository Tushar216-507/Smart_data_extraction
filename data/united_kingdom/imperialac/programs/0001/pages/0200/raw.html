Careers Service

Support for you at every step of your career journey.

  • Book an appointment
  • Access online resources
  • Opening hours

Your future starts here!

Whether you're just starting to think about your career or are ready to take the next big step, we're here to help! From personalised advice and job-hunting resources to workshops and events, we offer everything you need to explore your options, develop your skills, and connect with employers. Wherever you are in your journey, we’ll guide you along the way. Let’s build your future together!

Get in touch

We are here to help! Please contact us with any questions regarding Imperial's Careers Service. Our Information and Enquiries team will respond with advice and help to make the most of our services to Imperial students, alumni, staff, recruiters & other organisations.

Our services

For students

We’re here to support you with personalised career advice and guidance. Take advantage of our online resources, events, and one-on-one appointments to help you succeed in your career journey.

For employers

We’re here to help you stand out at Imperial! We offer a variety of services to help employers connect with students, from participating in events to advertising job vacancies.

For alumni

A range of services for recent alumni (up to three years of graduating)

For staff

Support for Imperial staff working with students, and Imperial Postdocs.

Career guidance

Plan your career

There are some key steps to take to plan your career. Start this process early and you will save yourself time later!

Applications and interviews

We can help you to to develop your CV and improve your interview performance with a range of support.

What's On

Each term, we host and share hundreds of events designed to help you succeed in your career journey. Explore different industries, roles, and the support available to you—discover how you can get ahead!
