This information is for students starting their course in autumn 2026.

Overview

Our Presidential Scholarships for students of Black heritage provide a package of financial and mentoring support.

They're for Home students of Black heritage applying for undergraduate study in autumn 2026 in:

- Faculty of Engineering
- Faculty of Medicine
- Faculty of Natural Sciences
- Imperial Business School

## What the scholarship covers

Each scholarship covers:

- the full cost of tuition for the duration of your course (for up to four years, excluding repeat years)
- mentoring support

The scholarships are paid on top of any other financial support you may also be eligible for. This includes:

- The [Imperial Bursary](/study/fees-and-funding/undergraduate/bursaries-grants-scholarships/imperial-bursary/), offering support of up to £5,000/year, which you don’t need to pay back
- UK government [Maintenance loan](/study/fees-and-funding/undergraduate/loans/maintenance-loan/)for living costs
- Subject scholarships – see what is available for your subject of interest via our [scholarships search tool](/study/fees-and-funding/scholarships-search/)

## Check if you're eligible

One Presidential Scholarship is available in the Faculties of Engineering, Imperial Business School, Medicine and Natural Sciences for firm-choice offer holders of Black Heritage who:

- have [Home fee status](/study/fees-and-funding/tuition-fees/fee-status/)
- qualify for the College's [contextual admissions schemes](/study/apply/undergraduate/process/admissions-schemes/). These schemes are designed to make our admissions process fairer by considering each applicant's performance in context – taking into account factors such as your economic, social and educational background

To meet the eligibility criteria for a Presidential Scholarship for students of Black heritage, your ethnic heritage must match one of the following (or their equivalent in the respective application systems):

- Black – African
- Black – Caribbean
- Black – Other
- Mixed – White and Black African
- Mixed White and Black Caribbean
- Other Mixed background – including Black African, Black Caribbean or Other Black background

## How to apply

To apply for a Presidential Scholarship for students of Black heritage, you need to:

- Apply for one of our [2026 entry undergraduate](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/study/courses/)courses by:
  - 18.00 (UK local time) on 15 October 2025 if you’re applying for our MBBS Medicine course; or
  - by 18:00 (UK local time) on 14 January 2026 if you’re applying for any other courses. If you're unsure of how to apply, see our [step-by-step guide](/study/apply/undergraduate/process/).
- Receive an offer of a place for 2026 entry by the scholarship deadline of 23.59 (UK local time) on 1 April 2026.
- Firmly accept the offer by the relevant deadline. You must choose Imperial College London as your firm choice with UCAS to be eligible for this award.
- Complete our [Presidential Scholarships application form](https://imperial.eu.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_bsI4SbCocd3Ruh8)by the scholarship deadline of 23.59 (UK local time) 1 April 2026.

## When you'll hear

We will contact successful applicants by the 30 June 2026 to let them know they've been awarded a scholarship.
