Dedicated wellbeing support

Support for students who have an impairment or disability, specific learning difficulties or another condition

The Disability Advisory Service provides support for all students at Imperial. 

The Disability Advisory Service provides guidance for prospective students and offer holders about disclosing and how to request support and adjustments, advice about accommodation and the teams and networks there for you at Imperial and in your department.

There’s also information for parents and carers, including how to contact the Service.

Mental health support, advice and counselling

Imperial's Student Counselling and Mental Health Advice Serviceoffers individual and group counselling, workshops and courses, as well as access to SilverCloud, an online platform for self-help programmes. 

The Service can also provide advice for coping with feelings of anxiety, stress, loneliness, low mood, depression, grief and loss, or problems at home.

The Student Support Zone offers advice about mental health, including tips about how you can support your mental wellbeing.

Access to medical care

The Student Support Zone provides information on how to access a doctor, dentist or urgent healthcare at Imperial.
