Welcome to your offer holder hub

Learn about your next steps, including how to accept your offer and submit your offer conditions, discover more about postgraduate life at Imperial and explore the opportunities on offer. We hope to see you at Imperial soon!

Remember – if your offer is conditional, you'll need to provide evidence to show that you meet the conditions of your offer before we can confirm your place.

Any questions?

If you want to talk to someone about your application or offer, you can reach us by phone or email. Visit our contact page to find the details for the Admissions team for your course.

Information for international students

International student support

You'll be in good company as an international student at Imperial. To help you feel settled when you arrive, we can offer specialist immigration advice, webinars, trips and events.

Find out more

Applying for a visa

If you don’t have a full British passport, settled or pre-settled status in the UK, or hold another visa that allows you to study in the UK for your entire course, you’ll need to apply for a student visa.

Read our visa guide to find out how
