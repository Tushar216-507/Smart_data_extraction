The following summary helps graduate students understand their eligibility for various benefits and resources (e.g., child grants, dental insurance, etc.) in light of the MIT and MIT GSU collective bargaining agreement (CBA).

Specifically, the information below provides a high-level snapshot of benefit eligibility based upon appointment/funding status and degree type. Click on each appointment/funding status to view eligibility for benefits.

Note that, in some cases, benefit eligibility is subject to certain conditions and/or requires a particular action (e.g., enrolling or applying).

## RA/TA/IG (full or partial)

[MIT SHIP premium coverage](https://mit.myahpcare.com/): TEST Eligible, other than students in programs that do not receive the [summer tuition subsidy](https://oge.mit.edu/gpp/admissions-and-registration/registration-requirements/tuition-and-fees/), who typically do not receive premium coverage, per program practice.

[MIT Student Dental Insurance Plan (MIT SDIP)](https://mit.myahpcare.com/products) – student-only premium subsidy (83.8%): Eligible for 83.8% subsidy on student premium. Student pays the balance of the premium.

[MIT Student Vision Insurance Plan (MIT SVIP)](https://mit.myahpcare.com/products): Eligible. Student pays premium.

[Parking](https://web.mit.edu/facilities/transportation/parking/student/index.html): RA/TA/IG eligible in their first year for [Daily Student Parking](https://web.mit.edu/facilities/transportation/parking/student/index.html). After their first year of enrollment at MIT, RAs/TAs/IGs may participate in the [Employee Parking Program](https://web.mit.edu/facilities/transportation/parking/student/index.html) with a higher annual cap, but access to more lots. RAs/TAs/IGs living on campus are eligible for [Campus Resident Parking Program.](https://web.mit.edu/facilities/transportation/parking/residents.html)

[Transit subsidy](https://web.mit.edu/facilities/transportation/tpass/students.html): 70% subsidy when purchased in advance via Atlas or over-the-counter sales. Students receive 70% off at counter by showing “Benefits” screen in the Atlas app. Students who believe they are owed reimbursements from prior purchases should complete the [MBTA Pass Reimbursement Form](https://mit.co1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_cUdqmxcUK56M8iG).

Biking ([Bluebikes subsidy](https://web.mit.edu/facilities/transportation/pw/index.html)): Employees and students may buy subsidized annual Bluebikes memberships for $49 (regularly $129)

[Child Grant (need-blind)](https://oge.mit.edu/student-finances/financial-assistance-and-grants/mit-grant-for-graduate-students-with-children/): Full-time students in PhD programs and Master’s students in progams that receive the [summer tuition subsidy](https://oge.mit.edu/gpp/admissions-and-registration/registration-requirements/tuition-and-fees/) are eligible subject to application criteria.

[Supplemental Child Grant (need-based)](https://oge.mit.edu/student-finances/financial-assistance-and-grants/mit-grant-for-graduate-students-with-children/): Full-time students in PhD programs and Master’s students in programs that receive the [summer tuition subsidy](https://oge.mit.edu/gpp/admissions-and-registration/registration-requirements/tuition-and-fees/) may apply for the supplemental grant subject to application criteria.

International student grant ($1,200 awarded in first semester only): J1/F1 visa holders are eligible. Funds are issued automatically; no application necessary.

[Short-term emergency fund](https://oge.mit.edu/student-finances/financial-assistance-and-grants/graduate-student-short-term-emergency-fund/): Eligible subject to funding criteria. Note: Sloan Master’s students apply to Sloan Immediate Needs fund.

[Long-term doctoral hardship funding](https://oge.mit.edu/student-finances/financial-assistance-and-grants/doctoral-long-term-financial-hardship-funding/): PhD students who meet funding criteria (generally, F1 or J1 visa and spouse unable to work)

[Guaranteed Transitional Support](https://oge.mit.edu/student-finances/financial-assistance-and-grants/guaranteed-transitional-support/): One semester of advisor-independent funding available for PhD and SM/PhD in situations of unresolved conflict; transitional funding may be available for other cases as well.

[Graduate Assistance & Information Network (GAIN)](https://hr.mit.edu/worklife/gain): MIT provides GAIN for all graduate students and their household members for confidential consultations and personalized research and referrals for many life concerns.

[Backup Child Care](https://hr.mit.edu/worklife/backupchildcare): Eligible students can request up to 15 days of backup child care per fiscal year (July 1 – June 30).
