Applications for financial aid are processed by our office of [Student Financial Services](http://sfs.mit.edu). You can learn more about the process [on their site](https://sfs.mit.edu/undergraduate-students/apply-for-aid/deadlines-requirements/), but here’s a quick summary:

### Domestic students

The following documents help us paint a detailed picture of what kind of aid you’ll need to be able to attend MIT affordably.

#### Three steps to apply for aid.

- [CSS Profile](https://sfs.mit.edu/undergraduate-students/apply-for-aid/domestic/#CSSProfile): a tool provided by the College Board that we use to determine if you qualify for a need-based[MIT Scholarship](https://sfs.mit.edu/undergraduate-students/types-of-aid/mit-scholarship/)
- [FAFSA](https://sfs.mit.edu/undergraduate-students/apply-for-aid/domestic/#FAFSA): the form you need to fill out to see if you are eligible for any federal or state student aid.
- [Parental tax returns or income documentation](https://sfs.mit.edu/undergraduate-students/apply-for-aid/domestic/#taxincome): your parents’ tax returns or income documentation must be submitted through the College Board’s secure[IDOC](https://idoc.collegeboard.org/idoc/)platform

### International students

The following documents help us paint a detailed picture of what kind of aid you’ll need to be able to attend MIT affordably.

#### Two steps to apply for aid.

- [CSS Profile](https://sfs.mit.edu/undergraduate-students/apply-for-aid/international/#CSSProfile): a tool provided by the College Board that we use to determine if you qualify for a need-based[MIT Scholarship](https://sfs.mit.edu/undergraduate-students/types-of-aid/mit-scholarship/)
- [Parental tax returns or income documentation](https://sfs.mit.edu/undergraduate-students/apply-for-aid/international/#taxincome): your parents’ tax returns or income documentation must be submitted through the College Board’s secure[IDOC](https://idoc.collegeboard.org/idoc/)platform. If your parents live outside the U.S., please provide the tax return from that country, along with a translation to English if applicable.

### Aid deadlines

- Early Action applicants must submit their required documents by November 30 in order to receive a financial aid offer by mid-January.
- Regular Action applicants must submit their required documents by February 15 in order to receive a financial aid offer by mid-March.
