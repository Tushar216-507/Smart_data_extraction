For more information please visit our [Privacy Statement](https://oge.mit.edu/graduate-admissions/admissions-policies/privacy/) page.

### Jeanne Clery Act

The [Jeanne Clery Act](https://www.clerycenter.org/the-clery-act), a consumer protection law passed in 1990, requires all colleges and universities who receive federal funding to share information about crime on campus and their efforts to improve campus safety as well as inform the public of crime in or around campus.

Institutions must disseminate an annual security report by October 1 of each year. Please review the [MIT Annual Security and Fire Safety Report](https://police.mit.edu/annual-security-report) for further information or visit the [MIT Police](https://police.mit.edu/) website.
