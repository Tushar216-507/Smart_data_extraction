## General financial literacy resources

[Cash Course](http://www.cashcourse.org/) – Collection of online customizable financial tools provided by the National Endowment for Financial Education

[Finding a budgeting tool that works for you](https://www.nerdwallet.com/article/finance/nerdwallet-vs-mint-free-budgeting-apps) – There are many free budgeting apps or websites with different features based on your needs

[MyMoney.gov](http://www.mymoney.gov/Pages/default.aspx) – Government website that provides information, games, and fun facts about money, saving, and planning for the future

[Bankrate](http://www.bankrate.com/) – Debt management resource

[Jumpstart Coalition](http://www.jumpstart.org/) – National coalition of organizations dedicated to improving financial literacy for students of all ages

[360 Degrees of Financial Literacy (American Institute of CPAs)](http://www.360financialliteracy.org/Life-Stages/College-Students) – Free resources to help teach financial management

[Smart About Money](https://www.smartaboutmoney.org/) – Financial health and fitness site

[Mapping Your Future](http://mappingyourfuture.org/) – Student financial planning, specifically dealing with student loan and debt management

[Practical Money Skills](http://www.practicalmoneyskills.com/) – To help consumers and students of all ages learn the essentials of personal finance, Visa has partnered with leading consumer advocates, educators, and financial institutions to develop the Practical Money Skills program

[Kiplinger Magazine](http://www.kiplinger.com/) – Articles on personal finance, business, and investing advice from Kiplinger Magazine

[Hands On Banking](http://www.handsonbanking.org/en/) – Age-specific financial planning and literacy resources

[Debt Slapped](http://www.debtslapped.org/) – Debt Management

[You Can Deal With It](http://www.youcandealwithit.com/) – Student Financial Aid and Debt Management

[Federal Student Aid](https://nsldsfap.ed.gov/login) – National Student Loan data system

[Annual Credit Report](https://www.annualcreditreport.com/index.action) – Source for free credit reports authorized by the federal government

[my Fico](http://www.myfico.com/) – Online credit report monitoring (paid site)
