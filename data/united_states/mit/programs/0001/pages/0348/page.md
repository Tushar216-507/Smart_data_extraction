# How to Apply to BCS Programs

## Prospective Undergraduates

Interested in a major within the Department of Brain and Cognitive Sciences? Find out more about:

- [Course 9](/academic-program/course-9-brain-and-cognitive-sciences), the Brain and Cognitive Sciences major
- [Course 6-9](/academic-program/course-6-9-computation-and-cognition), the Computation and Cognition major

and then contact the [MIT Admissions Office](http://mitadmissions.org/) for more on how to apply for admission as an MIT undergraduate. Students in the Computation and Cognition major are also eligible for the [Course 6-9 Master of Engineering](/node/6029) program.

## Post-Baccalaureate Students

BCS is home to the [Research Scholars Program](/postbac1), a two-year funded non-degree post-baccalaureate program for outstanding recent college graduates from underrepresented minority groups, first-generation college students, individual students with disabilities, economically disadvantaged backgrounds, or veterans. More information can be found on the [Post-Baccalaureate Research Scholars](/postbac1) page.

## Prospective Graduate Students

Students interested in graduate study in the department should apply for the [Brain and Cognitive Sciences PhD Program](/academic-program/brain-and-cognitive-sciences-phd-program).

Students admitted to the BCS doctoral program also have access to more specialized studies in the [Molecular and Cellular Neuroscience](/academic-program/molecular-and-cellular-neuroscience-program) (MCN) Program and the BCS Program in [Computationally-Enabled Integrative Neuroscience](/academic-program/computationally-enabled-integrative-neuroscience-program) (CEIN)

## Postdocs

Candidates interested in postdoctoral research at BCS should contact the appropriate [faculty member](/faculty) directly or search for available positions via the [MIT Careers site](https://hr.mit.edu/careers). Visit the BCS Postdocs section for information on stipends and teaching opportunities.

International candidates should contact the BCS Human Resources Officer, [Kim DeMayo](/directory/kimberli-demayo), for information on immigration and visas.
