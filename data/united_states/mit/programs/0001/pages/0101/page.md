## International students contribute to the rich diversity of the MIT community.

Of the approximately 11,000 students at MIT, roughly 3,600 are international students, hailing from over 110 different countries.

### International Students Office (ISO)

The ISO assists international students in maintaining their legal status in the United States, provides support for their dependents, and promotes interaction with and integration into the MIT community at large.

Visit us in Building [E18-219](https://whereis.mit.edu/?go=E18) (50 Ames St) or at the [ISO website.](https://iso.mit.edu/)

Follow news, events, and other updates on the main page of the [ISO website](https://iso.mit.edu/). Find out U.S. cultural information and what to expect in Boston at our [Life at MIT](https://iso.mit.edu/life-at-mit/) website, especially our [Cultural Adjustment](https://iso.mit.edu/life-at-mit/cultural-adjustment/) and [Explore Boston & Cambridge](https://iso.mit.edu/life-at-mit/cambridge-and-boston/) sections.

Like the ISO on [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/ISOatMIT/)

Stay up-to-date with our ISO [newsletter](https://iso.mit.edu/about-iso/newsletters/)

### Health and help

MIT Health maintains an [internationals page](https://medical.mit.edu/my-mit/internationals) with information and services related to health, doctors, counseling, and advice.

You can also find information on [MIT’s mental health and counseling services](https://medical.mit.edu/services/mental-health-counseling), including virtual walk-in sessions, on MIT Health’s website.

### Connect with cultural student organizations

MIT students from all over the world have formed groups to connect with people from their country or culture. To look for groups, visit the Engage@MIT platform — a centralized database of organizations on campus — and select “Cultural” in the “Group Categories” field.
