# Applying to MIT

We oversee the application process for students applying for

[first-year](https://mitadmissions.org/apply/firstyear/first-year-eligibility/)and[transfer](https://mitadmissions.org/apply/transfer/transfer-eligibility/)undergraduate admissions. We try to explain our[process](https://mitadmissions.org/apply/process/selection/), help you understand what[we’re looking for](https://mitadmissions.org/apply/process/what-we-look-for/), and think about how to[prepare](https://mitadmissions.org/apply/prepare/be-yourself/)for MIT. Most importantly, we try to[encourage](https://mitadmissions.org/apply/prepare/be-yourself/)young people to be students and community members first, and applicants second.
