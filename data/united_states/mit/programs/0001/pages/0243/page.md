Check out the resources below to learn about food at MIT and the surrounding area:

- [MIT food map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/viewer?ll=42.360563087188545%2C-71.09648094149668&z=16&mid=1i0YNzpQzO2Zou13n9ZahvjL1L7feLFA5): This interactive map showcases area grocery stores, on-campus dining locations, and local favorite food spots.
- [Cooking with Antoinette](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1rQ0hjf1Zs8_6yBuSKKYdFXp8yx1KUFwRhR53gYRg2QQ/edit#slide=id.p): This cookbook was created by the International Student Office, and has over 50 easy-to-follow recipes.
- [Grocery shuttles](http://web.mit.edu/facilities/transportation/shuttles/grocery.html): See when MIT’s grocery shuttle runs to Costco, Trader Joe’s, Whole Foods, Market Basket and more.

As graduate student Elise N. notes in her article [Eating and Socializing on a Budget in Cambridge](https://oge.mit.edu/eating-and-socializing-on-a-budget-in-cambridge/), changing the way we budget around food can create new opportunities for socializing and saving. To learn more about budgeting, check out the [OGE budget page](https://oge.mit.edu/student-finances/financial-wellbeing/budgeting/).

The [Food Security Action Team (FSAT)](https://doingwell.mit.edu/fsat/) at MIT is continuously working to develop coordinated efforts around food security with the goal of creating student supports leading to a food-secure campus. If you would like to get involved with the work of FSAT, please email one of the members listed on the FSAT page.
