Contacts for media inquiry

Journalists seeking information about EECS, or interviews with EECS faculty members, should email eecs-communications@mit.edu

Please note that the EECS Communications Office only handles media inquiries related to MIT’s Department of Electrical Engineering & Computer Science. Please visit other school, department, laboratory, or center websites to locate their dedicated media-relations teams.

Contacts for recruitment

Companies and organizations interested in forming recruitment relationships with EECS students should visit the EECS Alliance to learn more.

Those interested in broad recruitment across the MIT community can connect with Career Advising & Professional Development (CAPD).

Finally, if you are interested in sharing job openings, paid internships, event listings, or job opportunities with the EECS undergraduate community, consider sharing with our listserv!
