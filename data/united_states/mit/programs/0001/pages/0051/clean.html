A petition is a request to make changes to your academic record that are either non-standard actions or are outside of stated deadlines.  Graduate student petitions are evaluated and approved by the graduate administrator or graduate officer in the student’s home department, the instructor of a subject (if appropriate for the particular request), and the OGE. The Office of Graduate Education acts with authority on behalf of the Committee on Graduate Programs with regard to all petition requests.

Please note that it may take 7 to 10 business days to review and process a petition once received by OGE. To check on the status of a petition that has already been submitted, please log into DocuSign.

Add/Drop/Change petitions

Submit an online Add/Drop/Change petition to make a late Add, Drop, or Change of Grading Status in their academic record after a deadline has passed. A processing fee of $25 will be charged to your student account for each petition approved.

For cross-registration at Harvard

In order to be eligible to take classes at Harvard, you must meet MIT’s cross registration deadline. The cross registration deadline is the same as the Add Date deadline.

Please note, each of Harvard’s colleges have their own Registrar’s Offices and deadlines and make note of these deadlines. If you miss the cross registration deadline, please reach out to oge-petitions@mit.edu if you are still interested in taking a Harvard class.

If you are an MIT student and the deadline has passed for adding a Harvard class, you must first receive approval from the Harvard Instructor and the appropriate Harvard Registrar’s office before submitting a late add/drop petition. Each Harvard school has its own Registrar’s Office, but this process is the same for each school. A list of each of the Harvard’s Registrar’s Offices can be found here.

If you are an MIT student submitting a late drop petition for a Harvard subject, please use the General Petition Form in DocuSign (see below).

Joint Master Degree Thesis petition

Students who wish to embark on a joint master’s thesis project should consult first with their individual advisors, and also with the graduate officer or the chair of the graduate committee of the department in which they are registered. More information about Joint Master degree thesis, and the petition form, can be found online.

Special changes to student status

Submit a special petition to oge-petitions@mit.edu to change your student status:

Do NOT use a petition to:

  • Request delayed thesis publication (a “thesis hold”). Instead, follow these instructions for a thesis hold.
  • Request a Childbirth Accommodation (CBA) or Parental Accommodation (PA). Instead, follow these instructions for CBA or PA.
  • Apply for a waiver of summer tuition for a subject linked to an unpaid internship; please contact the Registrar’s office with this request: registrar-www@mit.edu
  • Request a Medical Leave. Instead read this webpage for information.
