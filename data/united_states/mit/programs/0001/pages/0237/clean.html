Nominate a faculty member

Nominations for the 2025-27 round of Committed to Caring (C2C) faculty are now closed. Thank you for your interest in honoring excellence in mentoring, and stay tuned later this year to learn more about the next group of C2C faculty.

Criteria for selection for a “Committed to Caring” honor include:

Impact.

  • Who has the nominee reached with their caring actions? How deep? How long has the nominee demonstrated these actions?

Reach.

  • Does the nominee have a leadership role that has an impact on many?
  • Demonstrated by multiple nominations?
  • Does the nominee demonstrate a commitment to influencing/changing systems

Excellence in scholarship.

  • Is the nominee committed to the academic and professional development of their students?

Contributions to graduate student belonging:

  • Champion revisions to policies and processes with an eye toward supporting equity
  • Support of and participation in efforts which support inclusion and belonging
  • Hosting summer interns or participating in programs that expand access to graduate education

Professional development as an advisor.

  • Take initiative to do self-study/professional development around various aspects of mentoring and advising, including management, communication skills, support of students with varied needs and backgrounds
  • Seek feedback and incorporates it into mentoring approach

Examples of caring actions may include:

  • Checking in with students about their well being
  • Taking an interest in the student beyond research
  • Sharing their own struggles and/or path to MIT, enabling students to relate
  • Being generous with time
  • Participating in or helping to coordinate student-run events (appearing on panels, giving talks, etc.)
  • Creating a supportive working environment for a diverse lab group
  • Providing mentorship on professional and/or personal goals
  • Working toward an end goal that will improve many lives
