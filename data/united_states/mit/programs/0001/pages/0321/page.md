# EECS Career Fair

Hosted by the EECS Alliance, the MIT EECS Career Fair is your direct connection to one of the most sought-after talent pipelines in engineering and computer science. Meet undergraduates, master’s students, and PhD candidates actively pursuing internships and full-time roles across software, hardware, AI, and electrical engineering.

All Course 6 students and those with EECS department appointments are encouraged to attend. Questions? Contact us at [eecs-alliance@mit.edu](mailto:eecs-alliance@mit.edu).
