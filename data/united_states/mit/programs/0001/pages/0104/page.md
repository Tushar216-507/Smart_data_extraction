#### General questions

#### Graduate Admissions

👉 For questions about general [admissions](https://oge.mit.edu/graduate-admissions/) information, application requirements, deadlines, or application fee waivers.

❗ Questions about application status or a specific graduate program (e.g. program curriculum, application requirements, eligibility, or financial support) should be emailed directly to [the department/program](https://oge.mit.edu/graduate-admissions/programs/).

#### GradSupport

[GradSupport@mit.edu](mailto:GradSupport@mit.edu)
👉 For questions or making an appointment with [GradSupport](https://oge.mit.edu/student-support-development/gradsupport/) regarding issues that impact your life as a current graduate student, including faculty/student relationships, changing your advisor, conflict negotiation, and excused absences.

#### Petitions

👉 For questions regarding [petitions](https://oge.mit.edu/gpp/faculty-oversight/petitions/) or to submit certain petitions.

#### GradDiversity

👉 For questions or support about [grad diversity initiatives](https://oge.mit.edu/community-diversity/graddiversity/), including the MIT Summer Research Program, Grad Catalyst, group visits to MIT’s campus, or [recruitment events](https://oge.mit.edu/news-and-events/events/).

#### GradFamilies

👉 For questions or support for [grad students with children](https://oge.mit.edu/families/), including resources on campus and beyond, grants, childcare, and events.

#### Fellowships

#### OGE Communications
