The following resources may help you save money. Each one notes whether the discounts are available to the MIT community broadly, to MIT students, or to students in general. We welcome comments or additional resources at [oge-comms@mit.edu](mailto:oge-comms@mit.edu).

## Transportation

[Airport stuttles](https://web.mit.edu/facilities/transportation/shuttles/airport.html). $15 fee; available by reservation around Thanksgiving, Winter, and Spring breaks.

[Amtrak](https://www.amtrak.com/student-discounts). Students 17-24 receive 15% off Amtrak’s regular adult fare for bookings made one day in advance.

Bike upkeep. There are [eight bicycle fix-it stations](https://web.mit.edu/facilities/transportation/pw/faq.html#fix) on campus equipped with an air pump and basic hand tools. You can also get help at the [MIT Bike Lab](https://engage.mit.edu/tbl/home/).

[Bluebikes bicycle rentals/bike share](https://sites.mit.edu/vpcssforms/bluebikes/). Students are eligible for a 50% discount.

Car rental. As a member of the MIT community, you may receive discount pricing on car rentals. Visit the [Procurement site](https://procurement.mit.edu/about/discounts-mit-community) and scroll down to “Car Rental.” [Community Pedal Power E-bike lending library](https://cpp.ebikelibrary.org/) is a small collection of E-Bikes that can be lent to Cambridge community members for up to a week at a time at zero cost.

[E-Bike voucher program](https://ma-ebikes.masscec.com/)provides vouchers to low-and-moderate income residents across Massachusetts to assist in the purchase of an E-Bike.

[Grocery shuttles](https://web.mit.edu/facilities/transportation/shuttles/grocery.html). Free shuttles will take you to Brothers Market, Market Basket, Star Market, Trader Joe’s, or Whole Foods, with several travel options during the week. Shuttles also make it easier to shop at Aldi, Cosco, and Target.

[MBTA/public transit pass](https://web.mit.edu/facilities/transportation/tpass/students.html). MIT subsidizes 50-70% of the cost of MBTA passes for bus, subway, commuter rail, and/or commuter boat for its students.

Zipcar can be accessed by MIT community members at a [discounted annual fee](https://web.mit.edu/facilities/transportation/ridesharing.html).

[Central Rock Climbing gym](https://mitac.mit.edu/events/central-rock-gym-mitoc-perk). Members of the MIT Outdoor Club (MITOC) can get a discounted membership at the Central Rock Gym!

Meditation app. The [Headspace meditation app](https://www.headspace.com/studentplan?utm_source=google&utm_medium=search&utm_campaign=HS_Headspace_Brand-Student-Plan-Exact_Search_US-NorAm_Google_NA&utm_content=&utm_term=headspace%20student%20discount&gad_source=1&gclid=Cj0KCQiAnfmsBhDfARIsAM7MKi3-6FGHZmoyi_tIIqVE9u467X1ASIO2p5Pb2tZ3VDmzsmgeVms7NnAaArvoEALw_wcB) student rate remains about $10 per year.

## Other

[Backup child care](https://hr.mit.edu/worklife/child-care/backup). MIT graduate students can use short-term backup child care for 15 days per fiscal year when regular child care or school arrangements for their child are disrupted.

[MIT community discounts](https://procurement.mit.edu/about/discounts-mit-community). As a member of the MIT community, you m ay make personal purchases with several suppliers that offer discount pricing to MIT, including Cambridge/Boston hotels, national hotel chains, computers and IT peripherals, office supplies, and cell phones and service.

[MIT Graduate Assistance & Information Network (GAIN)](https://www.mitgain.com/). Available 24/7, providing confidential support for personalized services and referrals on legal, work/career, parenting, financial, home/daily living, and wellness. GAIN can research government and community resources for you, including utility subsidies & food pantries, and assist with applications. Contact 844-MIT-GAIN (844-648-4246).

## Enrichment

[4-D Theater at the Museum of Science](https://mitac.mit.edu/events/4-d-theater-museum-science). MIT undergraduate and graduate students can access the 4-D theater at the Museum of Science at just $4 a ticket. In this theater, what’s on the screen is only half the fun. With effects you can feel (and even smell!) this is one giant leap beyond 3-D. After all, why should your eyes and ears have all the fun? [View current showings](https://www.mos.org/visit/4-D).

[Boston Children’s Museum](https://mitac.mit.edu/events/boston-childrens-museum). All visitors can visit the Boston Children’s Museum on Sundays 1-4pm for $1/ticket. The MIT community can purchase tickets via MITAC for any day for just $9 a ticket! Please note: After receiving your MITAC ticket, reservations are recommended through [BCM’s Ticketing page](https://estore.bostonchildrensmuseum.org/webstore/shop/viewItems.aspx?cg=TT&c=DTT2). Students with EBT cards or WIC can [purchase tickets online](https://estore.bostonchildrensmuseum.org/webstore/shop/viewItems.aspx?cg=TT&c=DTT2) for $3.

[Boston University Judson B. Coit Observatory](https://www.bu.edu/astronomy/community/open-night-observatory/). BU Coit Observatory Public Open Nights offer free admission for everyone with advance registration most Wednesday evenings.

[Cambridge Public Libraries](https://cambridgepl.libcal.com/calendar/publicevents?cid=7052&t=d&d=0000-00-00&cal=7052&inc=0) provides free events and classes for children, teens, and adults.

[EBT Card to Culture](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/ebt-card-to-culture-organizations) allows individuals on SNAP to access free and discounted admissions to many of the Commonwealth’s museums and cultural institutions.

[Harvard Museum of Natural History](https://mitac.mit.edu/events/harvard-museum-natural-history). Members of the MIT community can visit all four [Harvard Museums of Science and Culture](https://hmsc.harvard.edu/visit/) at a discounted rate of $8. Guests under 3 are free, and admission is also free to Massachusetts residents every Sunday morning from 9:00am to 12:00pm and on Wednesdays from 3:00pm to 5:00pm year-round. Proof of residency required.

[Institute of Contemporary Art](https://mitac.mit.edu/events/institute-contemporary-art) provides free admission for MIT students, faculty, and staff, and those under 18. [Reserve your timed entry ticket through the MITAC site](https://mitac.mit.edu/events/institute-contemporary-art). The public can also enter the ICA free of charge every Thursday evening from 5 pm to 9 pm by [reserving tickets in advance](https://www.icaboston.org/events/ica-free-thursday-night/?gad_source=1&gad_campaignid=17326898778&gbraid=0AAAAADhRLBEReetBo63oGiGCUTI4RSURU&gclid=Cj0KCQjwvJHIBhCgARIsAEQnWlDZ3rW7YkQC_vAuTB2saFJyeWruIQTdu1pCuleH7j9oN-7RVyDmHb8aAvy5EALw_wcB).

[Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum](https://mitac.mit.edu/events/isabella-stewart-gardner-museum). The [Council for the Arts](https://arts.mit.edu/camit/discount-tickets/) supports free admission for students, faculty, and staff to the [Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum](https://www.gardnermuseum.org/). Use the promo code “MIT” to make a free advanced reservation [online](https://tnew.gardnermuseum.org/events)– be sure to bring your MIT ID with you. Guests of MIT students, faculty, and staff will need their own tickets.

[MIT Museum](https://mitac.mit.edu/events/mit-museum). Free for MIT Students, faculty, staff, alum with ID + one guest! Additional non-MIT guests under the age of 19 or with a student ID can enter for just $10.

[New England Aquarium](https://mitac.mit.edu/events/new-england-aquarium). MIT students can reserve aquarium tickets at a discount using MITAC. Use your MITAC codes to make your reservation ahead of time. Make your reservation [online](https://tickets.neaq.org/Redeem.aspx) or via phone (617-973-5206). Children under 2 enter for free.

[MITAC](https://mitac.mit.edu/) offers savings on a variety of other museums, performances, tours, and more around the Boston area. [MIT Open Space events calendar](https://www.openspace.mit.edu/calendar) is a great spot to find free events, including fitness classes, networking events, clothing swaps, and movie nights.

[Museum of Fine Arts](https://www.mfa.org/membership/universities). The MFA’s University Membership Program offers free ticketed admission to MIT undergraduate and graduate students with valid ID. Organizational members can obtain same-day general admission and special exhibition tickets by visiting a ticket desk and presenting a valid student ID on the day of their visit. Want to bring kids along? Children under 6 get in for free, and youth 7 to 17 years old enjoy a discounted $14 admission.
