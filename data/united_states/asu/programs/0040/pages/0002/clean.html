Related Discipline

All students must complete 12 hours of approved Related Discipline courses. A Related Discipline is an are outside of sustainability that augments the student's sustainability education. Students must see their advisor for approval and to have courses added to their degree audit. Courses for the Related Discipline may not overlap with other major requirements. Students may use courses from a minor or certificate to satisfy the Related Discipline requirement.
