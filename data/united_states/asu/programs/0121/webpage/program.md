# Online Master of Science in Business Analytics

Are you a data guru looking to hone your quantitative skills and apply them to a professional business setting? If so, pursuing Arizona State University’s Master of Science in business analytics (MSBA) online may be an excellent choice. You’ll develop advanced skills in synthesizing large quantities of data at rest and using it to tell a story. Through quantitative-driven courses, you’ll build the technical skills needed to pursue diverse roles in the business analytics industry.

## Online business analytics courses

In this program, you’ll take courses that build core competencies in math, statistics and business. Topics range from using data-driven quality management and marketing analytics to data mining and enterprise analytics. The program culminates in an applied project, where you’ll use data provided by real organizations to help solve problems.

## What you can do with a master’s in business analytics

Because big data is an essential part of today’s global economy, business analytics is a fast-growing and lucrative field. As a graduate with an MSBA, you’ll find demand for analytical skills in marketing, finance, supply chain, information management and other areas of business.
