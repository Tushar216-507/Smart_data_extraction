# Online Bachelor of Arts in General Studies

Maximize the credits you’ve already earned and explore new possibilities with Arizona State University’s online Bachelor of Arts in general studies. One of ASU’s most transfer-friendly degrees, this program allows you to tailor your coursework to your goals. Whether you’re just getting started or returning to finish your degree, the curriculum adapts to your progress and future plans.

## The variety of jobs a general studies degree prepares you for

This degree can increase earning potential and open doors to upward mobility across industries. You’ll graduate prepared for roles that require a bachelor’s degree but not a specialized credential. These may include administrative positions in business, health care, nonprofits and education. You’ll also be well-positioned for graduate study in communication, health innovation, leadership, public administration and more.
