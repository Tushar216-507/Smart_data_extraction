Look around, and you'll see religion everywhere. By studying a variety of materials, you'll gain an understanding of the role of religion in shaping the past, present and future of human societies and cultures --- an understanding that will aid you in any field you decide to pursue.

In the Bachelor of Arts program in religious studies with a concentration in religion, culture and public life, you approach religion as a mode of human culture, as constituted in intellect, language, emotion, practice, art and everyday life.

The program offers you a focus on the role religions play in the formation of cultural identities, traditions, norms and memories. You examine, in particular, the impact that religions have on culturally specified ways that people reason, communicate, socialize, work, entertain, dress, eat, live and die. Special consideration is given to the influence of religion on gender roles and sexual norms; the formation of images of the body and the moral assessment of medical practices; and the reflection and reproduction of religion in film, popular culture and conventional, digital and social media.
