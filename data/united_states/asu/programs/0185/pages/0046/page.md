# Guarantee your transfer with MyPath2ASU®

## What are the steps to sign up with MyPath2ASU®?

- Step 1: Select your preferred way to study and current institution.
- Step 2: Choose the degree program you're most interested in. Don’t worry, you can still change your mind later.
- Step 3: Fill in your personal information and submit the form. Once submitted, create your Transfer Guide account and access your MyPath2ASU® transfer map.

## What is the MyPath2ASU® transfer process?

Although MyPath2ASU® is not required to transfer to ASU, it can make your transfer journey simpler and cost-effective by aligning your coursework with your intended ASU major. The earlier you sign up, the better. If you’re starting at a community college, MyPath2ASU® will guide you through your course planning starting with your first term at the community college. When you’re ready to apply to ASU, you’ll be able to transfer with confidence knowing exactly how your credits will transfer to ASU and your major.

## What are my next steps to applying to ASU?

If you have questions about the transfer admission process, requirements or transcripts, contact your [transfer coordinator](/contact/undergraduate) and they will be happy to help.

Or if you’re ready to apply to ASU, you can begin your [application for admission](https://webapp4.asu.edu/uga_admissionsapp/) up to a year before you are ready to transfer.

General university admission is guaranteed upon successful completion of the following conditions:

- Completion of an associate degree from a regionally accredited higher education institution with a minimum 2.00 cumulative GPA or
- completion of 24 or more transferable semester credits post-high school with a minimum cumulative transfer GPA of 2.50 (4.00=“A”) and verification of high school graduation or the equivalent.

Important notes:

- Some ASU colleges and schools have additional or higher requirements for admission into their majors. Visit [degree search](http://asu.edu/degrees)to find the requirements for your major.
- Students will also need to submit an Arizona State University admission application, including an application fee and other required documents, such as official college/university transcripts.
- MyPath2ASU® pathway programs are based on a minimum of 24 transfer credit hours; students with fewer than 24 credit transfer credit hours will also need to meet [first-year admission requirements](/apply/first-year/admission#webspark-anchor-link--69).
- International students will need to meet [international admission requirements](/apply/international/transfer).

If you signed a TAG agreement prior to summer 2017, find your [degree program outline](https://webapp4.asu.edu/transfercreditguide/app/home).

To view MyPath2ASU® pathways for the Maricopa Community Colleges from prior years, visit the [archive search tool](/transfer/maricopa-mapp-archive).
