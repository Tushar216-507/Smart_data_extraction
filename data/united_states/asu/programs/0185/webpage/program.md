Set yourself apart by earning the nation's first degree in military and veteran studies --- offered by an institution ranked No. 3 by U.S. News & World Report (2025) for best online bachelor's degree programs for veterans.

The Bachelor of Arts program in applied military and veteran studies is the nation's first interdisciplinary program that provides you with a comprehensive understanding of the military's role in society, and the experience and culture of veterans. The curriculum covers various aspects of military studies, including the historical and societal impacts of conflict and the personal experiences of veterans in media, literature, employment and leadership. You obtain transferable skills in leadership, critical thinking, resiliency, adaptability and transparent communication.

This career-connected program gives you a deeper understanding of military service and veterans, whether you are a historian, museum specialist, nonprofit or government coordinator, counselor, health care provider and U.S. Department of Defense contractor.

The study of applied military and veterans studies transcends geographic boundaries, spanning cities, states, provinces, countries and even continents. [Global education programs](https://goglobal.asu.edu/) help students dive deeper into their studies by enabling them to experience many cultures firsthand and to explore places where history and military action have intermingled. Through these encounters, students can discover the interconnections of ideas and places at primary source sites.

Graduates are prepared for roles that support public policy development and political activism, roles in the military, and leadership positions in military- and veteran-serving organizations.

Graduates are well-positioned for employment in:

- federal agencies across the U.S. and worldwide, such as with the DoD and the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs
- the government sector in Arizona and other states
- health care-related organizations that serve veteran populations
- nonprofit organizations around the U.S. that are dedicated to supporting military-connected individuals and veterans
