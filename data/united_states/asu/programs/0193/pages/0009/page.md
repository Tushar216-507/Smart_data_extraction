## International first-year student next steps

### Activate your ASURITE User ID

Your ASURITE User ID is the official login name you’ll use to access most ASU systems and services. You’ll get your ASURITE User ID and activation instructions shortly after you submit your application. A message from [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection) with the subject line "ASU Account Activation" will be sent to the email address you provided in your application.

If you can't remember you ASURITE User ID you can look it up at: [asu.edu/lostpassword](https://asu.edu/lostpassword)

[Activate ASURITE](https://weblogin.asu.edu/password/activation?_ga=2.64137455.1123871021.1635118501-788536687.1634233354)

You’ll log in to [My ASU](https://my.asu.edu/) with your ASURITE User ID and password to:

- View your priority tasks and next steps for enrollment
- View your advisor contact information
- View financial aid and billing information
- Connect to campus technology and services

How to log in to My ASU:

- Navigate to [my.asu.edu](https://my.asu.edu/).
- Enter your ASURITE User ID.
- Enter your password. (Forgot your password? Visit [asu.edu/lostpassword](https://asu.edu/lostpassword).)
- Click the “Sign In” button to access your account.

After you enroll in classes, visit [asu.edu/emailsignup](https://asu.edu/emailsignup) to activate your official @asu.edu email address. Once activated, you will access and check your official ASU email through your ASU Gmail account.

Be sure to check both your non-ASU personal email address and your ASU Gmail inbox regularly.

Remember to save your admit letter, which is posted to My ASU, so you have it for travel.

[Visit My ASU](https://my.asu.edu)

### Request your I-20 and apply for your visa

International students who wish to pursue their studies at ASU must obtain an F-1 or J-1 visa. To obtain a visa, students must first receive a SEVIS number and I-20 document from ASU.

ASU guides each student through the process of obtaining their I-20 using the I-20 Tracker in My ASU.

[More about I-20](/apply/international/student-visa)

### Give your parents or family access to your records and account

Through My ASU Parent Guest Access, you can share your academic records with your parent or guardian and give them access to pay your tuition and bills. Go to My ASU, select the Profile tab at the top and find “Add a Guest” under My ASU Guest Access Permissions.

[Visit My ASU](https://my.asu.edu)

### Pay your enrollment deposit (on-campus students only)

Pay your [enrollment deposit](/deposit) through [My ASU](https://my.asu.edu) to confirm you will attend. For fall 2026 admits, the enrollment deposit will be available beginning Oct. 1, 2025.

[Pay deposit](/deposit)

### Complete the math placement test

All first-year students are required to take the math placement test at least seven calendar days before their Academic Orientation Session. You will see the item “Take Math Placement Test” under Priority Tasks in [My ASU](https://my.asu.edu), and you will also see this task in ASU’s New Student Orientation Experience step 2, Academic Orientation.

### Submit your measles, mumps and rubella immunization record (on-campus students only)

All students born after Jan. 1, 1957, are required to meet the following [MMR immunization requirement](https://eoss.asu.edu/health/parents/immunization) before classes begin. You can upload required documentation in your ASU [My Health Portal](https://asuportal.pointnclick.com/login_dualauthentication.aspx).

The MMR vaccine is available for a fee at any of [ASU's Health Services](https://eoss.asu.edu/health/contact) locations.

[My Health Portal](https://asuportal.pointnclick.com/login_dualauthentication.aspx)

### Confirm housing and dining options

All ASU first-year students live in the residential college of their academic major. Barrett students live in the Barrett residential community for their first two years. The housing portal for fall 2026 admits opened in early January 2026.

Once the housing portal opens and you've paid your enrollment deposit, you can select your housing and dining preferences. The priority housing deadline is May 15. We cannot guarantee your housing preference if you apply after the priority deadline. Visit [ASU’s housing portal](https://asu.starrezhousing.com/StarRezPortalX/EF39AF6A/14/25/Home-Home?UrlToken=0888FD2E).

[Select housing](https://housing.asu.edu/)

[Learn about housing options](/campus-life/housing-dining).

If you are a nonresident or international student, your housing plans must be confirmed before you can register for an Academic Orientation Session.

### ASU’s New Student Orientation Experience

Through ASU’s New Student Experience, accessed online in My ASU, you will prepare to take classes in the fall by completing essential next steps for enrollment.

- Log in to [My ASU](https://my.asu.edu) to view details.
- Register and complete an Academic Orientation Session, where you’ll meet with your academic college and finalize your class schedule.

All students must complete the New Student Orientation Experience in order to enroll in classes.

[New Student Experience](https://eoss.asu.edu/orientation/new-student)

### Health insurance

Health insurance is mandatory for all registered international students and a condition of enrollment, and requires that international students have adequate medical and accident insurance coverage. Mandatory student health insurance is automatically charged for all international students.

[Insurance info](https://eoss.asu.edu/health/billing-insurance/international-students)

### Learn about tuition and fees

Tuition charges will be billed to your student account and may be viewed online through My ASU. By registering for classes, you agree to pay all applicable tuition and registration fees. If you decide not to attend, you must completely withdraw from all classes before the semester begins.

If tuition is not paid by the applicable due date, you will automatically be enrolled in the tuition installment plan and charged a nonrefundable enrollment fee.

ASU has partnered with [Flywire](https://students.asu.edu/flywire) to offer a streamlined and cost-effective payment method for you to send international payments. Flywire offers favorable exchange rates and a secure portal for processing your payment to ASU. Create a Flywire login to make payments and review your payment activity and history.

Visit the [Tuition and Billing](https://students.asu.edu/tuitionandbilling) website for more information.

[Tuition and Billing](https://students.asu.edu/tuitionandbilling)
