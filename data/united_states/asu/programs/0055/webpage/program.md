# Online Bachelor of Arts in Business – Tourism

Arizona State University’s Bachelor of Arts in business with a concentration in tourism combines foundational business abilities, such as management and marketing, with specific industry knowledge to support your growth in the field. In this program, you’ll gain an understanding of tourism marketing techniques and learn how to interact with many different types of businesses effectively. Upon graduation, you’ll have the skills and experience needed to pursue careers in travel, recreation and other related industries.
