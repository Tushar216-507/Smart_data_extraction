# Online Master of Leadership and Management

Arizona State University’s online Master of Leadership and Management prepares leaders to take on additional managerial responsibilities in global organizations or to make a career change across industries. In this interdisciplinary program, you’ll gain skills in cross-cultural communication, finance, data analytics and more. Upon graduation, you’ll be prepared for a career within many diverse and dynamic business environments.

## Online leadership and management courses

Made up of eight core courses, an applied learning project and a capstone, the curriculum explores topics of interest and the fundamentals of leadership. This program also offers a specialization if you're interested in AI, analytics and fintech innovations. You’ll complete three courses to obtain this specialization:

- AI, Chip Strategy and the Future of Work
- Analytics and Machine Learning in Global Business
- Navigating Global Fintech Innovations
