Online business data analytics courses

Courses in this program emphasize the skills needed to transform raw data into actionable insights efficiently and effectively, including business processes, data management, project management and solution development. As you progress through the program, you’ll also build various general business skills, such as critical thinking and effective verbal and written communication.
