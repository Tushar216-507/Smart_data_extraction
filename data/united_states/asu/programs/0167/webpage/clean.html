Online Master of Science in Technical Communication

The field of technical communication meets at the intersection of writing, design and technology. Arizona State University's online Master of Science in technical communication prepares working professionals to move into senior roles in content strategy, information design and communication leadership. You'll build the technical writing, visual design and research skills to turn complex information into clear, usable content. The curriculum is project-based and designed to fit around your career.

Technical communication graduate courses and culminating experience

During this online master's in technical communication, you'll move through a curriculum that covers the full scope of the field. Coursework spans applied rhetoric, research methods, visual communication, data and information visualization, technical editing, project management and user experience. You'll finish the program with a culminating capstone experience that results in a professional portfolio for future employers.

Technical communication careers, roles and salaries

Graduates of this online program move into content strategy and management, user experience writing and research, information design, technical editing and communication leadership roles across technology, health care, government and financial services. Technical writers, also called technical communicators, earned a median annual wage of $91,670 in May 2024, with about 4,500 openings projected each year through 2034, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. How to apply to the technical communication master's program

Applicants to the Master of Science in technical communication program must fulfill the requirements of both the Graduate College and the College of Integrative Sciences and Arts.
