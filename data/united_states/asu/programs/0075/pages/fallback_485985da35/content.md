## About CrazyGames

CrazyGames is a free browser gaming platform founded in 2014 by Raf Mertens. Since then, the platform has grown to over 60 million monthly users.

Our mission is simple: to create a browser-gaming platform that works seamlessly for users around the world and rewards developers both big and small. Visit our corporate site and learn more [about CrazyGames](https://about.crazygames.com/).
