What will I learn in an executive fire administration master’s program concentration?

Executive fire administration degree concentration courses

As part of this master’s program, you’ll take courses covering a variety of topics, from organization behavior to public policy and finance. You’ll also complete courses within the executive fire administration concentration. This program culminates with a capstone course, in which you’ll complete an applied project of your choosing.

What can I do with a fire administration degree concentration?

Graduates with a master’s in public safety leadership and administration with a concentration in executive fire administration degree are well-equipped to drive innovation from the upper levels of fire service in federal, state and local fire departments and emergency management agencies.
