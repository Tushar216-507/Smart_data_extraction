Online Master of Public Health – Public Health Technology

Arizona State University’s online Master of Public Health in public health technology prepares you with the skills needed to support healthy communities by providing a foundational understanding of public health best practices and new technology. You’ll learn to assess and address health challenges using evidence-based strategies and technology-informed tools. This program will also show you how to make lasting improvements in population health, whether your goal is community-driven impact or systems-level transformation.

What can you do with a public health master’s degree?

Online master of public health courses

In this online program, you’ll take a mix of courses that teach you how to evaluate and address public health challenges using data, policy and evidence-based practice. The role of technology in solving problems at scale plays a prominent role in this program’s curriculum, teaching you how to improve health access and outcomes at scale.

Public health careers you can pursue

Through transdisciplinary training in public health science, ethics, communication and policy, you’ll graduate ready to lead in government agencies, health systems, nonprofits and global health organizations. Your expertise in the technology and public health domains will give you the qualifications for a wide range of roles. Learn from public health experts

The School of Technology for Public Health is the first of its kind, bridging the disconnect between public health and other historically siloed disciplines to create impact at scale. Faculty expose students to a constellation of tools and technologies that teach them how to solve problems in the real world. Through placements with community and cross-sector partners, students explore how existing technologies can be deployed to improve public health systems, outcomes and processes.
