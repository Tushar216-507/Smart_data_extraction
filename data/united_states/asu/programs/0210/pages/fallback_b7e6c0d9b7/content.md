Kazuto Okawa, known professionally as LLLL (pronounced "four-el"), [is a Japanese musician.](#cite_note-pop-1)[The Japan Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Japan_Times) described his music as "sparkly, [J-pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J-pop)-inflected brand of [shoegaze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shoegaze)", [and](#cite_note-jp-2021-5) [PopMatters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PopMatters) described it as "post-[electropop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electropop)". [He released his first album in 2012.](#cite_note-pop-1) [He released his second album Faithful in 2015 through Progressive Form.](#cite_note-jp-2021-5) [That same year he released a remix of](#cite_note-6) [Meishi Smile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meishi_Smile)'s "Pastel".
