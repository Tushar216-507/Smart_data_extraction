## 📱 Best Emergency Apps in India

With the growing use of smartphones and the increasing need for personal safety, especially in emergency situations, several mobile applications have been launched by the Indian government and private developers. These apps offer real-time support, location tracking, and easy access to emergency services at your fingertips.

Below are some of the most reliable emergency apps in India, available on Android and iOS platforms:

### 1. 112 India App – India’s All-in-One Emergency App

Developer: Ministry of Home Affairs (Government of India)
Available on: Android and iOS
Purpose: National Emergency Response Support System (NERSS)

Key Features:

- SOS/Panic Button: Users can press a button or shake the phone to send an emergency alert.
- Live Location Sharing: Shares your real-time GPS location with emergency responders or pre-registered emergency contacts.
- Supports Multiple Emergencies: Whether it’s crime, fire, medical help, or women’s safety, the app connects users directly to the nearest emergency control room.
- Multi-language Interface: Available in multiple Indian languages for accessibility.
- Offline Support: Works partially even without internet or mobile data.

Why It’s Useful:
It is India’s official and integrated emergency app linked with the pan-India helpline number 112, making it essential for every citizen.

🔗 Download for Android | [Download for iOS](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/112-india/id1454433232)

### 2. Aarogya Setu – Health and Safety Monitoring App

Developer: Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)
Available on: Android and iOS
Purpose: COVID-19 risk tracking and health emergency alerts

Key Features:

- COVID-19 Risk Assessment: Detects proximity to COVID-positive individuals using Bluetooth and GPS.
- Access to Helpline Numbers: Quick access to central and state-wise health helplines.
- Self-Assessment Test: Helps users identify symptoms and risk level before contacting a doctor.
- Emergency Contact Features: Direct links to dial 1075 (Health Helpline) and other local health authorities.
- Real-time Alerts: Notifies users of high-risk zones and areas with virus outbreaks.

Why It’s Useful:
While originally launched for COVID-19, it remains a vital health app with emergency resources integrated into the interface. Especially helpful for the elderly and those with comorbidities.

🔗 Download for Android | [Download for iOS](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/aarogyasetu/id1505825357)

### 3. mParivahan App – Vehicle & Traffic Emergency Companion

Developer: Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH)
Available on: Android and iOS
Purpose: Road safety, traffic rule enforcement, vehicle validation

Key Features:

- Vehicle Information: Instantly access RC and DL status using registration or license number.
- Emergency Services: Allows reporting of accidents or violations directly to authorities.
- Virtual DL/RC Storage: Useful in traffic checks and during emergencies where documents are lost or unavailable.
- Traffic Challan Check: Check and pay fines instantly.
- Citizen Reporting: Users can upload images/videos of road rule violations or accidents.

Why It’s Useful:
In road accidents or car breakdowns, this app becomes an important tool to access verified vehicle info, report emergencies, and seek help without physical documents.

🔗 Download for Android | [Download for iOS](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/mparivahan/id1454074772)

### 4. UMANG App (Unified Mobile Application for New-Age Governance)

Developer: Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) & National e-Governance Division
Available on: Android, iOS, and web
Purpose: Access to 200+ government services through a single platform

Key Features:

- Emergency Health Services: Book hospital appointments, check health insurance details under Ayushman Bharat.
- Police Services: File police complaints, check FIR status in some states.
- State Emergency Helplines: Access direct links to 100, 108, 112, and state-specific support numbers.
- Integration with Digilocker & MyGov: For storing documents and participating in government safety programs.
- Multi-language Support: Makes emergency services accessible to non-English speakers.

Why It’s Useful:
Beyond emergencies, UMANG provides essential utilities like EPFO, Passport Seva, PAN services, and health e-cards — all of which are vital in urgent situations like identity loss, medical emergencies, or legal help.

🔗 Download for Android | [Download for iOS](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/umang/id1236448856)

## ⚠️ Do’s and Don’ts During an Emergency

### ✅ Do’s:

- Stay calm and breathe deeply.
- Call the correct emergency number quickly.
- Clearly state your name, location, and nature of the emergency.
- Follow instructions given by the responder.
- Use GPS or landmarks if you don’t know the exact address.

### ❌ Don’ts:

- Don’t panic or shout on the call.
- Don’t disconnect until told to do so.
- Don’t make prank or false calls – it’s illegal.
- Don’t use emergency numbers for casual queries.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) about Emergency Numbers in India and Worldwide

1. What is the emergency helpline number in India?
The primary emergency helpline number in India is 112, which integrates police, ambulance, and fire services for quick assistance in any emergency.

2. How does the 112 emergency number work across Indian states?
The 112 emergency number is accessible in all Indian states and union territories, providing a single toll-free helpline that connects callers to the nearest emergency response center.

3. What are the best emergency apps available in India?
The 112 India App is the official government app for emergencies, featuring an SOS button and live location tracking. Other important apps include Aarogya Setu for health emergencies, mParivahan for vehicle-related emergencies, and UMANG for accessing government emergency services.

4. Can I use 911 or other international emergency numbers in India?
No, emergency numbers like 911 are not valid in India. You must dial 112 to access emergency services anywhere in India.

5. Which number should I call in case of a road accident in India?
In a road accident, dial 112 or the ambulance number 102 immediately. The mParivahan app can also assist by verifying vehicles and reporting accidents.

6. Is there a dedicated emergency helpline for women’s safety in India?
Yes, women can call the 1091 women’s helpline or use the 112 India App SOS feature to send emergency alerts to the police.

7. What number should I call for child emergencies in India?
For child-related emergencies, dial the Childline helpline 1098, which operates 24/7 to provide immediate assistance to children in distress.

8. How do I get emergency medical help if I don’t know my exact location?
Dialing 112 uses your phone’s GPS to share your location automatically with emergency responders, ensuring quick medical assistance even if you are unsure of your location.

9. What are the emergency numbers to know when traveling abroad?
Common international emergency numbers include 911 in the USA, 999 in the UK, 000 in Australia, 999/998/997 in UAE, and 995/999 in Singapore. Always check local emergency numbers before traveling.

10. Are emergency calls to numbers like 112 free in India?
Yes, all emergency calls to numbers such as 112, 100, 101, 102, and 1098 are completely free from both mobile phones and landlines across India.

11. Can I call India’s emergency numbers from a foreign SIM card?
Emergency numbers in India generally work only with Indian SIM cards. If you are using an international SIM in India, it is recommended to install the 112 India App or use a local SIM for emergency calls.

12. What emergency services can I access through the UMANG app?
The UMANG app offers access to a wide range of government services including health appointments, police complaints, and emergency contacts for quick assistance.

13. What is the fire emergency number in India?
The fire emergency number in India is 101. Alternatively, calling 112 also connects you to fire services through an integrated response system.

14. Is there a special emergency number for railway-related emergencies?
Yes, for railway emergencies, dial 139 for general inquiries and 182 for security-related emergencies on trains.

15. How can I remember India’s emergency numbers easily?
Remember 112, the single emergency helpline number covering police, fire, ambulance, and disaster services across India. Downloading the 112 India App can help you access emergency services quickly.
