## Helpful financial resources

- [Getting started with FAFSA](https://tuition.asu.edu/financial-aid/fafsa): Use this site to get started on the FAFSA, complete with helpful resources and FAQs.
- [Tuition Estimator](https://tuition.asu.edu/cost/tuition-estimator): Answer a few questions to see an estimate of your tuition and fees based on your major, campus, residency and enrollment status.
- [College Cost Comparison Tool](https://tuition.asu.edu/financial-aid/cost-comparison): Quickly compare estimated college costs at ASU with other universities using the College Cost Comparison Tool — customize your residency, living situation and more.
- [Scholarship Universe](https://asu.scholarshipuniverse.com/): All admitted students can access ASU's Scholarship Universe. There are scholarships for a wide range of talents, personal stories, creativity and more. Scholarship Universe includes both ASU and non-ASU scholarships, so there’s something for everyone.

## Frequently asked questions

[steps to apply](https://admission.asu.edu/transfer/apply) and [FAFSA](http://fafsa.gov) by Jan. 15 for fall and Oct. 1 for spring to ensure a smooth enrollment process.

[My ASU](http://my.asu.edu) and your ASU email for notification of any additional documents needed to complete financial aid packaging.

[apply to ASU](https://webapp4.asu.edu/uga_admissionsapp/), you’ll answer a series of residency questions on the application, and ASU will use this information to make an initial residency determination. You can see your residency classification in [My ASU](http://my.asu.edu) in the My Application Status box. If you are classified as Non-Resident and would like to petition for reclassification, contact the [Residency Classification](http://registrar.asu.edu/residency) office after reviewing the residency requirements.

[Scholarship Universe](http://asu.scholarshipuniverse.com) to explore and apply for additional scholarships.

- Must be an Arizona resident for tuition purposes.
- Must be a transfer student from an Arizona community college.
- Must have a minimum 3.50 cumulative transfer GPA.
- Must demonstrate financial need as determined by the FAFSA.

[transfer admission team member](https://admission.asu.edu/contact/undergraduate) for further assistance.

[defer your admission](https://admission.asu.edu/apply/first-year/admitted/cancel-defer), or [reapply for admission](http://asu.edu/apply) if you plan to enroll elsewhere before attending ASU.

[admission deferment](https://admission.asu.edu/apply/first-year/admitted/cancel-defer) on file. Keep in mind that deferment requests must be submitted at least two weeks before the start of your admit term. You must also continue to meet all eligibility and renewal requirements for the Transfer Achievement Grant, which include submitting a FAFSA each year.

[reapply for admission](http://asu.edu/apply) when you’re ready to return to ASU. You may then be reconsidered for the Transfer Achievement Grant upon admission.
