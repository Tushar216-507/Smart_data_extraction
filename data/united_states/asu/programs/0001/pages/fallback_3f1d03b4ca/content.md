# Mass

Mass is the amount of matter present in any object or body. Everything we see around us has mass. For example, a table, a chair, your bed, a football, a glass, and even air has mass. This means all objects are light or heavy because of their mass. In this lesson, we will learn what is mass, how to calculate mass, and its examples while discovering interesting facts about it. ## What is Mass?

In Physics, mass is the most basic property of matter and it is one of the fundamental quantities. Mass is defined as the amount of matter present in a body. The SI unit of mass is the kilogram (kg).

### Mass Definition

The definition of mass says that mass is a quantity that represents the amount of matter in a particle or an object.

Note: The mass of a body is constant; it does not change at any time. Only in certain extreme cases when a huge amount of energy is given or taken from a body, the mass may be impacted. For example, in a nuclear reaction, a tiny amount of matter is converted into a huge amount of energy, which reduces the mass of the substance.

### Mass Formula

The mass of an object can be calculated using different formulas depending on the parameters given.

- The first formula of mass is expressed as, Mass = Density × Volume. This means if we know the density and [volume](https://www.cuemath.com/measurement/volume/)of an object, we can find its mass using this formula of mass.
- The second formula of mass is expressed as, Mass = Force ÷ Acceleration. This means if we know the force and acceleration of an object, we can find its mass using this formula of mass.
- The third formula of mass is expressed as, Mass = Weight ÷ Gravitational Acceleration. This means if we know the weight of an object and the gravitational acceleration, we can find its mass using this formula of mass.

## What is the Unit of Mass?

There are various units for calculating mass, like, kilograms, grams, lbs, pounds, etc., but the SI unit of mass is 'kilogram' or kg. Every unit of mass can be converted to other units by using a proper conversion formula without affecting the meaning and essence of the quantity to be measured.

Note: 1kg is equal to 2.20462 pounds.

## Solved Examples of Mass

- Example 1: State true or false with reference to mass. a.) Mass is always constant for a body. b.) The SI unit of mass is Newton

  Solution: a.) True, mass is always constant for a body. b.) False, the SI unit of mass is Kilograms.
- Example 2: The mass of an object is 6,000 pounds. Calculate its mass in tons.

  Solution: We know that 1 ton = 2000 pounds. Thus, 6000 pounds = 6000/2000 = 3 tons.
- Example 3: Damian has a book that has a mass of 2.2 kg. What is the weight of the book on the Earth?

  Solution:

  Mass of textbook = 2.2 kg and acceleration due to gravity = 9.8 m/s2

  Weight formula = Mass × Acceleration due to gravity

  Weight of textbook = 2.2 × 9.8

  Weight of textbook = 21.56 N

  Therefore, the weight of the textbook on Earth is 21.56 N.

## FAQs on Mass

### What is Mass?

The definition of mass says that mass is a quantity that represents the amount of matter in a particle or an object. In other words, everything we see around us has mass and all objects are light or heavy because of their mass. The SI unit of mass is kilograms.

### How to Find Mass?

The mass of an object can be measured using any ordinary balance. For example, beam balance, lever balance, pan balance, etc. It can also be calculated using the formula, Mass = volume × density. This means if we know the volume and density of an object we can easily find its mass. Apart from this, there are other formulas that help in calculating the mass of an object, for example, Mass = Force ÷ Acceleration and Mass = Weight ÷ Gravitational Acceleration

### What is the Mass Formula?

The mass of an object can be calculated using different formulas depending on the known values.

- The first formula of mass is expressed as, Mass = Density × Volume. This means if we know the density and volume of an object, we can find its mass using this formula of mass.
- The second formula of mass is expressed as, Mass = Force ÷ Acceleration. This means if we know the force and acceleration of an object, we can find its mass using this formula of mass.

### Does Air Have Mass?

Yes, the air has mass. For example, an inflated balloon weighs heavier than a deflated balloon, which proves that the air inside the inflated balloon has mass.

### Are Mass and Volume the Same?

No, mass is the amount of matter that an object is made up of. [Volume](https://www.cuemath.com/measurement/volume/) is the amount of vacant space that an object takes up. Hence, mass and volume are not the same.

### Are Weight and Mass the Same?

No, weight and mass are not the same. Weight is the amount of force acting on a body and mass is the amount of matter in a body. Weight can be changed depending upon the force while mass is constant. The SI unit of weight is Newton, while the SI unit of mass is kilograms.

### What is the S.I Unit of Mass?

The S.I Unit of Mass is Kilograms (kg). This means that the mass of an object is expressed in [kilograms](https://www.cuemath.com/measurement/kilogram/). For example, we can say that the mass of an object is 36 kilograms.

### What is the Mass Equation?

The mass equation is another name for the mass formula. And there are different formulas for calculating the mass of an object.

- If we know the density and volume of an object, we can find its mass using the formula: Mass = Density × Volume
- If we know the force and acceleration of an object, we can find its mass using the formula: Mass = Force ÷ Acceleration
- If we know the weight of an object and the gravitational acceleration, we can find its mass using the formula: Mass = Weight ÷ Gravitational Acceleration
