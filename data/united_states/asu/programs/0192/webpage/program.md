# Special Education (Applied Behavior Analysis), MA

# ABA (applied behavior analysis), AUTISM, Analyst, BCaBA, Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA), Child Development, Data, Developmental, EVIDENCE BASED, Early Intervention, Human Services, Organizational Behavior Management (OBM), Special Ed, disabilities

Prepare to support and advocate for individuals with diverse learning and behavioral needs.

The Master of Arts program in special education with a concentration in applied behavior analysis is an online program that combines an assortment of resources and technologies to provide you with the opportunity to interact directly with program faculty and classmates. Applied projects and assignments allow you to focus on the population that you plan to work while gaining the knowledge and skills you need in order to use applied behavior analysis across various populations and settings.

This program meets the behavior-analytic coursework requirements for those pursuing the Behavior Analyst Certification Board® Board Certified Behavior Analyst® examination Pathway 2.

The course sequence includes the Behavior Analyst Certification Board-required 315 classroom hours of graduate-level instruction aligned with the BCBA Test Content Outline (TCO 6th edition). It also meets the upcoming 2027 academic content standards under Pathway 2.

BCBA-supervised independent fieldwork supervision hours, required for certification, are arranged and completed by you separately from your ASU coursework. An optional remote BCBA supervised independent fieldwork practicum (SPE 580 Applied Behavior Analysis Practicum) is available. Once certified, you are equipped to meet diverse behavior needs and make a lasting difference in the community.
