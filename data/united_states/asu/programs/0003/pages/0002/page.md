Interdisciplinary Studies Concentrations

Students take an active role in designing their own major by selecting two subject areas of study.

Students select two concentrations from the options below. Credit hours vary depending on chosen concentrations. Concentrations require a minimum of 15 hours, of which at least nine are upper division. Approved IDS concentration area requirements align with respective minor and certificate requirements. Students pursuing IDS concentrations that are minors may not also earn the minor.
