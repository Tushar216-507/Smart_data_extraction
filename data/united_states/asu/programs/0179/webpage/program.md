## Applied business degree courses

General business courses cover fundamentals from accounting, analytics and computer information systems to economics, marketing and management. You’ll also take a set of elective courses determined by your focus areas.

Additionally, if you choose the applied internship course, you’ll earn up to 12 credits as you make a direct impact on an organization. You may be able to complete this experience with your current employer or one you identify.

Carey is advancing a school-wide effort to integrate artificial intelligence across the undergraduate business curriculum through a Mindful AI Triad framework: business knowledge, AI fluency, and ethical judgment.
