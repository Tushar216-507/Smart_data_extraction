THE IARE ADVANTAGE

Institute of Aeronautical Engineering (IARE), Hyderabad was established in the year 2000 by a devoted group of eminent professional and industrialists having a long and outstanding experience in educational system with a mission ‘Education for Liberation’. It was first founded in the year 1994, as Institute of Aircraft Maintenance Engineering offering certification program in aircraft maintenance engineering, later started B.Tech program in Aeronautical Engineering and has gradually transformed itself into a integrated multi-disciplinary technological institute.

IARE is a premier private autonomous institution approved by All India Council of Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi; affiliated to Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University Hyderabad (JNTUH), Hyderabad; accreditation by NAAC with A++ grade and 80% of courses offered are NBA accredited.

The founders and administrators of IARE are true academicians who are committed to helping the students to achieve their potential in the education, research and employment world of this exciting new millennium. They bring together almost twenty six years of engineering, medical and secondary education experience. Their backgrounds include teaching, research and professional training. The main focus of the institute is ensuring curriculum quality, the delivery of pedagogical services and ultimately, the student success. Our philosophy is that everyone has a claim to education to better their life, advance their career and reach their desired level of personal success.

Career-focused education - Connect with leading companies and prepare for real-world success.

Ranked constantly for the past eight years in a row, as one among top 200 engineering colleges nationally, and one among top 100 in Innovation by NIRF India Rankings, Ministry of Education, Govt. of India. This illustrates positive performance and quality standards we maintain in teaching, research, employability and innovation.

Dedicated faculty offer students an innovative and stimulating learning experience both inside the classroom and laboratory environment and within their community.

Professional and support staff offers students every opportunity to succeed in their studies and extracurricular activities with specialized services tailored to their individual needs.

Superior facilities are available to students, faculty and staff, from air-conditioned smart classrooms and labs to business innovation to sports installations and to good food service in cafeteria.

Big placements greet our students with ample opportunities with around 62 core and software companies visiting the institute every year and successful in placing 91% of eligible students. 17% of the students are going overseas for higher studies.

Research initiatives among students and faculty, and Entrepreneurship drive among students, makes IARE as one of the best institutions to enhance the performance of the students.

Affordable tuition fee - Get top-quality education without the high price tag.
