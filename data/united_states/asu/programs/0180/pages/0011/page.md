## Your next steps as an admitted graduate student

### After you activate your ASURITE User ID, visit My ASU

Your ASURITE User ID is the official login name you’ll use to access most ASU systems and services. You’ll get your ASURITE User ID and activation instructions shortly after you submit your application. A message from [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection) with the subject line "ASU Account Activation" will be sent to the email address you provided in your application.

If you can't remember you ASURITE User ID you can look it up at: [asu.edu/lostpassword](https://asu.edu/lostpassword)

You’ll log in to [My ASU](https://my.asu.edu) with your ASURITE User ID and password to:

- View your priority tasks and next steps for enrollment
- View your advisor contact information
- View financial aid and billing information
- Connect to campus technology and services

How to log in to My ASU:

- Navigate to [my.asu.edu](https://my.asu.edu).
- Enter your ASURITE User ID.
- Enter your password. (Forgot your password? Visit [asu.edu/lostpassword](https://asu.edu/lostpassword).)
- Click the “Sign In” button to access your account.

After you enroll in classes, visit [asu.edu/emailsignup](https://asu.edu/emailsignup) to activate your official @asu.edu email address. Once activated, you will access and check your official ASU email through your ASU Gmail account.

Be sure to check both your non-ASU personal email address and your ASU Gmail inbox regularly.

### Complete the Free Application for Federal Student Aid

To apply for federal financial aid, complete a [Free Application for Federal Student Aid](https://studentaid.gov/h/apply-for-aid/fafsa). View your FAFSA information on your Finances tab in My ASU, and view your Priority Tasks for important financial aid next steps. Complete any items listed under Priority Tasks early to ensure you do not have any barriers to receiving your aid.

We encourage you to [compare financial aid and college costs](/cost-aid).

Note: Arizona law requires verification of citizenship or other lawful presence in the U.S. for all students receiving financial aid funded by state money.

[Learn more](/cost-aid)

### Submit your MMR immunization verification form (on-campus students only

All students born after Jan. 1, 1957, are required to meet the following [MMR immunization requirement](https://asuportal.pointnclick.com/Mvc/Portal/Login) before classes begin. You can upload the required documentation in the [My Health Portal](https://asuportal.pointnclick.com/login_dualauthentication.aspx).
The MMR vaccine is available for a fee at any of [ASU's Health Services](https://eoss.asu.edu/health/contact) locations.

[Learn more](https://eoss.asu.edu/health/parents/immunization)

### Pay your enrollment deposit (on-campus students only)

Confirm your place in ASU by submitting your enrollment deposit via [My ASU](http://my.asu.edu). Then, be sure to check My ASU often for any Priority Tasks needed to complete your steps to enroll.

[Enrollment deposit](https://admission.asu.edu/deposit#graduate)

### Register for classes

Once your enrollment deposit is paid, you can meet with your academic advisor for more information on [how to register for courses](https://students.asu.edu/register-for-classes?_ga=2.93573652.847924599.1595263629-538413994.1582755467). You can find the name, email and phone number for your academic advisor in the Academic Support Team box in My ASU. Click on the Academic Advisor option there to find this contact information and schedule your appointment. Note that you may have a hold on your enrollment that will need to be completed before registering for classes.

Spring admits: Class registration typically becomes available mid-October. Review and complete any outstanding holds you may have under Priority Tasks in [My ASU](https://my.asu.edu/).

Fall admits: Class registration typically becomes available mid-spring. Review and complete any outstanding holds you may have under Priority Tasks in [My ASU](https://my.asu.edu/).

[Register for classes](https://students.asu.edu/register-for-classes)
