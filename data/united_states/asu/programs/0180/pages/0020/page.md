## In-state transfer student tuition cost

Paying for college isn’t always easy, but it pays off down the road. You’re already well on your way toward a bachelor’s degree, and financial aid and ASU’s competitive tuition rate can make it easier to get there.

Please note that everyone’s net tuition cost is specific to their own circumstances and will be different for each person. To get an idea of what you can expect to pay, please see the [ASU tuition calculator](https://tuition.asu.edu/cost/tuition-estimator).
