WUE update

Students admitted to ASU under the Western Undergraduate Exchange, or WUE, program in spring 2026 or earlier will continue to receive the WUE tuition rate for the duration of their undergraduate program, provided they maintain continuous enrollment and meet renewal criteria.

Introducing the ASU Commitment Scholarship

Beginning with students admitted for fall 2026, ASU will no longer participate in the WUE program.

ASU is launching the ASU Commitment Scholarship, a new scholarship designed for nonresident students to make an ASU degree more accessible to students nationwide. Unlike WUE, the ASU Commitment Scholarship is open to first-year*, out-of-state (not Arizona) U.S. citizens, regardless of their state of residency, campus choice or major.

This renewable scholarship is available to out-of-state students enrolled in any degree program on the Phoenix-area campuses, as well as the ASU California Center in Los Angeles. It can also be combined with the New American University Scholarship, giving students even more opportunity to lower their cost of attendance.

The ASU Commitment Scholarship ensures nonresident students have access to a high-quality education at a great value, wherever they begin their ASU journey.

Explore estimated scholarships you could be eligible for on ASU's Scholarship Estimator.

*Transfer students are not eligible for the ASU Commitment Scholarship. However, out-of-state transfer students can be automatically considered for a New American University Scholarship upon admission. You must have a minimum 3.0 cumulative transfer GPA for scholarship consideration, and scholarship amounts vary based on GPA.
