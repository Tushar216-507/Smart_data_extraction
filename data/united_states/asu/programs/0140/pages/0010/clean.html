Military students and families

Military admission and application process

Military students are required to fill out the online application form, pay the nonrefundable application fee and submit their official transcripts to Arizona State University. New students who have not used any military education benefits at an institution of higher education may also apply for benefits.

A thriving community of military students

When you enroll at ASU, you're joining nearly 11,000 military-affiliated students pursuing their degrees online at ASU. You’ll also be part of a top-ranked university with support services designed to meet your unique needs as an active duty service member, veteran or military family member.

Is ASU Online a good school for military students?

Yes! ASU Online is a great school for military-affiliated students. In fact, ASU ranks #3 for best online bachelor’s programs for veterans by U.S. News & World Report, 2026. We’re also ranked in the top 5 for our online master’s in education and MBA programs for veterans, and in the top 10 for our online MBA, master's in engineering, and criminal justice programs for veterans.
