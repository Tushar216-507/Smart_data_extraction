International students

International student admission

The application process for international students is almost the same as it for domestic students, but there are some differences including a requirement to submit proof of English proficiency. Check out our resources below to get the details on English language proficiency and academic requirements for international students, and submit your application to Arizona State University.

Study from anywhere in the world

ASU consists of learners from locations across the globe, including nearly 1,000 foreign national students enrolled from abroad. ASU Online typically considers any foreign national enrolled from abroad to be an international student. However, those with foreign credentials, regardless of location or citizenship, may also be required to submit translated transcripts or proof of English proficiency.

International students include transfer and first-year students, and those pursuing undergraduate or graduate degrees.

Why international students choose ASU Online

Students all around the world are part of the ASU Online community. ASU Online enables learners outside of the U.S. to be a part of the university that’s been ranked the most innovative in the U.S. for 11 years in a row and a top world university based on our programs, groundbreaking initiatives, partnerships and research.

ASU Online provides international students a unique opportunity to advance their knowledge and skills by learning from our world-class faculty who teach on-campus programs without having to leave their families or careers. The knowledge you gain can set you apart by increasing expertise in your current employment or helping you shift toward new potentials, especially for those working in the private sector.
