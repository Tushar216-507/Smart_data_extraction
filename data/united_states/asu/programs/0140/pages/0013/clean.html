How to apply to ASU

We support our undecided students through each step of their journey, including during admission. To apply to Arizona State University, you’ll follow the standard application process including submitting the online application, paying the application fee and sending your transcripts. We have enrollment coaches who can assist with each step in the process.

Can I be an undecided student at ASU Online?

Deciding on a major is a big decision for college students. Not sure which one is right for you, but want to enroll at ASU Online? You can enroll in one of our exploratory programs, which lets you explore your passions to discover the right path for you. Each exploratory track focuses on a general academic theme and is composed of a three-semester major map, culminating in the declaration of a major no later than after the completion of 45 credit hours.

Not looking to earn a degree? Learn more about our continuing education opportunities and learning opportunities for nondegree seeking students.

Why ASU Online is right for you as an undecided student

ASU Online offers undecided students the opportunity to begin their academic journey on one of four exploratory tracks. These three-semester tracks focus on one of four general themes:

  • Health and life sciences.
  • Humanities, fine arts and design.
  • Math, physical sciences, engineering and technology.
  • Social and behavioral sciences.

Exploratory students also receive services personalized to their unique needs. This includes career and major exploration events and workshops, personalized academic advising and assistance with staying on track to a timely graduation.

Find your path with support from us

ASU Online recognizes that all students have a unique journey, and we’ll be there for you with a suite of personalized services. This includes online tutoring, financial aid assistance and a dedicated success coach who will empower you to stay on track to a timely graduation.
