Who are first-year students at ASU?

First-year students at ASU are students who have never attended college before or have less than 12 transferable credit hours, and have a high school diploma or GED. First-year students are new college students pursuing an undergraduate degree.

Support from your first year to graduation and beyond

Navigating college can be stressful, especially for incoming first-year students. ASU Online offers full support to you in your first year of college, providing you with several ways to connect with others, become active members of the ASU community and to make the most of the ASU first-year experience. Student-led clubs are available as well as success coaches, career advisors, online tutoring and more.
