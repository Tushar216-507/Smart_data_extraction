Transfer students

Who are transfer students at ASU Online?

You qualify as a transfer student if you’re an undergraduate applicant who meets one of the following criteria:

  • You’re looking to complete a bachelor’s degree and have 12 or more transferable credits post-high school.
  • You’re seeking an additional bachelor’s degree.

While graduate applicants may be eligible to transfer up to 12 credits, depending on their intended program, they follow the graduate student admission process, not the transfer student process.

Make your credits count

Whether you’re continuing your education or returning to finish what you started, we’re here to help. Our personalized transfer tools are designed to help you bring as many of your credits with you as possible, so you can move forward without starting over.

  • ASU’s Transfer Guideis an online tool designed to help you plan your transfer. You can save course searches, manage your transfer pathways, contact an advisor, track your degree progress and more.
  • MyPath2ASUprovides personalized guidance to align your completed courses with ASU degree requirements. It’s designed for students just starting their college journey, so you can maximize your credit transfers and graduate on time.

Transfer-friendly degrees

At ASU, most undergraduate programs accept up to 64 transfer credits. For students looking to transfer even more credits, we offer a variety of transfer-friendly degrees that allow a faster path to graduation and long-term savings.

  • Our transfer-friendly degrees accept more transfer credits than our other programs, including electives and coursework from different majors.
  • With fewer prerequisites and flexible course substitutions, you won’t lose time retaking similar classes.
  • You can customize your degree plan to align with your academic background and career goals.

Whether you’ve changed majors, taken time off school or are pursuing something new, these programs are built to adapt to your unique journey and keep you moving forward.
