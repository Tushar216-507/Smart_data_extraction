# Graduate students

## Explore graduate student admission

To be admitted into an online graduate program at Arizona State University, you must meet the minimum requirements outlined by the university, as well as the specific program you’re applying to. You’ll also have to complete an application, pay an application fee, send official transcripts and submit additional materials, depending on the program. Explore our resources to learn more about graduate admission and the application process.

## Who are graduate students at ASU?

You’re considered a graduate student at ASU if you’re working to complete a master’s or doctorate degree. Graduate students must meet all of the admission requirements and enroll in a specific program.

## Become an expert in your field

Earning a graduate degree is a great way to set yourself apart from the competition and advance your career. With courses focused on expanding your skill set, graduate programs give you expertise in your field and real-world experience in your community.

While many schools offer graduate programs, at ASU Online, you can further your education without having to pause your career. With more than 200 online master's and graduate programs, ASU Online makes it easy to embrace your passion and earn a graduate degree on your terms.

Take the [DegreeMe quiz](https://degreeme.asuonline.asu.edu/) to explore online graduate programs based on your interests and goals. Answer a few questions to receive personalized program matches and custom content about why each program might interest you.
