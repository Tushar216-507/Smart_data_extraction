ASU Online scholarships

Apply for scholarships and grants

Online students at Arizona State University may be eligible for scholarships, including ASU Online scholarships. Submit your FAFSA and you’ll automatically be considered for the ASU Online scholarships you qualify for. No separate application required.

Please note most nondegree seeking students are not eligible for scholarships or other financial aid.
