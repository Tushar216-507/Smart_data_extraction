Overview

Our graduate students have opportunities to learn from and engage collaboratively with renowned faculty across a wide range of fields, in an environment where research, creative work, and teaching are tested, refined, and reimagined. Beyond the classroom, we offer a huge variety of special events that bring students and faculty into ongoing conversations.

Structured opportunities for professional development and mentoring—such as ASU's Preparing Future Careers and our own departmental workshops, professionalization courses, and widely lauded internship program — help students translate their graduate training into meaningful careers both within and beyond academia.

Finding the program that is right for you

The Department of English offers many graduate degree programs, each with distinct goals and culminating experiences. Understanding the degree you are pursuing—and how its structure aligns with your academic and professional aspirations—is an essential part of graduate success. We encourage you to explore these differences carefully as you chart your path forward.

No matter where your graduate studies take you, your journey begins here. For more information, contact us by emailing [email protected] to learn how our programs can help you shape your future.
