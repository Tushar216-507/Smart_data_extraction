### Explore the Possibilities

Internships help students connect classroom learning with meaningful professional experiences while developing the knowledge, skills, and confidence to pursue a wide range of careers. Through internships, students explore their interests, build professional networks, strengthen communication and leadership skills, and gain valuable real-world experience before graduation.

Whether you're still exploring career options or already have a specific path in mind, internships can help you discover what you enjoy, develop transferable skills, and prepare for life after ASU.

Looking for inspiration or ideas? Explore our [Department of English Internships & Career Futures LinkedIn page](https://linkedin.com/showcase/asu-english-internships-and-career-futures) for internship and job opportunities, career resources, and professional development tips. You can also visit the [National Humanities Alliance Career Success](https://nhalliance.org/career-success/) page to learn more about career pathways and the value of humanities internships.
