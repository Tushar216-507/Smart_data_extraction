Immerse yourself in our campus culture

One of the many benefits of studying in the Department of English at Arizona State University is the vibrant, Tempe campus atmosphere. From theatre to sporting events to high-tech research spaces and top-notch libraries, there’s so much to explore and discover.

Just a short walk from campus, you’ll be in the heart of downtown Tempe with fun-filled activities for every personality type. Outdoor adventurist? Enjoy a hike. Food enthusiast? Try one of the hundreds of restaurants nearby. Environmentalist? Ride the light rail or bike around town. Whatever you desire, the Tempe campus delivers.

But, don’t take our word for it.

Visit campus and find out for yourself.
