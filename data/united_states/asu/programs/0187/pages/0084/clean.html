Your degree from ASU will set you apart. The benefits of having a college education are numerous and documented. Added value is that many employers actively seek English and humanities majors. Lyon Poetry Award, Nick Ivins Memorial Literature Scholarship, Friends of the Department of English Scholarship and more to help fund your education. If you’re ready to start constructing your future, we’ll help reward you for your efforts.
