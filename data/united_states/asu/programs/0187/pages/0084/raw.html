Tuition and Paying for College

Fund your undergraduate education

Your degree from ASU will set you apart. The benefits of having a college education are numerous and documented. Added value is that many employers actively seek English and humanities majors. So don’t let the cost of tuition hinder your decision to study in the Department of English. We believe a humanities education at ASU is worth the investment.

Along with generous financial aid and federal work study options, you’ll also have access to an array of Department of English scholarships and awards, including the Don and Alleen Nilsen Humor Scholarship Award, Glendon and Kathryn Swarthout Awards, Homecoming Writing Contest, John Doebler Memorial Award in Shakespeare and Renaissance Studies, Jules Anatole Creative Writing Scholarship, Mabelle A. Lyon Poetry Award, Nick Ivins Memorial Literature Scholarship, Friends of the Department of English Scholarship and more to help fund your education. If you’re ready to start constructing your future, we’ll help reward you for your efforts.
