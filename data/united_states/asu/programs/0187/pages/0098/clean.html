Are you passionate about your field of study? Do you enjoy meeting new people and sharing your ASU experience? The Department of English is seeking enthusiastic undergraduate students to serve as Student Ambassadors for the upcoming academic year. Help inspire future Sun Devils while developing valuable leadership, communication, and professional skills.

What you'll do

  • Represent the Department of English at recruitment and outreach events, both in person and online.
  • Assist in welcoming prospective students and families through department tours, individual meetings, phone calls, and virtual conversations.
  • Share your experiences as a Department of English student and help prospective students explore academic opportunities.
  • Contribute to departmental communications that highlight student experiences and the value of the humanities.
  • Support department events, student engagement activities, and community outreach initiatives.

What you’ll gain

  • Leadership and professional experience
  • Public speaking and communication skills
  • Networking opportunities with faculty, staff, and community partners
  • Experience that strengthens your résumé, graduate school applications, and professional portfolio
  • Optional internship credit through ENG 484

We’re looking for students who …

  • Are enrolled in a Department of English undergraduate major, minor, or certificate.
  • Are in good academic standing.
  • Enjoy connecting with others and sharing their ASU experience.
  • Demonstrate professionalism, strong communication skills, and enthusiasm for representing the Department of English.

If this sounds like you, we'd love to hear from you.

What to expect

Student Ambassadors serve from August through May, contributing approximately three hours per week through recruitment events, outreach activities, ambassador meetings, and departmental programs.

Optional internship credit (ENG 484) is available for students who wish to earn academic credit for their participation.

How to apply

  • Submit the following by Aug. 31, 2026 to [email protected]:
  • A 250-word statement of interest describing why you'd like to serve as a Department of English Student Ambassador.
  • A current résumé.

We hope you'll join us!
