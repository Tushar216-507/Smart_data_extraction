The BS in integrative health degree provides opportunities for students to pursue two separate pathways.

The pre health grad school track provides an opportunity for students interested in pursuing graduate study in the health professions. This track includes the core required courses for most graduate programs. Students pursuing a graduate program in health should consult with an advisor to review the complete curriculum for graduate school preparation in the health professions.

The nursing and general health track includes the prerequisites for the nursing program at Arizona State University. This track also provides options for students interested in health care.
