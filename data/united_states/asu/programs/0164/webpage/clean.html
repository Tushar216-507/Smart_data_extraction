Why W. P. Carey’s online MBA stands out

W. P. Carey online MBA courses

The online MBA curriculum at W. P. Carey covers the key disciplines every business leader needs, from finance and operations to strategy and marketing. Core coursework is shared across the MBA programs regardless of modality, so you benefit from the same academic rigor. Elective courses let you pursue areas tied to your professional ambitions, and if you choose a concentration, your electives will go deeper in a single discipline.

Careers for MBA graduates

A degree from W. P. Carey prepares you to lead at the senior level across industries. For example, general and operations managers earn a median annual wage of $102,950, with top earners exceeding $239,200, according to the Bureau of Labor Statistics, 2024. This online MBA builds cross-functional skills for management, executive and strategy roles in business, technology, finance and beyond.

How to apply

Applicants must fulfill the requirements of both the Graduate College and the W. P. Carey School of Business.

This program has a highly competitive admission process.
