Professional Practicum Courses

BSW advancement into practicum courses requires completion of the following:

1) Successful completion of SWU 171, SWU 291, SWU 295, SWU 303, SWU 306, SWU 311, SWU 312, SWU 340, SWU 374

2) Documented completion of 40 hours human services experience

3) Level 1 Fingerprint Clearance Card

4) Signed Standards of Professional and Ethical Behavior form

Solution-Based Learning

Successful completion of the college's Solution-Based Learning (SBL) requirement is expected of each student by degree completion. Successful completion of SWU 291, SWU 412, or SWU 414 will satisfy the SBL requirement.

Interdisciplinary Requirement

Area 1: Community Resources and Development

Area 2: Criminology and Criminal Justice

Area 3: Public Affairs

Area 4: Public Service and Community Solutions

Students can choose courses from any of the four Watts interdisciplinary areas. To meet the requirement, students must take two courses from at least two different groups.
