# Online Master of Science in Biological Data Science

Arizona State University’s Master of Science in biological data science develops your expertise in using analytical methods and computational tools to interpret scientific data. In this program, you’ll gain a comprehensive understanding of natural sciences through a tailored curriculum and hands-on learning experiences. Upon graduation, you’ll be equipped for a variety of careers in one of the fastest-growing fields.

## Biological data science master’s courses

This interdisciplinary program covers a range of topics combining the functions of life sciences, mathematics, statistics, computing and more. In addition to your core curriculum, you’ll select from a variety of elective courses and complete a hands-on capstone project.

## Prepare for jobs in bioinformatics, data science and more

In this program, you’ll gain analytical skills in ecological, environmental and other biological applications that prepare you to work in emerging areas of interdisciplinary research.
