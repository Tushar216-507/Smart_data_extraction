About Company

Applied Auto Parts Pvt. Ltd. is a trusted auto parts manufacturer in India and a leading manufacturer of precision machined components, serving automotive and engineering industries worldwide. Established in 1981 in Rajkot, Gujarat, the company has grown from a small family-owned unit into a large-scale manufacturing organization with advanced infrastructure and a skilled workforce. With expertise in precision machining, forging, and casting, we provide end-to-end manufacturing solutions under one roof, ensuring high accuracy, consistency, and reliability in every component. Our focus on quality, technology, and continuous improvement enables us to meet customer-specific requirements and build long-term partnerships with OEM clients across global markets.

Policy

Applied Auto Parts Pvt. Ltd. is committed to manufacturing high-quality products that meet customer-specific requirements while building long-term relationships through customer satisfaction. We continuously focus on quality excellence, technological advancement, on-time delivery, innovation, workplace safety, and team development to achieve sustainable business growth and exceed customer expectations.
