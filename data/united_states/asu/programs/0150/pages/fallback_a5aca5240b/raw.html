What is EMS Consignment Number?

An EMS consignment number is a unique identifier assigned to Express Mail Service shipments sent through India Post. This 13-digit tracking number allows you to track the progress and location of your express package as it moves through the postal system with priority handling.

The EMS consignment number follows the international standard format with 13 characters. The first letter indicates the service type - 'E' for EMS Express Mail Service. The following characters indicate origin, unique identification number, and destination country code 'IN' for India.

Types of EMS Services
  • EE stands for EMS Express Mail Service for international shipments
  • EM stands for EMS Express Mail Service for domestic shipments
  • EP stands for EMS Premium service with additional features
  • ED stands for EMS Documents service for important papers
  • EC stands for EMS Commercial service for business packages

Understanding EMS Tracking Numbers

An EMS tracking number in India Post is a unique identifier assigned to Express Mail Service shipments. It is also commonly referred to as an "EMS tracking ID" or "express consignment number." This number allows both the sender and the recipient to track the progress and location of the express package as it moves through the priority postal network.

The EMS tracking number is comprised of 13 characters, with the first two letters indicating the service type. For instance, if you take the example of EE123456789IN, 'EE' represents EMS Express Mail Service. The middle 9 digits represent the unique identification number, and the last 2 digits, 'IN,' signify that the package is destined for or originated from INDIA.

EMS Service Types
  • EE stands for EMS Express Mail Service (International)
  • EM stands for EMS Express Mail Service (Domestic)
  • EP stands for EMS Premium Express Service
  • ED stands for EMS Document Express Service
  • EC stands for EMS Commercial Express Service
