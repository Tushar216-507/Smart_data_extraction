Nondegree graduate student admission

Admission standards

Admission process

  • Submit an

    application for graduate admission, indicating nondegree on the application.

  • Pay your application fee.

  • Nondegree applicants do not have to send transcripts or supportive documents to be admitted.

  • Submit your application at least one week prior to the first day of classes.

Nondegree status restrictions

  • Financial aid is not available to nondegree students.
  • Your application will remain active for up to two terms or as long as you are enrolled in classes.
  • VA education benefits are often not applicable to nondegree-seeking students.
