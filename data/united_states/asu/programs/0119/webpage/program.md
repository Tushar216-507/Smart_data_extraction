# Online Master of Science in Biomimicry

Arizona State University’s Master of Science in biomimicry online explores the connection between biological principles and design. Upon graduation, you’ll understand how to draw inspiration from nature’s evolutionary successes and apply this knowledge to solve complex human challenges. These versatile skills open doors to impactful roles in business, government and nonprofit organizations.

## Biomimicry master’s courses

Developed in collaboration with Biomimicry 3.8, this program features courses taught by one of the field’s top leaders. You’ll explore biome-based applications, business case studies, biological strategy research and virtual labs to gain a well-rounded foundation in biomimicry. The program offers flexibility to tailor your coursework and projects to your interests, culminating in a final practicum that provides hands-on experience applying biomimicry principles to your chosen field.

## What you can do with a biomimicry master’s

Biomimicry is an emerging discipline and its applications create demand for specialized roles across various industries in both the public and private sectors. This program is ideal for professionals looking to integrate nature-inspired design principles into their current field or pursue opportunities as consultants.
