[Tamil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamil_language)-language [action drama film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Action_drama_film) [written and directed by](#cite_note-4) [Lokesh Kanagaraj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lokesh_Kanagaraj) and produced by S. Xavier Britto under XB Film Creators. The film stars [Vijay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vijay_(actor)) in the main lead role as the protagonist and [Vijay Sethupathi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vijay_Sethupathi) as the antagonist. [Malavika Mohanan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malavika_Mohanan), [Andrea Jeremiah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrea_Jeremiah), [Arjun Das](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arjun_Das), [Shanthanu Bhagyaraj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanthanu_Bhagyaraj) and [Gouri G. Kishan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gouri_G._Kishan) play supporting roles. It is the first project of the production house, which also co-produced the film with Jagadish Palanisamy and [Seven Screen Studio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven_Screen_Studio). The film follows John Durairaj "JD", an alcoholic professor, who takes a three-month teaching job at a juvenile home, and clashes with Bhavani, a ruthless gangster, who uses the children as the [scapegoat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scapegoating) for his criminal activities.

[Rathna Kumar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rathna_Kumar) and Pon Parthiban being credited as co-writers. [Principal photography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principal_photography) commenced in October 2019 and ended in February 2020; the entire film was shot across [Delhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delhi), [Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai), [Chennai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chennai) and [Karnataka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karnataka) within 129 working days. Originally announced under the tentative title Thalapathy 64, the official title Master was announced on 31 December 2019. The music is composed by [Anirudh Ravichander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anirudh_Ravichander), while cinematography and editing are performed by [Sathyan Sooryan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sathyan_Sooryan) and [Philomin Raj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philomin_Raj), respectively.

[COVID-19 pandemic in India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_India). The makers preferred to wait for a theatrical release, rather than release it on any streaming service. After being delayed for several months, the film was finally released in theatres on 13 January 2021, a day before [Pongal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pongal_(festival)). In addition, the film was simultaneously dubbed and released in [Telugu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telugu_language), [Kannada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kannada) and [Hindi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindi) (titled Vijay the Master) languages.[Amazon Prime Video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_Prime_Video) 16 days after its theatrical release on 29 January 2021, which includes a [Malayalam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malayalam)-language version.

## Plot

[Nagercoil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagercoil), a timid 17-year-old Bhavani witnesses his family's murder by his father's rivals. They force him to take the blame, and they incarcerate him in a [juvenile detention facility](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youth_detention_center), where the staff torture him mercilessly. As a consequence, he develops a ruthless and violent persona. Years later, Bhavani is released from the facility and owns the [lorry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truck) and [trade business](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trading_company).

[Chennai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chennai), John Durairaj (J.D) is a [college](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College) [lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lecturer) beloved by his students for his student-friendly approach. He struggles with [alcoholism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alcoholism) and bitter dislike from fellow staff. Charulatha, aka Charu, a newly hired lecturer at his college, is the only one who commiserates with him. J.D. decides to hold an election at the school on a bet with the college principal and if the election fails, they will dismiss him from the college. Despite a successful initial election, displeased goons attacked the school. Following the botched election, the chairman assigns him to a juvenile detention facility for a 3-month assignment he does not recall volunteering for. J.D. reluctantly assumes his new position. The prison authorities warn him of the rampant drug abuse and violence there. However, he pays no heed to their warnings.

[NGO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-governmental_organization) worker, admonishes him for his negligence. While in detention, J.D. discovers the brothers' note detailing the horrific activities in the detention centre. Heartbroken, J.D. clobbers Bhavani's goons sent to kill him. He calls Bhavani and promises to pay for the boys' deaths. After quitting drinking, J.D. gains control over the institution and begins to reform the inmates. However, Das, an older inmate responsible for controlling the younger children, opposes him. With the help of Undiyal, J.D. discovers that Bhavani regularly makes inmates take the blame for his heinous crimes. He records a video of younger inmates, including Undiyal, discussing Das's crimes in the facility. Bhavani's goons target Charu and retrieve the film, sparing her only because of Undiyal's timely intervention. Undiyal reveals that select inmates are tattooed with the Tamil letter 'ப' (Pa), allowing them to access free food and drugs in exchange for surrendering for Bhavani's crimes.

Bhavani retaliates by killing J.D.'s batchmate, Prem and ex-student, Bhargav, in two separate lorry accidents. With J.D. incapacitated by grief, Das abducts most of the inmates, intending to take them out of [Tamil Nadu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamil_Nadu) along with Bhavani's Benami documents. With the help of [archer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archer) Vanathi, J.D. defeats Das's men and rescues the children. He confronts Das, and J.D. reveals he is the link between Bhavani and the crimes in the detention facility. Das reforms and transports J.D. and the children to Bhavani's [meatpacking plant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meat-packing_industry).

[bangle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangle). Bhavani later urges J.D. to join him in politics, but J.D. kills him, avenging Sabari's and Manikandan's deaths. With the children safe, J.D. and Das surrender to the police. The government shuts down the detention facility and sends inmates to other detention facilities and state prisons based on age. The government sends J.D., Das, and the other adults from the facility to another prison, where they intend to reform the inmates.

### Critical response

[review aggregator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Review_aggregator) website [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), 90% of 23 critics' reviews are positive, with an average rating of 7.4/10.

[The Times of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Times_of_India) gave 3.5 out of 5 stars and wrote "The charismatic performances of Vijay and Vijay Sethupathi that keeps us rooting." [LM Kaushik of](#cite_note-TOI_review-19) [Sify](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sify) gave 3.5 out of 5 stars and wrote "Master is a different Vijay film as well as a different Lokesh film. Thalapathy just keeps growing from strength to strength while Lokesh leaves his mark in the ‘mass hero’ genre too. Despite its pace issues and the length, ‘JD’ Vijay and ‘Bhavani’ Vijay make it worthwhile." [S. Srivatsan of](#cite_note-:2-111) [The Hindu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hindu) wrote that although the film is "a bit drag and flab," the film breaks several tropes associated with the "Vijay formula." [Ranjani Krishnakumar of](#cite_note-227) [Firstpost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Firstpost) gave 2.5 out of 5 stars and wrote "Master is unfortunately the kind of film that concerns itself with too many things but can hardly focus on any of it beyond adorning the hero."

[Saibal Chatterjee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saibal_Chatterjee) of [NDTV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NDTV) criticised the film's length, but wrote "Master is overlong all right, but Vijay and Vijay Sethupathi make a winning combination. Buoyed by a fantastic score by Anirudh Ravichander, here is a film that, for all its flaws, is keenly aware of the star power at its disposal and seldom punches below its weight." [Janani. K of](#cite_note-NDTV-18) [India Today](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India_Today) described Lokesh's script as "boring" and criticised the nearly three-hour runtime, but called Vijay's performance as a flawed character an improvement over his usual roles as "a righteous man belting out socially-conscious messages" and also praised the soundtrack. [Manoj Kumar. R of](#cite_note-229) [The Indian Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Indian_Express) gave 2.5 out of 5 stars and wrote "Master is neither completely a Vijay film nor entirely a Lokesh Kanagaraj film. Lokesh's self-imposed limitations and obligation to be in fan-service undermine the film's impact. He has used so many good talents as just fillers and wasted resources on ideas that don't take the story forward. And, those are not the qualities of Lokesh who made [Maanagaram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maanagaram) and [Kaithi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaithi_(2019_film))."

[The Quint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Quint) gave 2 out of 5 stars and opined that "Master wouldn't have become a drastically different movie, if the film lack a few illogical elements. Maybe, if the entire film had been about Bhavani alone, it would have been fun!"[Baradwaj Rangan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baradwaj_Rangan) of [Film Companion South](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_Companion_South) wrote "There's one part of us waiting to see 'A Lokesh Kanagaraj Film'. There's another part of us waiting for 'A Vijay Entertainer'. The film is a not-bad balance." [Anna Smith of](#cite_note-232) [Deadline Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deadline_Hollywood) wrote "Master may deliver its lessons with a heavy hand, but it's got the charisma to see you through."[Ananda Vikatan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ananda_Vikatan) rated the film 43 out of 100.
