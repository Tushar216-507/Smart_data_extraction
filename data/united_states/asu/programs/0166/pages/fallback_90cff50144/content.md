Minimum Rate of Wages – Madhya Pradesh (April, 2026)

April 1, 2026

st Apr 26 12:30 pm

The Labour Commissioner’s Office, Madhya Pradesh (Indore) has notified revised minimum wages effective 1 April 2026 to 30 September 2026 for workers in scheduled employments across the state.

Effective period: 1 April 2026 to 30 September 2026
