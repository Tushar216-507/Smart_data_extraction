Honoring Arizona’s first innovators

ASU recognizes the 22 tribal nations that include the original inhabitants of the land on which ASU is situated, and further acknowledges the innovations in the region’s canal system that Indigenous peoples designed centuries ago, which continue to make the area livable today.
