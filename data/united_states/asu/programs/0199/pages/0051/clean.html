How do I pick a major?

Try one of ASU’s exploratory programs, which enable you to explore different academic areas without losing any time or credit toward your degree. You can also take ASU’s major and career quiz, me3®, which can help you narrow down the right major for you. Additionally, try talking to your academic advisor for guidance. And know that you’re not alone — 80 percent of students switch majors at least once.
