# Community Health, Disease, Environment, Environmental Health Science, Epidemiology, Global health, Health Policy, Health Promotion, International health, Pandemics, Population Health, biostatistics

Are you interested in addressing global challenges and making a meaningful impact in health and health care? Develop the innovative mindset needed to help tackle the complex public health issues that affect local and international communities.

The Bachelor of International Public Health program is designed to build and strengthen the international public health workforce.

Aligned with U.S. accreditation standards in public health, the program teaches new approaches to:

- communicable disease prevention, control and surveillance
- environmental and occupational health
- global health systems and management
- health promotion and education
- public health issues and solutions
- public health policy

You develop a holistic, equitable, ethical and sustainable approach to public health practice, collaborating with diverse stakeholders to create and implement strategies to ensure health for all.

You engage in evidence-based practice and community collaboration, preparing you to work across sectors, cultures and borders to design effective, culturally responsive and sustainable strategies that advance health equity and improve public health on a global scale.

Program learning outcomes identify what a student will learn or be able to do upon completion of their program. This program’s learning outcomes include the following:

- Critically analyze health challenges and develop context-relevant solutions that protect and promote health in diverse communities.
- Incorporate a holistic approach to public health practice, by being able to collaboratively implement strategies that ensures health for all.
- Incorporate principles of public health including equitable, ethical, and sustainable development within their public health practices.
- Locate, assess, analyze, and effectively communicate public health information to diverse audiences.
- Engage professionally by having the foundation for lifelong learning, innovation, and leadership.

Through study abroad, international public health students have the opportunity to gain exposure to culturally specific health approaches and community-based opportunities and to learn about distinct health environments and differences in the human condition worldwide.

With more than 300 options available, whether in a foreign country, in the U.S. or online, [Global Education programs](https://goglobal.asu.edu/) enable students to tailor their experience to their specific interests and skill sets.
