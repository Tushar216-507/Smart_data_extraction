## What is MyPath2ASU®?

MyPath2ASU® is ASU's transfer admission guarantee, designed to simplify your journey to a bachelor's degree.

MyPath2ASU® creates personalized, course-by-course transfer maps for any regionally accredited community college or four-year institution. Simply enter your chosen major and transfer school, and MyPath2ASU® will identify the courses at your current institution that directly apply to your ASU major and satisfy ASU transfer requirements. This personalized map helps you save time and money and minimize credit loss as you prepare to transfer to ASU.
