Military and veteran admissions at Arizona State University

Meet with John McGuire
Coordinator Senior, Military and Veteran Recruitment
Admission Services

John works directly with military-connected students and their families to help them navigate the admissions process at Arizona State University. Whether you're just getting started or preparing to enroll, he can answer your questions and help you understand your next steps.

During a 30-minute appointment, you can discuss:

  • Applying to ASU
  • Military and veteran education benefits
  • Financial aid and scholarships
  • Military and transfer credit
  • Enrollment and next steps
  • Veteran student support services and the Pat Tillman Veterans Center

Pat Tillman Veteran Center staff and locations

The Pat Tillman Veterans Center staff is dedicated to supporting military-connected students and their families throughout their ASU journey. From VA education benefits and enrollment support to academic success, advocacy and community connections, the center provides personalized resources to help veterans, active-duty service members, National Guard and Reserve members, military spouses and dependents thrive at Arizona State University.
