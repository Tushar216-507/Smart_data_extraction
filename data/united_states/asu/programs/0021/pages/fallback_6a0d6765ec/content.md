## Our Verticals

### Mental Health

Under Aditya Birla Education Trust's mental health pillar, Mpower leads the charge in transforming how people understand and access mental wellness. By raising awareness, improving education, and delivering inclusive, world-class care, Mpower works to ensure that support is not just available, but comprehensive and stigma-free.

[Learn More About Mpower](https://www.abet.co.in/our-work/our-initiatives/mpower)

### Menstrual Health

As part of the Trust’s commitment to dignity and basic health equity, Ujaas addresses menstrual health with urgency and empathy. It equips girls and women with sustainable hygiene practices and accurate information. Many still face barriers to safe, affordable menstrual care, whether due to limited access, misinformation, or long-held stigma, and Ujaas works to break these barriers.

[Learn More About Ujaas](https://www.abet.co.in/our-work/our-initiatives/ujaas)

### Education

ABET’s education initiatives, Aditya Birla World Academy, Aditya Birla Education Academy, The Aditya Birla Integrated School, and Nalanda, share a common intent. To expand access to learning and unlock potential. Together, they nurture young talent, champion inclusivity for students with intellectual and learning disabilities, and help educators continually grow in a rapidly changing world.

[Learn More About Our Education Initiatives](https://www.abet.co.in/our-work/our-verticals/education)
