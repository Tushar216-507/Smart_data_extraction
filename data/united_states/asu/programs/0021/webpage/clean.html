Online electrical engineering courses

In this online EE degree, you’ll complete a mix of foundational and advanced courses designed to build expertise in electrical and electronic systems, including circuits, signal processing, embedded systems and energy systems used in power systems, communications and advanced electronic technologies. Over four years, you’ll progress from core engineering principles to specialized topics, culminating in a two-semester, team-based capstone design project. Elective offerings include machine learning applied directly to hardware platforms — reinforcing the AI and embedded systems focus the program emphasizes.
Courses are delivered in a flexible online format, with 7.5-week sessions in your first two years. Upper-division courses follow a 15-week format, giving you more time to dive deeper into complex engineering concepts.

Jobs you could get with an electrical engineering degree

Electrical engineers power nearly every modern industry such as clean energy, aerospace, telecommunications, robotics, automotive, defense and health care technology.

Graduates commonly pursue roles including:

  • Electrical or electronics engineer
  • Power or controls systems engineer
  • Embedded systems engineer
  • Energy engineer

According to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, electrical engineering roles are expected to grow 7% from 2024 to 2034, which is faster than average. Additionally, the median annual salary for electrical engineers is $118,780. These figures reflect strong demand for engineers across energy, robotics and advanced technology sectors.
