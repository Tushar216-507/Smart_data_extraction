## Online business administration courses

You'll build expertise in general business management through courses in leadership, finance, supply chain operations and business law, with a capstone project that asks you to apply that knowledge to a real business problem. The W. P. Carey School of Business curriculum is designed to meet employer expectations, not only course requirements.

W. P. Carey is advancing a school-wide effort to integrate artificial intelligence across the undergraduate business curriculum through a Mindful AI Triad framework: business knowledge, AI fluency, and ethical judgment. Curriculum enhancements currently underway will incorporate AI tools, applied use cases, and responsible use principles. This approach is designed to ensure graduates are prepared to lead thoughtfully and effectively in an AI-driven business environment.

## The jobs this business administration degree prepares you for

This online degree in business administration prepares you for a wide range of roles across industries. Graduates pursue diverse career paths in both the public and private sectors, with the versatility to move between industries as opportunities arise.

According to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, many business-related roles are projected to grow faster than average from 2024 to 2034, including market research analysts (median salary $76,950, 7% projected growth), management analysts (median salary $101,190, 9% projected growth) and logisticians (median salary $80,880, 17% projected growth). This demand reflects employers’ need for professionals who can analyze data, improve operations and guide strategic decision-making.

With growing demand across multiple sectors, business administration graduates find opportunities in industries such as healthcare, finance, manufacturing, retail, education, technology, government and nonprofit organizations.
