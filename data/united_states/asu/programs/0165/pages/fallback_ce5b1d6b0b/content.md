Software (SW) consists of [computer programs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_program) that instruct the [execution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Execution_(computing)) of a [computer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer). [Software also includes design documents and specifications.](#cite_note-pis-p16-1)

The history of software is closely tied to the development of digital computers in the mid-20th century. Early programs were written in the [machine language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machine_language) specific to the hardware. The introduction of [high-level programming languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-level_programming_language) in 1958 allowed for more human-readable instructions, making [software development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_development) easier and more portable across different [computer architectures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_architecture). Software in a programming language is run through a [compiler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compiler) or [interpreter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interpreter_(computing)) to [execute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Execution_(computing)) on the architecture's hardware. Over time, software has become complex, owing to developments in [networking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_network), [operating systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operating_systems), and [databases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Databases).

Software can generally be categorized into two main types:

- [operating systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operating_system), which manage hardware resources and provide services for applications
- [application software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Application_software), which performs specific tasks for users

The rise of [cloud computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cloud_computing) has introduced the new software delivery model [software as a service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_as_a_service) (SaaS). In SaaS, applications are hosted by a [provider](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Application_service_provider) and [accessed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HTTP) over the [Internet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet).

The process of developing software involves several stages. The stages include [software design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_design), [programming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_programming), [testing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_testing), [release](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_release_life_cycle), and [maintenance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_maintenance). [Software quality assurance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_quality_assurance) and [security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_security) are critical aspects of software development, as [bugs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_bug) and [security vulnerabilities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vulnerability_(computing)) can lead to system failures and security breaches. Additionally, legal issues such as software licenses and intellectual property rights play a significant role in the distribution of software products.

## History

The first use of the word software to describe computer programs is credited to mathematician [John Wilder Tukey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Tukey) in 1958. [The first programmable computers, which appeared at the end of the 1940s,](#cite_note-4) [were programmed in](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEGabbrielliMartini2023519-5) [machine language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machine_language). Machine language is difficult to debug and not [portable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portability_(computing)) across different computers. [Initially, hardware resources were more expensive than](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEGabbrielliMartini2023520–521-6) [human resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_resources). [As programs became complex,](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEGabbrielliMartini2023522-7) [programmer productivity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Programmer_productivity) became the bottleneck. The introduction of [high-level programming languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-level_programming_language) in 1958 [hid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abstraction_(computing)) the details of the hardware and expressed the underlying [algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algorithm) into the code. [Early languages include](#cite_note-FOOTNOTETracy20211-9) [Fortran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortran), [Lisp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisp_(programming_language)), and [COBOL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COBOL).

## Types

There are two main types of software:

- [Operating systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operating_system)are "the[layer of software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abstraction_layer)that manages a computer's resources for its users and their[applications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Application_software)".[There are three main purposes that an operating system fulfills:](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEAndersonDahlin20146-10)
  - Allocating resources between different applications, deciding when they will receive [central processing unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_processing_unit)(CPU) time or space in[memory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_memory).
  - Providing an interface that abstracts the details of accessing [hardware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_hardware)details (like physical memory) to make things easier for programmers.
  - Offering common services, such as an interface for accessing network and disk devices. This enables an application to be run on different hardware without needing to be rewritten.
- Allocating resources between different applications, deciding when they will receive
- [Application software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Application_software)runs on top of the operating system and uses the computer's resources to perform a task.[There are many different types of application software because the range of tasks that can be performed with modern computers is so large.](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEAndersonDahlin20146–7-14)[Applications account for most software](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEJones2014121-15)[and require the](#cite_note-FOOTNOTETracy202166-16)[environment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operating_environment)provided by an operating system, and often other applications, in order to function.

Software can also be categorized by how it is [deployed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_deployment). Traditional applications are purchased with a perpetual [license](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_license) for a specific version of the software, downloaded, and run on hardware belonging to the purchaser. [The rise of](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEO'Regan2022386-18) [the Internet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Internet) and [cloud computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cloud_computing) enabled a new model, [software as a service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_as_a_service) (SaaS), [in which the provider hosts the software (usually built on top of rented](#cite_note-FOOTNOTECampbell-KellyGarcia-Swartz2015156–157-19) [infrastructure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infrastructure_as_a_service) or [platforms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platform_as_a_service)) [and provides the use of the software to customers, often in exchange for a](#cite_note-FOOTNOTERosatiLynn202023-20) [subscription fee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subscription_fee). [By 2023, SaaS products—which are usually delivered via a](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEO'Regan2022386-18) [web application](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_application)—had become the primary method that companies deliver applications.

## Development and maintenance

Software companies aim to deliver a high-quality product on time and under budget. A challenge is that [software development effort estimation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_development_effort_estimation) is often inaccurate.[Software development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_development) begins by conceiving the project, evaluating its feasibility, analyzing the business requirements, and making a [software design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_design). [Most software projects speed up their development by](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEDooley20171-24) [reusing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_reuse) or incorporating existing software, either in the form of [commercial off-the-shelf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commercial_off-the-shelf) (COTS) or [open-source software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open-source_software).[Software quality assurance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_quality_assurance) is typically a combination of manual [code review](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Code_review) by other engineers [and automated](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEO'Regan2022117–118-27) [software testing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_testing). Due to time constraints, testing cannot cover all aspects of the software's intended functionality, so developers often focus on the most critical functionality.[Formal methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_method) are used in some safety-critical systems to prove the correctness of code, [while](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEO'Regan2022267-29) [user acceptance testing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/User_acceptance_testing) helps to ensure that the product meets customer expectations. [There are a variety of](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEO'Regan202220-30) [software development methodologies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_development_methodologies), which vary from completing all steps in order to concurrent and iterative models. [Software development is driven by](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEO'Regan20229-31) [requirements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_requirements) taken from prospective users, as opposed to maintenance, which is driven by events such as a change request.

Frequently, software is [released](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_release_life_cycle) in an incomplete state when the development team runs out of time or funding. [Despite](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEReifer201222-33) [testing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_testing) and [quality assurance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_quality_assurance), virtually all software contains [bugs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_bug) where the system does not work as intended. Post-release [software maintenance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_maintenance) is necessary to remediate these bugs when they are found and keep the software working as the environment changes over time. [New features are often added after the release. Over time, the level of maintenance becomes increasingly restricted before being cut off entirely when the product is withdrawn from the market.](#cite_note-FOOTNOTETripathyNaik20144,_27-34) [As software](#cite_note-FOOTNOTETripathyNaik201489-35) [ages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_aging), it becomes known as [legacy software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legacy_software) and can remain in use for decades, even if there is no one left who knows how to fix it. [Over the lifetime of the product, software maintenance is estimated to comprise 75 percent or more of the total development cost.](#cite_note-FOOTNOTETracy20213-36)

Completing a software project involves various forms of expertise, not just in [software programmers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_programmer) but also testing, documentation writing, [project management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_project_management), [graphic design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graphic_design), [user experience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/User_experience), user support, [marketing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marketing), and fundraising.

## Quality and security

[Software quality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_quality) is defined as meeting the stated requirements as well as customer expectations. [Quality is an overarching term that can refer to a code's correct and efficient behavior, its reusability and](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEGalin20183-41) [portability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porting), or the ease of modification. [It is usually more cost-effective to build quality into the product from the beginning rather than try to add it later in the development process.](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEGalin201826-42) [Higher quality code will reduce lifetime cost to both suppliers and customers as it is more reliable and](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEO'Regan202268,_117-43) [easier to maintain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maintainability). [Software failures in](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEVarga201812-45) [safety-critical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safety-critical_system) may result in serious harm, including injury or death. [By some estimates, the cost of poor quality software can be as high as 20 to 40 percent of sales.](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEO'Regan20223,_268-44) [Despite developers' goal of delivering a product that works entirely as intended, virtually all software contains bugs.](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEO'Regan2022119-46)

The rise of the Internet also greatly increased the need for [computer security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_security) as it enabled malicious actors to conduct [cyberattacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyberattack) remotely. [If a bug creates a security risk, it is called a](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEO'Regan2022266-49) [vulnerability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vulnerability_(computing)).[Software patches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_patch) are often released to fix identified vulnerabilities, but those that remain unknown ([zero days](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zero-day_(computing))) as well as those that have not been patched are still liable for exploitation. [Vulnerabilities vary in their ability to be](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEDaswaniElbayadi202126–27-52) [exploited](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exploit_(computer_security)) by malicious actors, [and the actual risk is dependent on the nature of the vulnerability as well as the value of the surrounding system.](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEAblonBogart20172-50) [Although some vulnerabilities can only be used for](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEHaberHibbert20185–6-53) [denial of service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denial_of_service) attacks that compromise a system's availability, others allow the attacker to [inject](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Code_injection) and run their own code (called [malware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malware)), without the user being aware of it. [To thwart cyberattacks, all software in the system must be designed to withstand and recover from external attack.](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEAblonBogart20172-50) [Despite efforts to ensure security, a significant fraction of computers are infected with malware.](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEO'Regan2022266-49)

## Encoding and execution

### Programming languages

Programming languages are the format in which software is written. Since the 1950s, thousands of different programming languages have been invented; some have been in use for decades, while others have fallen into disuse. [Some definitions classify](#cite_note-FOOTNOTETracy2021117-55) [machine code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machine_code)—the exact instructions directly implemented by the hardware—and [assembly language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assembly_language)—a more human-readable alternative to machine code whose statements can be translated one-to-one into machine code—as programming languages. [Programs written in the](#cite_note-FOOTNOTETracy2021118–120-56) [high-level programming languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-level_programming_languages) used to create software share a few main characteristics: knowledge of machine code is not necessary to write them, they can be [ported](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ported) to other computer systems, and they are more concise and human-readable than machine code. [They must be both human-readable and capable of being translated into unambiguous instructions for computer hardware.](#cite_note-FOOTNOTETracy2021118–119-57)

### Compilation, interpretation, and execution

The invention of high-level programming languages was simultaneous with the [compilers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compiler) needed to translate them automatically into machine code. [Most programs do not contain all the resources needed to run them and rely on external](#cite_note-FOOTNOTETracy2021121-59) [libraries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_library). Part of the compiler's function is to link these files in such a way that the program can be executed by the hardware. Once compiled, the program can be saved as an [object file](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Object_file) and the [loader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loader_(computing)) (part of the operating system) can take this saved file and [execute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Execution_(computing)) it as a [process](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Process) on the computer hardware. [Some programming languages use an](#cite_note-FOOTNOTETracy2021122–123-60) [interpreter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interpreter_(computing)) instead of a compiler. An interpreter converts the program into machine code at [run time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Execution_(computing)), which makes them 10 to 100 times slower than compiled programming languages.

## Legal issues

### Liability

Software is often released with the knowledge that it is incomplete or contains bugs.[[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed)] Purchasers knowingly buy it in this state,[[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed)] which has led to a legal regime where [liability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Product_liability) for software products is significantly curtailed compared to other products.

### Licenses

Since the mid-1970s, software and its source code have been protected by [copyright law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copyright_law) that vests the owner with the exclusive right to copy the code. The underlying ideas or algorithms are not protected by copyright law, but are sometimes treated as a [trade secret](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trade_secret) and concealed by such methods as [non-disclosure agreements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-disclosure_agreement). [A](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEO'Regan2022394–396-64) [software copyright](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_copyright) is often owned by the person or company that financed or made the software (depending on their contracts with employees or [contractors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_contracting_in_the_United_States) who helped to write it). [Some software is in the](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEO'Regan2022403-65) [public domain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_domain) and has no restrictions on who can use it, copy or share it, or modify it; a notable example is software written by the [United States Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_government_of_the_United_States).[[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed)] [Free and open-source software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_and_open-source_software) also allow free use, sharing, and modification, perhaps with a few specified conditions. [The use of some software is governed by an agreement (](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEO'Regan2022403-65)[software license](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_license)) written by the copyright holder and imposed on the user. [Proprietary software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proprietary_software) is usually sold under a restrictive license that limits its use and sharing. [Some free software licenses require that modified versions must be released under the same license, which prevents the software from being sold
or distributed under proprietary restrictions.](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEO'Regan2022394,_404-66)

### Patents

[Patents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patent) give an inventor an exclusive, time-limited license for a novel product or process. [Ideas about what software could accomplish are not protected by law and concrete implementations are instead covered by](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEO'Regan2022395-68) [copyright law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copyright_law). In some countries, a requirement for the claimed invention to have an effect on the physical world may also be part of the requirements for a software patent to be held valid.[Software patents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_patent) have been [historically controversial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_patent_debate). Before the 1998 case [State Street Bank & Trust Co. v. Signature Financial Group, Inc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Street_Bank_&_Trust_Co._v._Signature_Financial_Group,_Inc.), software patents were generally not recognized in the United States. In that case, the [Supreme Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_the_United_States) decided that business processes could be patented. [Patent applications are complex and costly, and lawsuits involving patents can drive up the cost of products.](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEJones201419-70) [Unlike copyrights, patents generally only apply in the jurisdiction where they were issued.](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEO'Regan2022398-71)

## Impact

Engineer [Capers Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capers_Jones) writes that "computers and software are making profound changes to every aspect of human life: education, work, warfare, entertainment, medicine, law, and everything else". [It has become ubiquitous in](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEJones201432-74) [everyday life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everyday_life) in [developed countries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Developed_countries). [In many cases, software augments the functionality of existing technologies such as household](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEKitchinDodge2011iv-75) [appliances](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Home_appliance) and [elevators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elevator). [Software also spawned entirely new technologies such as](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEKitchinDodge20115-76) [the Internet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Internet), [video games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_games), [mobile phones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobile_phones), and [GPS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPS). [New methods of communication, including](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEJones2014xxviii-77) [email](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Email), [forums](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_forum), [blogs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blog), [microblogging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microblogging), [wikis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiki), and [social media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_media), were enabled by the Internet. [Massive amounts of knowledge exceeding any paper-based library are now available with a quick](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEManovich2013329-78) [web search](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_search). [Most creative professionals have switched to software-based tools such as](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEJones2014xxviii-77) [computer-aided design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer-aided_design), [3D modeling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3D_modeling), digital [image editing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_editing), and [computer animation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_animation). [Almost every complex device is controlled by software.](#cite_note-FOOTNOTEManovich2013333-73)
