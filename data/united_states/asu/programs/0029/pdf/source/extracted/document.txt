Summary

Lightcast® is a software company that collects and analyzes labor market data in the United States.
These data are aggregated in the Developer™ database, which includes online job postings
collected from over 65,000 sources. This database allows advanced searches of over 70 data
fields that are extracted from each posting, including job title, occupation, employer, industry,
required skills, credentials, and salary. Duplicate job postings are excluded from Developer
search results.

The Developer database was used to assess the national employment demand for behavior
analysts from the past 16 years (2010-2025) and the demand by state from the past 12 months.
Key Findings

· Demand for behavior analysts continues to grow. Annual nationwide demand for individuals
holding BCBA or BCBA-D certification has increased each year since 2010, with a 28% increase
from 2024 to 2025. Demand was highest in five states in 2025 (ordered from most to least):
California, New Jersey, Texas, Massachusetts, and North Carolina. These five states account for
38% of the recent demand for behavior analysts, with California alone accounting for 15% of the
2025 demand.

· The demand for assistant behavior analysts decreased in 2025. After a significant 131%
increase in demand from 2023 to 2024, the demand for BCaBA certification decreased
between 2024 and 2025; however, there is still an overall increasing trend for BCaBA
certification demand since 2010. Demand was highest in five states in 2025 (ordered from
most to least): California, Florida, Michigan, Texas, and Pennsylvania. These five states account
for 43% of the recent demand for assistant behavior analysts, with California alone accounting
for 15% of the 2025 demand.
