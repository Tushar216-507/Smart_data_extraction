Online Master of Arts in Special Education – Applied Behavior Analysis

Prepare to become a Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA) with Arizona State University’s online Master of Arts in special education with a concentration in applied behavior analysis — a pathway to BCBA certification in a high-demand field. In this ABA master’s online program from one of the nation’s top education colleges, you’ll learn how to apply evidence-based principles of ABA across diverse settings, from classrooms and clinics to community health organizations. This practice is widely used in ABA therapy for autism, helping individuals build communication, social and adaptive skills.

The curriculum also builds skills in assessment, intervention and data-driven decision-making through coursework that meets the Behavior Analyst Certification Board requirements for Pathway 2 eligibility, including 2027 standards.

What jobs can I get with an online master’s in applied behavior analysis?

Demand for behavior analysts with BCBA or BCBA-D certification has grown steadily since 2010, with an increase of nearly 30% from 2024 to 2025, according to the Behavior Analyst Certification Board’s analysis of national job posting data. Graduates may pursue roles in education, behavioral consulting, program management, social services and more.

ABA is commonly applied in special education to assist students with learning or behavior challenges, such as autism or ADHD, but it’s also used to improve organizational performance, support community health initiatives and enhance team dynamics in fields like sports.

This degree can support BCBA career pathways as well as roles that apply behavior analysis without certification. This degree does not lead to teacher certification.

How to apply

Applicants to the Master of Arts in special education who want to pursue the applied behavior analysis concentration must fulfill the requirements of both the Graduate College and the Mary Lou Fulton College for Teaching and Learning Innovation.
