Online Master of Legal Studies in Health Care Compliance and Administration

Health care organizations need leaders who can interpret regulations, manage risk and make informed decisions that protect patients. Arizona State University's online Master of Legal Studies in health care compliance and administration develops that legal fluency in health care laws and regulations, and aligns with Compliance Certification Board (CCB) pathways. You'll graduate ready to step into administrative, compliance and health care leadership roles in one of the country's largest, fastest-growing industries.

What is health care compliance?

Health care compliance MLS courses

Your coursework is structured to build foundational legal training and move into program core options that align with your emphasis in compliance and administration. A few electives round out your degree, giving you room to explore adjacent areas such as contract or employment law. The capstone brings it all together, applying your full legal toolkit to complex health care challenges.

Health care compliance careers and salary outlook

Graduates are prepared for compliance, administrative and risk management roles across hospitals, health systems, insurers and other related industries. The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics projects 23% growth for medical and health services manager roles through 2034, with median annual pay of $117,960 as of 2024.
