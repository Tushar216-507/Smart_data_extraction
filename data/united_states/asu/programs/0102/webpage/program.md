# Online Master of Arts in Communication – Health Communication

Turn health knowledge into meaningful action. The online Master of Arts in communication with a concentration in health communication at Arizona State University prepares you to translate complex health information into clear, evidence-based messages that improve outcomes at scale. You’ll build skills in strategy, research and campaign design to support health care organizations, nonprofits and policy institutions.

## Health communication courses

In this online health communication master’s program, you’ll explore topics such as health communication campaigns, patient-provider communication, workplace wellness and occupational safety, health disparities and equity, and digital stress and well-being. You’ll gain hands-on experience with surveys, experiments, focus groups and interviews, and complete a mentored capstone project with a health organization or create a health campaign.
