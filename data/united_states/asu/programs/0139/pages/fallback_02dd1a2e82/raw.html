Global Challenge: Can You Identify the Nation?

Globle will assess your geographic knowledge. The objective of the game is to pinpoint the undisclosed country on the global map. Following each attempt, you'll observe the country you've selected on the map, with the intensity of color indicating proximity to the concealed nation. With an unlimited number of attempts at your disposal, make the most of the color clues and locate the target country swiftly.

How to play Globle?

  1. Enter first guess

    Begin the game by inputting the name of a country. Following this, the globe will highlight the country's territory, providing you with additional clues.

  2. Follow the hints

    Examine hints to expedite your search for the mysterious country. The more intense the color of the country's territory, the nearer you are to uncovering it. Additionally, the displayed distance in kilometers/miles will indicate the country's proximity.

  3. Guess the country

    Strive to identify the country using as few attempts as possible. Engage in the daily game mode with friends, or enhance your memory skills in the practice mode.
