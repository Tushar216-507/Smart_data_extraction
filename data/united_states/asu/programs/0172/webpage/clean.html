Military and veterans studies courses

In this program, you’ll take a variety of skills-building courses in areas such as writing and research and then focus your learning in your portfolio track. Depending on your specialization, your courses will hone in on the history of the military and war, management and team building, counseling, clinical research and more.

The jobs you could get with a military and veterans studies degree

This program prepares you for civilian and military careers in a range of industries from business, education and military community support to public and mental health, public affairs and intelligence. There’s also a demand for skilled employees from federal agencies that support military-connected and veteran individuals, such as the Department of Defense and the Department of Veterans Affairs.
