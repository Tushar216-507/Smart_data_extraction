# Online Bachelor of Arts in Business – Sustainability

As companies explore new ways to support ecological stability, the demand for sustainability-focused business leaders has grown. Arizona State University’s online Bachelor of Arts in business with a concentration in sustainability combines a core business curriculum with rigorous courses in sustainability. In this program, you’ll learn how to make decisions that strengthen businesses and support the environment.

## Business and sustainability courses

The curriculum is designed to build your strengths in traditional business topics and then delve into specialized knowledge in sustainability. Topics you can explore range from the economics of sustainability and sustainable technologies to sustainable development and the processes that gave rise to the world in which we live.

## The jobs in business and sustainability you’ll be prepared for

Environmental concerns exist across all sectors and industries. While you might gravitate toward renewable energy, agribusiness and other green industries, you can also pursue sustainability-focused positions in the wider business community. Program graduates are excellent fits for positions within corporate responsibility, management, marketing, sales, supply chain management and more.
