# Online Master of Education in Elementary Education – Teacher Certification

Ready to begin your career as a well-prepared certified teacher? Arizona State University’s Master of Education in elementary education – teacher certification program online provides those with a bachelor’s degree in any field the chance to earn a graduate degree from a top-ranked college of education and become certified to teach grades K–8. This program provides a solid foundation in teaching and content knowledge, guided by field experts. Upon graduation, you’ll be prepared for success in today’s dynamic and ever-changing educational landscape.

## Elementary education and teacher certification courses

This program’s curriculum integrates core coursework with hands-on experiences in K–8 classroom settings. You’ll build a solid foundation in teaching and deepen your content knowledge. Additionally, you’ll undertake a final applied research project where you’ll identify an educational challenge and design a targeted intervention for your own classroom.
