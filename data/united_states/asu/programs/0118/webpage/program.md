# Online Bachelor of Arts in Business – Law

Arizona State University’s online Bachelor of Arts in business with a concentration in law offers a versatile combination of business and legal expertise. By improving your legal knowledge, you’ll be equipped to offer a unique perspective when legal challenges arise in business settings. Upon graduation, you’ll be equipped to pursue business management, contracts and compliance, human resources, legal assistance, research analysis and more.

## Business and law courses

In this program, you’ll explore how legal principles intersect with business strategy and decision-making. Courses cover topics such as the legal system, regulatory agencies, ethical considerations and intellectual property. You’ll also study argumentation, justice theory and persuasive writing to build the critical thinking and communication skills needed in today’s business and legal environment.
