Online Master of Arts in Museum and Curatorial Practices

Museums and heritage organizations shape how communities understand their past and imagine their future. The Master of Arts in museum and curatorial practices at Arizona State University, offered online, prepares you to engage with art museums, heritage sites and curatorial projects through inclusive, forward-looking practices. You’ll build skills in research, interpretation and exhibition development to support careers in curating, education, research and related museum fields.

How to apply

Applicants to the online Master of Arts in museum and curatorial practices program must fulfill the requirements of both the Graduate College and the Herberger Institute for Design and the Arts. Degrees in the social sciences (history, anthropology, archaeology) and the arts (art history, museum studies) are most helpful to prepare students, but not required.

The creative heart of Arizona State University

ASU’s Herberger Institute for Design and the Arts is the home to top-ranked creative programs from various disciplines, including art, design, dance, fashion, film, games, media arts and sciences, music and theatre.
