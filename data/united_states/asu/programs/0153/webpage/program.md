# Online Master of Science in Management

Gaining graduate-level management expertise early in your career builds a strong foundation for future opportunities and advanced study. Arizona State University’s online Master of Science in Management (MiM) enhances your ability to solve real organizational challenges and make data-informed decisions. Through this accelerated, high-impact program, you’ll enhance your resume and open doors to more career possibilities.

## Business management courses you’ll take

Courses in this program build the fundamentals you need for early-career leadership. You’ll strengthen your business professionalism, learn to collaborate across teams, and develop ethical decision-making and managerial judgment. You’ll also sharpen your analytical reasoning, communication and presentation skills while building strong time management, accountability, adaptability and resilience in dynamic business settings.

## How to apply

Applicants for the online Master of Science in management must fulfill the requirements of both the Graduate College and the W. P. Carey School of Business.
