## What are transfer student tuition costs?

Paying for college pays off down the road. You’re already well on your way toward a bachelor’s degree, and financial aid like transfer student scholarships and ASU’s competitive tuition rate can make it easier to get there.

Please note that everyone’s net tuition cost is specific to their own circumstances and will be different for each person. To get an idea of what you can expect to pay, please see the [ASU tuition calculator](https://tuition.asu.edu/cost/tuition-estimator).
