Prepare for careers in human resources management

HR degree concentration courses

Courses delve into every facet of managing the employment cycle. This includes strategizing an organization's human capital needs; selecting, onboarding and training employees; developing compensation and benefits packages; and overseeing post-employment processes — all while ensuring legal compliance. You’ll also develop global competence and learn about cross-national differences in human resources policies and practices.
