### International Welcome

All students are expected to attend international welcome at their campus.

- Students who register will receive an Uber pass to use for a complimentary ride home from the Phoenix Sky Harbor International Airport.
- You must register early enough to receive the email.

## Pre-arrival webinars

### Attend to meet other students, get reminders on dates, checklists and important items

As you prepare for the fall 2026 semester, please note that all international students are expected to attend classes in person.

If you arrive earlier than your recommended date, you will be responsible for your own housing accommodations (e.g., hotel, staying with family or friends, etc.) until then. [ASU Family](http://eoss.asu.edu/family) has coordinated a number of discounted hotel offerings from partner hotels near all campus locations and you can [view them here](https://eoss.asu.edu/welcome#webspark-anchor-link--13363).

## Resources and events

Outside of the International Arrival and Welcome, here are some videos we suggest you watch, resources you should review and events you should attend to make the most of your ASU experience.

### International Students and Scholars Center programming

We encourage you to attend the events designed for international students by the ISSC. These community events and activities include weekly workshops and free English tutoring and conversation practice. See the full schedule and learn more.

### International Student Engagement

International Student Engagement supports, advocates and designs experiences for international undergraduate and graduate students from admittance through graduation and beyond.

ISE provides students with personal and professional development opportunities outside of the classroom. Students build community, develop their leadership skills and participate in many global celebrations.
