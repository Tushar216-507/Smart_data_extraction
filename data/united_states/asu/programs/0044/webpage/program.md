## Online computer science degree courses

This program features a math-based curriculum, beginning with calculus I. Courses also focus on balancing technical knowledge with understanding the conceptual framework of computer science. Prominent course topics include data structures, principles of programming languages, information assurance, database management and more. Additionally, you’ll have the opportunity to explore cutting-edge technology like artificial intelligence and machine learning through courses like CSE 471: Introduction to Artificial Intelligence and CSE 475: Foundations of Machine Learning. The program then culminates in a capstone project completed over two semesters.
