    Study at Courses

    MBBS

    The duration of the MBBS course is 4 ½ years followed by compulsory rotating internship training of one year.

    Nursing

    Students who pursue nursing courses are trained to take care of patients, assist doctors, and manage general tasks in a hospital.

    DGAFMS/DG-1D

    AFMS plays a pivotal role in the Post Graduate training of doctors within the Armed Forces Medical Services, addressing the specialized requirements essential for comprehensive healthcare.

    Post Graduate

    Post Graduate degree programmes provide them with in-demand knowledge and skills through immersive learning.

    SuperSpeciality

    SuperSpeciality is a medical super specialisation that can be done in branches like Cardiology, Neurology, Nephrology, Gastroenterology and more.

    Para Medical

    Para Medical courses emphasize fundamental skills, such as first aid, nursing, and emergency medical services, essential for supporting healthcare providers.
