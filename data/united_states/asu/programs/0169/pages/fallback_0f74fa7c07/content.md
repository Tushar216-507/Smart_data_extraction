Ques. What is the admission procedure for MBBS Colleges in Pune?

Ans. There are two ways to get admission to the MBBS program in Pune: through NEET UG Counselling and the Management Quota.

- NEET UG: NEET UG is the standard method for admission to the MBBS program in Pune. Admission requires a valid score in the NEET UG Entrance Exam, followed by AIQ counselling (MCC) or Maharashtra NEET Counselling conducted by the State CET Cell. The National Testing Agency (NTA) conducts the test every May. Admission to AFMC Pune is based on the NEET UG score, followed by AFMC's own screening process (ToELR, interview, and medical examination) with a service liability.
- Management Quota: Admission to the MBBS program in private colleges can also be obtained through this route. A valid NEET UG score is still mandatory for Management Quota seats.

SMCW Pune and SIU Pune come under Symbiosis International (Deemed University), BVMC Pune under Bharati Vidyapeeth (Deemed University), and DYPMC Pune under Dr. D.Y. Patil Vidyapeeth (DPU), Pune.

Ques. What is the internship stipend at Medical Colleges in Pune?

Ans. The internship stipend at Medical Colleges in Pune is INR 18,000 per month as per Maharashtra Government norms (applicable to government colleges from February 2024, and directed by the DMER to private colleges as well). AFMC Pune interns receive INR 60,000 – 75,000 per month as per the 7th CPC norms.

Ques. What are the important documents required for MBBS admission?

Ans. Common documents required for MBBS admission:

- NEET UG Scorecard & Admit Card.
- Class 10 (DOB) certificate, Class 12 marksheet & passing certificate (PCB + English).
- Transfer Certificate / Character Certificate (if required).
- Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS) and domicile certificate (for state quota).
- Photo ID (Aadhaar / Passport / Voter ID).
- Passport-size photographs (as per specification).
- Medical fitness certificate, migration certificate (if needed).
- Payment receipts/bank drafts for fees or deposits.

Ques. Is there any college providing direct admission for MBBS in Pune?

Ans. No, direct admission to the MBBS course is not allowed in India. Even for the management quota, students must at least qualify for the NEET exam; only then are candidates eligible to participate in the management quota.
